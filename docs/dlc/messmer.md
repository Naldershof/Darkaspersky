# Messmer the Impaler

> Marika's hidden son, sent to the Land of Shadow to subjugate the Hornsent people before the age of the Erdtree -- a genocide erased from history, and living proof that the Golden Order was built on conquest.

## Overview

Messmer is a demigod who does not appear in the base game, whose name is never spoken in the Lands Between, and whose existence was deliberately scrubbed from the Golden Order's history. He is a son of [Marika](../demigods/marika-radagon.md), tasked with the military subjugation of the [Land of Shadow](land-of-shadow.md) and the extermination of the [Hornsent people](the-hornsent.md) who lived there. He accomplished this mission with brutal efficiency, wielding flame connected to the Erdtree itself, and was then sealed in the shadow realm along with the evidence of what he had done.

Messmer is not a rogue element. He is a weapon his mother pointed at a civilization and then hid in a drawer.

## Lore Details

Messmer's flame is distinct from the fire of the Giants or the Frenzied Flame. It is an Erdtree flame -- golden fire bent toward destruction rather than grace. This is significant: the same power that sustains the [Erdtree's](../lore-concepts/the-erdtree.md) cycle of life was used as a tool of genocide. The fire that blesses and the fire that burns are the same fire.

His title, "the Impaler," is earned through the fields of impaled Hornsent bodies that litter the Land of Shadow. These are not battlefield casualties -- they are displays of terror, the deliberate spectacle of a conqueror establishing dominance. Messmer did not merely defeat the Hornsent. He made an example of them.

Despite this, Messmer is not portrayed as a simple villain. His dialogue suggests devotion to his mother and genuine belief in the necessity of his mission. He was a son who did what his mother asked, and was rewarded by being forgotten. His relationship with Marika mirrors a pattern she repeats: use, discard, conceal.

The serpent imagery associated with Messmer connects him to the base-of-the-Erdtree iconography and possibly to older, pre-Golden Order symbolism. His serpentine second phase in the boss fight may represent something the Erdtree absorbed or suppressed -- another layer of history the Golden Order buried.

## Connections & Analysis

**Messmer and Mohg -- Marika's discarded sons.** The parallel between Messmer and [Mohg](../demigods/mohg.md) is the DLC's most damning commentary on Marika. Both are her sons. Mohg was born an Omen and cast into the Shunning-Grounds beneath [Leyndell](../locations/leyndell.md) -- rejected for what he was. Messmer was sent to the Land of Shadow and erased from history -- rejected for what he did on her behalf. One was thrown away for being inconvenient; the other was thrown away for being too convenient. Both responded by building their own kingdoms in the dark.

**Messmer and Morgott -- the loyal son.** [Morgott](../demigods/morgott.md) is Mohg's twin, also born Omen, also cast into the sewers -- and he still chose to defend the Erdtree. Messmer, too, carried out his mother's will without question. Both loyal sons were rewarded with abandonment. The Golden Order does not reward loyalty; it consumes it.

**The Erdtree as weapon.** Messmer's flame reframes the Erdtree. In the base game, the Erdtree is presented as a source of life and grace, with darker implications underneath. Messmer makes those implications explicit: the Erdtree's power was used to burn civilizations to the ground. The [Golden Order](../lore-concepts/the-golden-order.md) is not merely a flawed religion -- it is a post-conquest regime that sanctified its own violence.

**Marika's pattern.** Messmer is one node in a repeating pattern: Marika empowers agents to do terrible things, then discards them. Godfrey was exiled after serving his purpose. The Tarnished are recalled only when needed. Maliketh guards Destined Death in isolation. Messmer was sealed away with his sins. Marika does not betray her servants out of cruelty -- she does it out of architectural necessity. Each one is a load-bearing wall she removes once the structure is complete.

## Unresolved Mysteries

!!! question "What is Messmer's serpent form?"
    Messmer's second phase involves a dramatic serpentine transformation. Is this connected to [Rykard's](../demigods/rykard.md) God-Devouring Serpent? To pre-Erdtree serpent worship? To something the Erdtree consumed and incorporated? The serpent imagery may point to a suppressed religious tradition that predates the Golden Order.

!!! question "Who is Messmer's other parent?"
    Marika is confirmed as his mother, but his father is never named. If it is Godfrey, Messmer would be a brother to Godwyn, Morgott, and Mohg. If it is Radagon (i.e., Marika herself), Messmer would share the dual-natured parentage of Malenia and [Miquella](../demigods/miquella.md). The identity of his father may explain the nature of his flame and his serpentine transformation.

!!! question "Did Messmer know his history would be erased?"
    His dialogue suggests loyalty to Marika, but also a deep weariness. Did he consent to being sealed in the Land of Shadow, or was he imprisoned there? The answer changes whether he is a willing martyr or another of Marika's victims.
