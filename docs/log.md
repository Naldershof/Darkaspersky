**Key connections surfaced during initial compilation:**

1. Marika and Ranni pursued parallel rebellions against the Golden Order — Marika from within (shattering the Ring), Ranni from without (the Night of the Black Knives)
2. Every Outer God operates through a proxy: Greater Will → Two Fingers → Marika; Rot God → Malenia; Formless Mother → Mohg; Dark Moon → Ranni; Frenzied Flame → the Three Fingers
3. Miquella's DLC arc reveals he was not the innocent victim the base game implied — he deliberately shed his humanity to become a god, manipulating followers through supernatural charm
4. The Crucible represents the Erdtree's primordial form before the Golden Order's dogma sanitized it, connecting to the Omen curse, the Crucible Knights, and the underground civilizations
5. Hewg's chains and Roderika's spirit-tuning both point to the Roundtable Hold being purpose-built as a forge for god-slaying weapons
