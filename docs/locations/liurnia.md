# Liurnia of the Lakes

> A drowned, melancholy region of mist and grey water, where the once-great Carian royal family has disintegrated and the Academy of Raya Lucaria has sealed itself away from a world it no longer wishes to serve.

## Overview

Liurnia of the Lakes occupies the northwest of the Lands Between, a vast shallow lake dotted with submerged ruins, overlooked by the sealed Academy of Raya Lucaria at its center and the decaying Carian Manor at its northern edge. The region is defined by water, moonlight, and loss. Everything here was once magnificent -- the architecture, the magical infrastructure, the royal dynasty -- and everything here has curdled into melancholy.

The region's story is inseparable from the Carian royal family. Queen [Rennala](../demigods/rennala.md) ruled through sorcerous might, her marriage to [Radagon](../demigods/marika-radagon.md) cemented a political alliance between the moon-worshipping Carians and the Golden Order, and their children -- [Ranni](../demigods/ranni.md), [Radahn](../demigods/radahn.md), and [Rykard](../demigods/rykard.md) -- were among the most powerful demigods. When Radagon left Rennala to become Marika's consort, every institution in Liurnia fractured. The Academy rebelled, the manor fell to ruin, the children scattered to war and death and rebellion, and Rennala retreated into grief.

## Lore Details

### The Academy of Raya Lucaria

The Academy sits on a massive island at the center of Liurnia's lake, sealed behind a glintstone barrier passable only with a specific key. The seal is a statement: after the Shattering, the scholars rejected the outside world entirely. Inside, the Academy is a sprawling complex of lecture halls, libraries, and waterwheel-powered mechanisms, its architecture adorned with celestial motifs -- stars, moons, and constellations carved into stone. These decorations reflect the Academy's foundational connection to cosmic understanding that predates the Erdtree.

The scholars have become their own horror. Many have glintstone growths erupting from their bodies, particularly their skulls. Senior sorcerers wear glintstone crowns that appear fused to the bone beneath. This is the endpoint of glintstone study taken to extremes: the sorcerers literally crystallize, their flesh converting to the material they study. The Academy is not merely a school; it is a place of fatal competition and obsessive transformation.

### Rennala and the Debate Parlor

Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon, is found on the floor of the grand library, clutching a glowing amber egg, surrounded by juvenile scholars who crawl and chant around her in a lullaby-like vigil. She is no longer in command. The scholars tend to her but also keep her contained. She has retreated into obsessive attempts at rebirth after Radagon's departure, using the amber egg he left as a parting gift.

The boss encounter tells the entire story of Liurnia in miniature: a frail, broken woman on the floor, and then the spectral projection of who she once was -- a devastating full-moon sorceress, magnificent and terrifying. The distance between the two Rennalas is the distance between what Liurnia was and what it has become. Uniquely among shardbearers, she survives her defeat, remaining to offer the player rebirth. She is not an enemy; she is a grieving mother.

### Carian Manor and the Three Sisters

Carian Manor at Liurnia's northern edge is the seat of the Carian royal family, defended by massive spectral hands that burst from the ground -- a family that expected and feared assault. Inside, the opulence decays. Knights have become hostile spirits. Servants have transformed into finger spiders. Pidia hides in a sealed room, terrified of the puppet soldiers. Royal Knight Loretta guards the innermost courtyard as a lingering spirit; the real Loretta has departed for [the Haligtree](the-haligtree.md) to find a home for the Albinaurics.

Beyond the manor, three towers stand on a windswept plateau: Ranni's Rise, Renna's Rise, and Seluvis's Rise. [Ranni](../demigods/ranni.md) holds court here in doll form, attended by Blaidd, Iji, and Seluvis -- a deposed princess operating an insurgency from exile. Beneath Seluvis's tower, a secret puppet chamber hides his human-turned-puppet victims, demonstrating that even the rebel faction harbors darkness.

### The Albinauric Village

Beneath a cliff in southwestern Liurnia lies the Village of the Albinaurics, half-submerged in swampy shallows, its first-generation inhabitants largely slaughtered. The massacre was carried out by Gideon Ofnir's forces in search of the Haligtree Secret Medallion half carried by Albus. The slaughter of an entire community of beings who cannot receive grace -- artificial creations rejected by the Erdtree -- to obtain a key to [the Haligtree](the-haligtree.md) reflects the ruthless pragmatism of the Roundtable Hold's leadership and the expendability of those outside the Golden Order's blessing.

Latenna, a first-generation Albinauric found nearby with her dead wolf companion, cannot walk. Her quest to bring the Albinaurics' birthright to Miquella's sanctuary is one of the most poignant in the game -- a story of seeking homeland when every door is closed.

### The Four Belfries

Four bell towers on Liurnia's western heights, each containing a portal activated by an Imbued Sword Key. These portals lead to sealed memories or locations of profound significance: the Chapel of Anticipation (where the Tarnished's journey began), a vision of Farum Azula, and the Nokron aqueduct. The inscription at the summit reads: "The Four Belfries look upon the lands below, each a portal to places sealed away." These portals function as windows into sealed time -- moments the Golden Order would prefer remain inaccessible.

### The Divine Tower of Liurnia

Accessible only by inverting the Carian Study Hall using an artifact from Ranni's questline, this tower is unique among Divine Towers. It does not activate a Great Rune. Instead, at its summit lies Ranni's discarded empyrean body, slumped in a chair, bearing the wound of the blade of death. This is physical evidence of the Night of the Black Knives: Ranni used a fragment of the Rune of Death to kill her own body, severing herself from the Two Fingers' control. She placed her mortality at the highest point of Carian power and walked away.

## Connections & Analysis

Liurnia's drowned landscape is the physical expression of the Carian dynasty's collapse. The lake itself -- grey, shallow, choked with ruins -- mirrors Rennala's mental state: once deep and vital, now stagnant. Structures protrude from the water at odd angles, their foundations eroded, suggesting the water level has risen or the land has sunk since the Shattering. The Academy's island position, elevated above the flood, makes it a literal ivory tower looking down on the drowned civilization it was meant to serve.

The moon is the unifying symbol of Liurnia. Rennala is Queen of the Full Moon. Ranni pursues the Dark Moon. The Academy studied celestial bodies. The Carian royal crest features lunar imagery. This lunar identity stands in direct opposition to the Golden Order's solar Erdtree, establishing Liurnia as a region defined by an alternative cosmology. When Radagon left for the Erdtree, he did not merely abandon a wife -- he pulled the political bridge between the moon and the sun out from under an entire civilization.

The spatial relationship between the Academy, Carian Manor, and the Three Sisters traces the disintegration of power. The Academy, which once served the Carians, rebelled and sealed itself. The manor is infested. The Three Sisters towers are isolated outposts. Ranni's questline physically moves the player through these spaces in order: from the Academy (where Rennala sits broken) to the manor (where spectral defenses rage) to the towers (where the insurgency hides). The architecture tells the story of withdrawal -- from institution to manor to tower to doll.

The Albinauric village connects Liurnia to [the Haligtree](the-haligtree.md) and [Mohgwyn Palace](mohgwyn-palace.md) through the Haligtree Medallion and Miquella's promise of sanctuary for the rejected. Loretta's departure from Carian Manor to the Haligtree traces the same hope. Liurnia is where the dispossessed begin their journey to the places that might accept them.

## Unresolved Mysteries

!!! question "Why did the Academy rebel against the Carians after Radagon's departure?"
    **Interpretation A:** Without Radagon's political alliance linking the Carians to the Golden Order, the Academy saw no benefit in continued servitude to a weakened dynasty and seized independence.
    **Interpretation B:** The Academy had always chafed under royal authority, and Radagon's departure merely removed the last political constraint. The crystallization of scholars suggests an inward turn that predates the Shattering.
    **Gap:** No single source explains the Academy's revolt. The sealed barrier and the hostile scholars imply a deliberate, institutional decision, but the specific catalyst is never stated.

!!! question "What caused Liurnia's flooding?"
    **Interpretation A:** The Shattering's physical impact on the landscape caused subsidence or water-table changes across the region.
    **Interpretation B:** The flooding predates the Shattering and may be connected to a longer-term decline of the land, possibly related to the Erdtree's root system or the underground river systems below.
    **Gap:** No item description or dialogue explains the water level. The submerged ruins suggest a gradual process rather than a single catastrophic event.
