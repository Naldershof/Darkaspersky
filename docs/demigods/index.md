# Demigods of the Lands Between

> The demigods are the children and consorts of Queen Marika the Eternal, each bearing a shard of the shattered Elden Ring and warring for supremacy in the wake of the Shattering.

## Overview

The demigods of the Lands Between are the inheritors of divine lineage -- offspring of Queen Marika and her consorts Godfrey and Radagon. When Marika shattered the Elden Ring, its shards became the Great Runes, and each demigod claimed one (or chose to discard theirs). The resulting war reshaped the continent, destroyed entire regions, and left the Golden Order fractured beyond repair.

Not all figures listed here are demigods in the strict sense. Rennala holds a Great Rune but is mortal. Melina's nature is ambiguous. Godfrey is the First Elden Lord but not divine by birth. They are included because their fates are inseparable from the demigod lineage.

## Lineage

The demigod family tree is tangled by the revelation that Marika and Radagon are one being:

- **Marika + Godfrey (Hoarah Loux):** Godwyn the Golden (deceased), Morgott, Mohg
- **Rennala + Radagon:** Ranni, Radahn, Rykard
- **Marika + Radagon (i.e., Marika with herself):** Malenia, Miquella
- **Marika (unknown):** Melina (strongly implied), possibly the Gloam-Eyed Queen

The children of Marika and Radagon -- Malenia and Miquella -- were born Empyreans but also born cursed, as if their dual-natured parentage produced inherent instability. This is one of the deepest unresolved questions in the lore.

## Thematic Threads

Each demigod represents a different response to the collapse of the Golden Order:

- **Loyalty:** Morgott defended the Order. Godfrey obeyed even in exile.
- **Rebellion:** Ranni rejected the Two Fingers. Rykard devoured the gods.
- **Ambition:** Mohg sought to create a new dynasty. Miquella sought to become a god.
- **Endurance:** Malenia and Radahn fought endlessly with no resolution.
- **Desperation:** Godrick grafted limbs. Rennala tried to rebirth what was lost.
- **Sacrifice:** Melina offered herself as kindling.
