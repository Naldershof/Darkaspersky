# Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon

> Not a demigod by blood but bound to their lineage through her marriage to Radagon, Rennala is the master of the Full Moon and Queen of the Carian royal family, broken by Radagon's departure and endlessly attempting to use his parting gift -- the amber egg -- to rebirth what was lost.

## Overview

Rennala is the queen of the Carian royal family and master of the Academy of Raya Lucaria -- the Lands Between's preeminent institution of sorcery. She is not a demigod. She holds no divine blood. But her marriage to Radagon and the three children they produced -- [Ranni](ranni.md), [Radahn](radahn.md), and [Rykard](rykard.md) -- place her at the center of the demigod lineage. And the Great Rune of the Unborn, the amber egg Radagon gifted her upon their parting, gives her a shard of the Elden Ring despite her mortal status.

The Remembrance of the Full Moon Queen captures her tragedy in a single sentence: "After her champion, Radagon, left her to become the consort of Queen Marika, the queen was left bereft and broken, holding fast to the amber egg he gifted her."

When the player encounters Rennala, she is in the Grand Library of the Academy, surrounded by her students (now reduced to crawling, singing attendants), cradling the amber egg and attempting to use its rebirth power endlessly. She is not hostile in the traditional sense; she is a grief-stricken woman performing the same ritual over and over, trying to rebirth something that cannot be recovered. The second phase of her fight is not even Rennala herself but a projection created by [Ranni](ranni.md) to protect her mother.

## Lore Details

### The Carian Royal Family and the Academy

The Carian royal family and the Academy of Raya Lucaria represent a power structure independent of the Golden Order. The Carians drew their power from the moon and the stars -- celestial forces that predate the Erdtree and exist outside the Greater Will's domain. Rennala's mastery of the Full Moon made her one of the most powerful sorcerers in the Lands Between, and under her leadership, the Academy flourished as a center of glintstone sorcery.

The Academy's relationship with the Golden Order was contentious. Radagon led a military campaign against the Carians before being captivated by Rennala and marrying her. This marriage was effectively a peace treaty, uniting the two powers. When Radagon left, the treaty collapsed, and the Academy eventually sealed itself behind a barrier, becoming isolated and hostile.

### The Marriage to Radagon

Radagon came to Rennala as a conqueror and stayed as a husband. The circumstances of their courtship are unusual: he fought against her people, then fell in love with her. They had three children -- Ranni, Radahn, and Rykard -- and their union represented the merger of Golden Order martial power with Carian magical tradition. The children inherited aspects of both: Ranni's sorcery, Radahn's gravity magic (a fusion of star-lore and physical might), and Rykard's ambition.

The marriage's end was sudden and unexplained from Rennala's perspective. Radagon left to become Marika's consort -- a union that, given Radagon's identity as Marika, was essentially a return to his/her other self. He left Rennala the amber egg containing the Great Rune of the Unborn as a parting gift.

### The Amber Egg and the Great Rune of the Unborn

The egg is unique among Great Runes. It does not require activation at a Divine Tower. It does not enhance the player's combat abilities. Instead, it grants rebirth -- the ability to reallocate one's attributes, to be "reborn anew." The Great Rune of the Unborn is described as "an unborn and formless Great Rune," suggesting potential rather than power. It is the possibility of becoming something different.

For Rennala, the egg became an obsession. She uses it to endlessly attempt rebirth -- of her students, of herself, of something. The crawling, singing students in the Grand Library are the products of incomplete rebirth: beings that were unmade but never properly remade. Rennala's grief has transformed the rebirth mechanism into a tool of unmaking.

### Ranni's Protection

In the boss fight's second phase, the player faces not Rennala but a spectral projection created by [Ranni](ranni.md). Ranni uses her power to create an illusion of Rennala in her prime -- a full-moon sorcerer at the height of her abilities. This is Ranni protecting her mother, but it is also Ranni presenting an image of what Rennala once was, contrasted with what she has become.

After the fight, Rennala is unharmed. She remains in the Grand Library, continuing her rebirth rituals. The player can use her services to reallocate stats -- mechanically, the grief-broken queen becomes a tool for the Tarnished's optimization. The game does not comment on this irony, but it is present.

## Connections & Analysis

### The Only Mortal Holding a Great Rune

Rennala's possession of a Great Rune despite being mortal is anomalous. Every other Great Rune holder is a demigod (or, in Rennala's case, connected to one through marriage). The amber egg was Radagon's gift -- a shard of the Elden Ring given not as a weapon or a claim to power but as a consolation. The Elden Ring's power of rebirth, given to a woman who lost her reason to live, is either deeply cruel or deeply generous depending on interpretation.

The fact that the Great Rune is "unborn and formless" may reflect Radagon's own conflicted nature. As [Marika's](marika-radagon.md) other self, Radagon was a being of split identity. The unborn rune -- potential without form -- mirrors his own existence: something that has not fully become what it could be.

### The Academy as Star-Knowledge Center

Rennala's Academy was the primary institution studying the stars in the Lands Between. This connects directly to [Ranni's](ranni.md) later pursuit of the Age of Stars and the Dark Moon. The Carian royal family's moon-worship and the Academy's star-study represent an alternative cosmological framework to the Golden Order's Erdtree-centered theology.

When [Radahn](radahn.md) held back the stars, he was in a sense opposing his mother's entire intellectual tradition. The stars that Rennala's Academy studied were the same stars Radahn conquered. Whether Radahn saw this as honoring or betraying his mother's legacy is unclear, but the structural irony is present: the son of the Moon Queen became the Starscourge.

### Rennala and Marika: Mirrors of Grief

Both Rennala and [Marika](marika-radagon.md) were connected to Radagon, and both were broken by loss. Marika lost Godwyn and shattered the Elden Ring. Rennala lost Radagon and retreated into endless, failed rebirth. Both women's grief produced catastrophic consequences: Marika's produced the Shattering; Rennala's produced the Academy's decline and the corruption of her students.

The parallel extends further: both women were left holding fragments of the Elden Ring (Marika as its shattered vessel, Rennala as holder of the Unborn rune), and both are encountered in states of profound diminishment. Marika hangs broken in the Erdtree; Rennala sits broken in the library. Radagon's departure destroyed them both -- fitting, since Radagon was both of their consort.

### The Moon Lineage

Rennala's connection to the Full Moon, and her daughter Ranni's connection to the Dark Moon, suggests a matrilineal succession of lunar power. The Full Moon (Rennala's patron) and the Dark Moon (Ranni's patron) may be different aspects of the same celestial entity, or rival lunar powers. In either case, the mother-daughter transmission of moon-aligned magic represents a feminine power tradition that operates entirely outside the Golden Order's patriarchal structure.

This connects to the broader lore of the Eternal Cities (Nokron, Nokstella), which worshipped the stars and moon before being cast underground by the Greater Will. The Carian royal family may be inheritors or survivors of the same tradition the Greater Will attempted to suppress. Rennala's Academy, in this light, was not merely a school but a repository of pre-Erdtree cosmological knowledge.

## Unresolved Mysteries

!!! question "Did Radagon leave Rennala willingly?"
    **Interpretation A:** Yes. Radagon returned to Marika (his other self) because the Greater Will demanded it. His marriage to Rennala was genuine but subordinate to his divine obligations.
    **Interpretation B:** No. Radagon was recalled -- pulled back into unity with Marika against his will. The amber egg was a genuine farewell gift from someone who wanted to stay but could not.
    **Interpretation C:** The question is meaningless because Radagon and Marika are one being. Radagon's "departure" was a reintegration, and his time with Rennala was an anomalous period of separation, not a genuine independent life.
    **Gap:** No source describes Radagon's emotional state when he left Rennala. The egg is the only evidence of his feelings.

!!! question "What is Rennala trying to rebirth?"
    **Interpretation A:** She is trying to rebirth Radagon -- to use the amber egg to bring back what she lost. The students she transforms are failed attempts to recreate her beloved.
    **Interpretation B:** She is trying to rebirth herself -- to become the Rennala who existed before Radagon left, the powerful queen who commanded the Academy.
    **Interpretation C:** She is no longer trying to rebirth anything specific. The ritual has become compulsive, an expression of grief without direction.
    **Gap:** Rennala's dialogue is minimal and fragmented, offering no clear statement of intent.

!!! question "Was Rennala aware that Radagon was Marika?"
    **Interpretation A:** No. The secret of Radagon/Marika's dual nature appears to have been hidden. Rennala married Radagon believing him to be an independent being.
    **Interpretation B:** She suspected. The Carian royal family's star-knowledge and the Academy's research capabilities might have uncovered evidence.
    **Interpretation C:** She learned after his departure, and this knowledge contributed to her breakdown.
    **Gap:** No source addresses Rennala's knowledge of the Radagon/Marika identity.
