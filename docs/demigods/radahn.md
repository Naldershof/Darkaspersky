# Starscourge Radahn

> The mightiest of the demigod children, conqueror of the stars, who studied gravity magic under an Alabaster Lord, held back the celestial bodies to halt fate itself, and was reduced to a mindless beast by Malenia's Scarlet Rot.

## Overview

General Radahn is the son of Radagon and [Rennala](rennala.md), making him half-brother to [Malenia](malenia.md) and [Miquella](miquella.md) through Radagon's identity as [Marika](marika-radagon.md). He is universally acknowledged as the strongest of the demigod warriors. The Remembrance of the Starscourge states plainly: "Starscourge Radahn is undoubtedly the mightiest demigod, and the general of the mightiest army."

Radahn combined his inherited power from both the Golden Order (through Radagon) and the Carian magical tradition (through Rennala) into something unique: gravity magic, learned from an Alabaster Lord of the stars. He used this power to conquer the stars themselves -- halting their movement across the sky. This act, the "Starscourge" from which he takes his name, was not merely a display of strength. It had profound cosmic consequences, locking fate in place and preventing the fulfillment of prophecies tied to the stars' alignment.

After the Shattering, Radahn fought [Malenia](malenia.md) in the Battle of Aeonia. The battle ended when the Scarlet Rot bloomed involuntarily from Malenia, devastating [Caelid](../locations/caelid.md) and destroying Radahn's mind. He was reduced to a feral creature, endlessly consuming the corpses of his own soldiers, his once-legendary army reduced to a feast for their mad general. The Radahn Festival was established to gather warriors who could give him an honorable death.

## Lore Details

### Origins and Training

Radahn was born of Radagon's union with [Rennala](rennala.md) at the Academy of Raya Lucaria, alongside his siblings [Ranni](ranni.md) and [Rykard](rykard.md). Despite the Carian royal family's deep connection to sorcery, Radahn inherited his father's affinity for martial combat and the Golden Order's incantations. His red hair comes from Radagon, a fact the game emphasizes through the "Radagon's Red Wolf" and descriptions that link red hair to the Golden Order's lineage.

What distinguishes Radahn is his study of gravity magic under an Alabaster Lord -- one of the alien lords who came from the stars. This is a remarkable detail: a demigod of the Golden Order learned forbidden cosmic magic from an extraplanar entity. Gravity magic is associated with the stars, the Eternal Cities, and powers that predate and rival the Erdtree. That Radahn pursued this knowledge suggests an intellectual curiosity and ambition that transcended factional loyalty.

### Holding Back the Stars

Radahn's signature feat was using his gravity magic to halt the movement of the stars. This act has multiple layers of significance:

1. **Cosmic scale**: The sheer power required to hold celestial bodies in place is staggering, and the game treats it as Radahn's defining achievement.
2. **Fate manipulation**: In Elden Ring's cosmology, the stars govern fate. The Eternal Cities studied astrology, and the movement of stars determined the arrival of certain prophecies. By halting the stars, Radahn froze fate itself.
3. **Ranni's destiny**: [Ranni](ranni.md)'s path -- the Age of Stars -- requires the stars to move. While Radahn held them in place, Ranni's fate could not be fulfilled. Whether Radahn did this deliberately to protect his sister from a terrifying destiny or for his own reasons is debated.

When Radahn is defeated, the stars resume their movement, and a star crashes into the Lands Between, opening the path to Nokron. This directly enables Ranni's questline.

### The Battle of Aeonia

The clash between Radahn and [Malenia](malenia.md) is the defining battle of the Shattering. Two of the mightiest demigods met in Caelid, and their armies collided in what should have been a decisive engagement. Radahn's strength was physical and gravitational; Malenia's was martial and Rot-infused. Neither could achieve a clean victory.

At the climax, the Scarlet Rot bloomed from Malenia -- an involuntary eruption of the Outer God's power. The bloom transformed Caelid into a festering wasteland. Radahn, despite his godlike constitution, could not resist the Rot. It consumed his mind while leaving his body largely intact, creating the grotesque spectacle of the game's most powerful warrior reduced to a crawling, corpse-eating beast.

### The Radahn Festival

The Festival at Redmane Castle was established by Radahn's loyal followers, led by Castellan Jerren, as a merciful solution. They gather warriors from across the Lands Between -- including the player -- to fight Radahn in a grand battle that might give him the honorable warrior's death he deserves. The festival is not a celebration; it is a mercy killing organized by people who loved their general.

Radahn's Great Rune reflects his nature: it "raises maximum HP, FP, and Stamina," embodying the sheer fortitude and ambition of the Starscourge. Even as a shard of the broken Elden Ring, it radiates vitality.

### Leonard the Horse

One of the most humanizing details in Radahn's lore: he learned gravity magic in part so that he could continue riding his beloved horse, Leonard. Radahn's massive body would have crushed any normal mount, so he used gravitational magic to reduce his own weight, allowing Leonard to carry him. In the boss fight, Leonard is a tiny, emaciated horse carrying a giant -- the visual absurdity is intentional and poignant. Even in his madness, Radahn has not harmed Leonard. The horse survives because the last shred of Radahn's consciousness protects it.

## Connections & Analysis

### Radahn and Ranni: The Sibling Question

The most debated connection is between Radahn's starholding and [Ranni's](ranni.md) fate. Ranni's Age of Stars ending requires the stars to move, and Radahn is the one holding them still. Two readings exist:

**Protective brother**: Radahn held the stars to protect Ranni from the destiny the stars portended -- possibly her need to die, or the coming of Astel, or the fulfillment of the Dark Moon's prophecy. This reading casts Radahn as a loving sibling who used his power to shield his sister, even after she had already killed her own body during the Night of the Black Knives.

**Ambitious conqueror**: Radahn held the stars because conquering them was the ultimate expression of his power. The title "Starscourge" is a boast, not a sacrifice. Ranni's fate being frozen was a side effect, not the goal.

The game does not resolve this, but the emotional weight of the narrative favors the protective reading. Radahn studied alien magic, conquered the cosmos, and used his strength to hold fate in place -- and when he fell, the stars moved again, enabling the sister who had already chosen death.

### The Tragedy of the Mightiest

Radahn's arc is a meditation on the futility of strength. He was the strongest demigod. His army was the mightiest. His gravity magic could hold the stars. None of it mattered against the Scarlet Rot. The Outer God of Rot did not care about Radahn's power; it consumed him as indifferently as it consumed the land.

This parallels a broader theme: in Elden Ring, the mightiest figures are not the ones who shape the world. [Ranni](ranni.md), physically the weakest (inhabiting a doll), achieves the most radical change. [Morgott](morgott.md), cursed and scorned, is the last defender of the capital. [Godrick](godrick.md), the weakest demigod, outlasts many of the stronger ones simply by hiding. Strength in this world is not correlated with success or survival.

### Radahn and Malenia: Mutual Destruction

The Battle of Aeonia destroyed both participants. Radahn lost his mind; Malenia lost consciousness and had to be carried home. Neither gained anything. The Shattering produced no winner in this conflict -- only two broken demigods and a ruined land. This makes the Battle of Aeonia the purest expression of the Shattering's futility: the demigods fought for shards of a broken ring, and the fighting only broke more things.

### The Festival as Eulogy

The Radahn Festival is structurally unique in the game. No other demigod receives a communal effort organized around giving them a dignified end. Jerren and the festival participants do not seek to exploit Radahn or claim his rune -- they want to free him from his suffering. This speaks to the immense respect and love Radahn commanded even after his fall. The festival is the Lands Between's equivalent of a military funeral, but one where the honored dead must first be killed.

## Unresolved Mysteries

!!! question "Did Radahn hold the stars to protect Ranni?"
    **Interpretation A:** Yes. Radahn loved his sister and used his power to prevent the stars from fulfilling their prophecy, which would require Ranni's death or transformation.
    **Interpretation B:** No. Radahn conquered the stars for his own glory, and the effect on Ranni's fate was incidental.
    **Interpretation C:** Radahn held the stars before the Night of the Black Knives, meaning his motivation predates Ranni's need for protection. He may have done it for reasons no longer recoverable.
    **Gap:** No in-game source explicitly states Radahn's motivation for holding back the stars.

!!! question "Was Malenia's march on Caelid her own decision?"
    **Interpretation A:** Malenia chose to confront Radahn as part of the Shattering's power struggle, seeking his Great Rune for Miquella's plans.
    **Interpretation B:** The Outer God of Rot drove Malenia toward Radahn, knowing the confrontation would produce a bloom that would spread the Rot across a new region.
    **Gap:** The motivation for Malenia's specific campaign against Radahn is never stated. Why Caelid? Why Radahn?

!!! question "What did Radahn's mind contain before the Rot consumed it?"
    **Interpretation A:** Radahn was a brilliant strategist and scholar of alien magic -- his mind was as formidable as his body. The Rot's destruction of his intellect is the cruelest part of his fate.
    **Interpretation B:** Radahn was primarily a warrior, and his "mind" was less complex than figures like Ranni or Miquella. The Rot reduced him to instinct, but there was less distance to fall.
    **Gap:** Few sources describe Radahn's personality or intellect beyond his martial achievements and his love for Leonard.
