# Godfrey, First Elden Lord / Hoarah Loux

> The mightiest warrior of his age, exiled when his wars were won — the first Tarnished, and possibly the instrument of Marika's final gambit.

## Overview

Godfrey was once Hoarah Loux, a fearsome warrior chieftain from beyond the Lands Between. Marika chose him as her consort and first Elden Lord, and together they waged war to establish the Golden Order's dominion. He led the campaign against the giants, the dragons, and the storm lord — every enemy the Erdtree needed destroyed.

When there were no more wars to fight, Marika stripped Godfrey and his warriors of their grace, exiling them from the Lands Between. They became the first Tarnished — graceless wanderers sent to fight and die in foreign lands. This exile was not a punishment but a calculated move: Marika needed warriors who would grow strong through struggle, then return when she needed them.

Godfrey suppressed his berserker nature by taking Serosh, the Lord of Beasts, as a regent grafted to his back. Serosh tempered his bloodlust, allowing the warrior to rule as a lord. When the Tarnished finally return and Godfrey reaches the Erdtree, he tears Serosh from his back and reverts to Hoarah Loux — the warrior he always was — for one final battle.

## Lore Details

Godfrey's campaign established the Golden Order through conquest. He defeated the Storm Lord (possibly connected to Stormveil), subjugated the giants of the Mountaintops, and warred with the ancient dragons. His lion imagery pervades the capital — Radahn admired him and adopted similar iconography.

The exile of the Tarnished is among Marika's most calculated acts. Her words at the Erdtree suggest foreknowledge: she told the Tarnished to struggle, grow strong, and return. This implies the entire Tarnished exile was a long-term plan — possibly to create warriors capable of challenging the gods themselves.

Godfrey's return at the foot of the Erdtree makes him the penultimate boss. He fights first as the composed Elden Lord, then tears away Serosh and becomes Hoarah Loux — a pure grappler fighting with raw physical might. This transformation is the shedding of everything the Golden Order imposed on him.

## Connections & Analysis

**The First and Last Tarnished:** Godfrey's story bookends the Tarnished's entire arc. He was the first to receive grace and the first to lose it. The player is among the last to return. That Godfrey stands as the final obstacle before the Elden Throne suggests Marika's plan always culminated in Tarnished fighting Tarnished — only the strongest would reach the Ring.

**Marika's Instrumentalism:** Godfrey was Marika's weapon. She used him to win her wars, discarded him when he was no longer useful, then engineered his return as another piece on her board. This pattern repeats with every character Marika touches — [Radagon](marika-radagon.md), [Maliketh](../lore-concepts/destined-death.md), even [Melina](melina.md).

**Nepheli's Inheritance:** [Nepheli Loux](../npcs/nepheli-loux.md) carries Godfrey's bloodline. Her questline ends with her becoming ruler of Limgrave — a warrior who learns to lead without domination. She inherits Godfrey's strength but not his role as a weapon of the Golden Order.

**Hoarah Loux and the Crucible:** Godfrey's original warrior culture predates the Golden Order. His fighting style as Hoarah Loux is purely physical — no incantations, no golden power. This connects him to the pre-Erdtree age, the [Crucible](../lore-concepts/the-erdtree.md), and the raw physicality the Golden Order tried to civilize away.

**Parallels with the DLC:** The DLC reveals Marika sent [Messmer](../dlc/messmer.md) to conquer the Land of Shadow before sending Godfrey to conquer the Lands Between. Her pattern is consistent: use warriors to establish dominion, then discard or hide them.

## Unresolved Mysteries

!!! question "Did Godfrey know about Marika's plan?"
    **Interpretation A:** Godfrey was a willing participant who understood the exile was temporary — Marika's words at his departure suggest a pact between them.
    **Interpretation B:** He was used and discarded without understanding why — his return is driven by grace's rekindling, not his own will.
    **Gap:** His dialogue at the Erdtree suggests genuine grief for Marika, which could support either reading.

!!! question "Who was Hoarah Loux before Marika?"
    His origins are deliberately vague. He came from "beyond the Lands Between" with his warriors. The chieftain's culture, beliefs, and connection to the beasts (Serosh) all predate the Golden Order, but almost nothing is known about his homeland.
