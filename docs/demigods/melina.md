# Melina

> The kindling maiden who guides the Tarnished — Marika's most mysterious legacy, possibly her final weapon against the order she built.

## Overview

Melina appears at the beginning of the Tarnished's journey, offering to serve as their maiden in exchange for being taken to the foot of the Erdtree. She is bodiless — she manifests at Sites of Grace but exists in a state between life and death. She carries knowledge of Marika's words spoken at the foot of the Erdtree, fragments she shares throughout the journey.

At the Forge of the Giants, Melina offers to sacrifice herself to kindle the flame that burns the Erdtree, fulfilling what she says is her purpose — "to serve as kindling to burn the Erdtree." She refers to this as her destined duty given to her "at the foot of the Erdtree."

Her left eye is permanently closed — or burned shut. In the Frenzied Flame ending, where the player takes the Frenzied Flame instead of letting Melina burn the Erdtree, she survives. In the final shot, her left eye opens, revealing a dark, possibly Destined Death-marked eye. She speaks: "Lord of Frenzied Flame... I will seek you, as far as you may travel... to deliver you what is owed."

## Lore Details

Melina shares fragments of Marika's words at Sites of Grace — words spoken "at the foot of the Erdtree." These include Marika's commands to the Tarnished, her thoughts on Radagon, and her declarations about the Golden Order. Melina presents herself as a messenger, but her intimate knowledge of Marika's private words raises questions about her true identity.

She refers to a "mother" inside the Erdtree, strongly implying she is Marika's daughter. Her torched eye and connection to Destined Death link her to the Gloam-Eyed Queen — the leader of the Godskin Apostles who once challenged the Greater Will with the power of Destined Death.

At the Church of Pilgrimage, Melina says: "I was born at the foot of the Erdtree. My purpose was given to me by my mother." This, combined with her knowledge of Destined Death and her sacrificial role, paints her as Marika's contingency plan — a living weapon designed to burn the Erdtree when the time came.

## Connections & Analysis

**Marika's Final Weapon:** If Melina is Marika's daughter, she may be the culmination of Marika's rebellion. Marika couldn't destroy the Erdtree herself — she's imprisoned within it. So she created (or repurposed) Melina as a walking spark, someone who could kindle the flame when a Tarnished reached the Forge. This makes the entire journey a Rube Goldberg machine built by [Marika](marika-radagon.md): exile the Tarnished → call them back → guide them with Melina → burn the Erdtree → shatter the existing order.

**The Gloam-Eyed Queen Connection:** The [Godslayer's Greatsword](../lore-concepts/destined-death.md) and Godskin lore describe the Gloam-Eyed Queen as someone who wielded Destined Death against the Greater Will. Melina's burned/sealed eye, her knowledge of Destined Death, and her ability to kindle a flame that destroys the Erdtree all connect her to this figure. If Melina IS the Gloam-Eyed Queen (or her successor), Marika may have created her specifically to wield the one power that could destroy the Golden Order's foundation.

**The Frenzied Flame Reversal:** Melina's reaction to the [Frenzied Flame](../lore-concepts/the-frenzied-flame.md) ending is the most revealing moment in the game. She survives, opens her sealed eye, and becomes an avenger. This suggests her eye contains power she normally keeps sealed — possibly the same Destined Death power the Gloam-Eyed Queen wielded. Her vengeance implies she isn't just a passive tool; she has her own will, and that will is opposed to total annihilation.

**Parallel with Ranni:** Both [Ranni](ranni.md) and Melina are daughters of Marika who reject the Golden Order's framework. Ranni kills her own body; Melina sacrifices herself to burn the Erdtree. Both use Destined Death as their instrument. But while Ranni pursues her own vision (the Age of Stars), Melina's purpose seems entirely defined by Marika's plan.

**The DLC Connection:** [Miquella](miquella.md) shed parts of himself to become a god. Melina may have undergone a similar process — she is bodiless, exists between worlds, and has had her purpose "given" to her. Was she stripped of something to become the perfect kindling?

## Unresolved Mysteries

!!! question "Is Melina the Gloam-Eyed Queen?"
    **Evidence for:** Her sealed eye, Destined Death knowledge, ability to kindle the Erdtree's destruction, the Godskin lore about a "gloam-eyed" leader who wielded death.
    **Evidence against:** The Gloam-Eyed Queen was defeated by Maliketh, who took Destined Death from her. If she's the same person, how did she survive?
    **Gap:** No direct statement confirms or denies the connection.

!!! question "What is Melina's true form?"
    She is bodiless and manifests only at Sites of Grace or in specific locations. Is she dead? A spirit? A fragment of Marika? The butterflies that appear around her are associated with death in the game's visual language.

!!! question "What does her open eye contain?"
    In the Frenzied Flame ending, her left eye opens with a dark, possibly black/purple iris. Is this the mark of Destined Death? The Gloam-Eyed Queen's power? Something else entirely?
