# Age of Fracture

> The default ending. The Tarnished mends the Elden Ring without modification, becomes Elden Lord, and restores the Golden Order exactly as it was before the Shattering. Nothing changes. The system that produced the catastrophe is reinstated by the person who fought through its wreckage.

## Overview

The Age of Fracture requires no Mending Rune and no special questline. The player defeats the Elden Beast, approaches the shattered Elden Ring, and mends it. The Tarnished sits upon the throne as Elden Lord. The Erdtree stands. The Golden Order resumes.

This is the ending the game gives you if you do nothing special -- if you simply fight your way to the end and claim the prize. Its simplicity is its statement: the path of least resistance is the path of least change.

## Lore Details

**The Elden Ring is mended as-is.** No Mending Rune means no philosophical modification. Destined Death remains sealed. The Omen remain persecuted. The Greater Will's influence continues through the Two Fingers. The Erdtree resumes its consumption of the dead. Every systemic flaw that caused the Shattering is preserved.

**The Tarnished becomes Elden Lord.** This is the role [Godfrey](../demigods/godfrey.md) once held -- consort to the vessel of the Elden Ring. But Marika is shattered. The Tarnished takes the throne beside a broken god, inheriting an authority whose source is in ruins.

**The fracture remains.** The name itself -- "Age of Fracture" -- suggests that the mending is incomplete. The Ring is repaired, but the cracks remain visible. The world will function, but it will not be whole. This is restoration, not renewal.

## Connections & Analysis

**Arguably the worst ending.** This is a strong claim, but the game supports it. Every other ending at least attempts to address the Golden Order's fundamental problems. [Fia](../npcs/fia.md) restores death. [Goldmask](../npcs/goldmask.md) removes divine caprice. [Ranni](../npcs/ranni-questline.md) replaces the system entirely. The Age of Fracture does none of these things. It reinstates the exact conditions that led to the Night of the Black Knives, the Shattering, and the wars that devastated the continent. It is an ending that learns nothing.

**The irony of the Tarnished's journey.** The player fights across the entire Lands Between, defeats every demigod, burns the Erdtree, and slays the Elden Beast -- only to put everything back the way it was. The journey's cost is enormous; its result is zero change. This irony may be deliberate: FromSoftware's commentary on the player who follows the main path without questioning its destination.

**Godfrey's shadow.** The Tarnished sits where Godfrey once sat. Godfrey was exiled when his wars were won because a warrior without war has no purpose. If the new Elden Lord is also a warrior defined by combat, the same cycle will repeat. Marika engineered the Tarnished's return; she may engineer their exile too, if history rhymes.

**The Golden Order's victims persist.** Those Who Live in Death remain abominations. The Omens remain imprisoned beneath Leyndell. The Nox remain underground. The merchants' genocide remains unavenged. The Age of Fracture is an ending that works for those who benefited from the Golden Order and changes nothing for those it crushed.

**A stable system is not a just system.** The Age of Fracture's greatest appeal is stability -- the wars end, the Ring is mended, order returns. But stability was the Golden Order's promise before the Shattering, and that promise masked systemic cruelty. Restoring stability without addressing the cruelty is a choice to prioritize peace over justice.

## Unresolved Mysteries

!!! question "Is the Tarnished a puppet?"
    Marika orchestrated the Tarnished's exile and return. Grace guided them to the Erdtree. The Two Fingers directed their path. If the Tarnished simply mends the Ring as expected, have they exercised free will, or fulfilled a script?

!!! question "Will this Elden Lord last?"
    Godfrey was exiled when he was no longer useful. If the new Elden Lord's purpose was to mend the Ring, what happens after? Does Marika -- even shattered -- retain the power to discard them?

!!! question "Can the Ring stay mended?"
    The Shattering was caused by Marika's internal contradictions. If those contradictions remain (Radagon and Marika still sharing one body), what prevents another Shattering?
