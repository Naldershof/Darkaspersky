# Lore Concepts

> The foundational ideas, metaphysical systems, and cosmic forces that shape the Lands Between.

## Overview

The world of Elden Ring is governed by overlapping systems of power, faith, and cosmic influence. At the center sits the [Elden Ring](the-elden-ring.md) itself -- not a physical object but the governing law of reality, housed within the [Erdtree](the-erdtree.md) and maintained by the [Golden Order](the-golden-order.md). When Queen Marika shattered the Ring, the resulting war -- [the Shattering](the-shattering.md) -- fractured every institution, faith, and alliance in the Lands Between.

Behind the mortal conflicts, a constellation of Outer Gods vie for influence: the [Greater Will](the-greater-will.md) through its [Two Fingers](the-fingers.md), the [Frenzied Flame](the-frenzied-flame.md) through the Three Fingers, the [Outer God of Rot](outer-god-of-rot.md) through Malenia, the [Formless Mother](the-formless-mother.md) through Mohg, and the enigmatic [Dark Moon](the-dark-moon.md) through Ranni. Each offers a different answer to the question of what order should govern existence.
