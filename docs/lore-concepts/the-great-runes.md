# The Great Runes

> Shards of the shattered Elden Ring, claimed by the demigod shardbearers. Each Great Rune's power reflects the psychology, values, and obsessions of the demigod who holds it -- they are mirrors of their owners' souls.

## Overview

When Queen [Marika](../demigods/marika-radagon.md) shattered the [Elden Ring](the-elden-ring.md), its fragments became the Great Runes -- structural shards of reality's governing law, each retaining a portion of the Ring's power. The demigod children of Marika claimed these shards, and the war over them became [the Shattering](the-shattering.md).

The Great Runes are not inert objects. They respond to and are shaped by their holders. Each rune's power reflects what the demigod who claimed it valued most, revealing their character in mechanical terms. To activate a Great Rune, the Tarnished must visit a Divine Tower; to use its power, they must consume a Rune Arc. This ritual framework -- recovery, consecration, activation -- reinforces the Great Runes as sacred objects requiring effort and devotion to wield.

## Lore Details

### Godrick's Great Rune: The Weakness That Covets Everything

*"A shard of the Elden Ring, this Great Rune is the weakest among them, and yet even so, its power is a boon from the Erdtree."*[^1]

Godrick's Great Rune raises all attributes. It is explicitly the weakest Great Rune, and yet Godrick "exerted his will upon it, bending its strength to bolster all attributes."[^1] This is a perfect expression of the grafting mentality: Godrick, the least among the demigods, wanted everything and settled for nothing. Rather than mastering one domain, he sought to cover every weakness simultaneously. His rune is the only one that boosts all stats, mirroring how he grafted all manner of limbs to his body -- quantity over quality, breadth over depth.

The rune is activated at the Divine Tower of Limgrave, accessible from Stormveil Castle. That even the weakest Great Rune is described as "a boon from the Erdtree" reminds us that these are fragments of the fundamental law of reality, not mere power-ups.

### Great Rune of the Unborn: Rennala's Gift of Rebirth

*"This Great Rune is the amber egg that was given to Rennala by her beloved Radagon, upon their union. Contained within the egg is the Great Rune of rebirth, an unborn and formless Great Rune."*[^2]

[Rennala's](../demigods/rennala.md) Great Rune is unique. She is not a blood relative of Marika, and her rune was not claimed through battle but given to her by [Radagon](../demigods/marika-radagon.md) as a wedding gift. The rune is "unborn and formless" -- it holds the power of rebirth (respec in gameplay terms) rather than a fixed enhancement. It does not need activation at a Divine Tower.

The amber egg that contains it symbolizes potential rather than achievement. Rennala, broken by Radagon's departure, clutches the egg obsessively, using its rebirth power on her students in a cycle of madness -- birthing and rebirthing them endlessly. The rune of infinite possibility has become a prison of endless repetition.

### Radahn's Great Rune: The Mightiest's Fortitude

*"Radahn the Starscourge imbued it with the force of the stars. Raises maximum HP, FP, and Stamina."*[^3]

[Radahn's](../demigods/radahn.md) Great Rune raises maximum HP, FP, and Stamina -- the three fundamental resource pools. This reflects his identity as the mightiest demigod: not specialized but supremely capable in all dimensions of combat. He is the conqueror of the stars, the general of the largest army, and the warrior who held the heavens in place through sheer gravitational will. His rune grants the endurance to stand against anything.

### Morgott's Great Rune: Devotion Beyond Reason

*"Though Morgott was an Omen, and therefore unblessed by the Erdtree, his devotion to the Erdtree was unwavering. This Great Rune greatly raises maximum HP."*[^4]

[Morgott's](../demigods/morgott.md) Great Rune greatly raises maximum HP -- a simple, defensive enhancement. Morgott was not a conqueror or an innovator. He was a guardian. His rune reflects pure endurance: the ability to take punishment and keep standing. For an Omen, shunned by the very Order he defended, his rune is heartbreaking in its simplicity. He asked for nothing but the strength to protect what he loved, even though what he loved rejected him.

### Rykard's Great Rune: The Devourer's Appetite

*"Rykard the Blasphemous fed it to the serpent, granting the power to restore HP upon defeating enemies."*[^5]

[Rykard's](../demigods/rykard.md) Great Rune restores HP upon killing enemies. He fed his rune to the God-Devouring Serpent, and it now reflects the serpent's insatiable hunger -- every death feeds the wielder. This is the Great Rune as predator: sustaining itself through violence, growing stronger with each kill. Rykard's philosophy of devouring the gods is encoded directly into his shard of reality.

### Mohg's Great Rune: Blood Dynasty

*"Lord Mohg imbued it with his own Accursed Blood, granting the ability to bestow a blessing of blood upon summoned spirits."*[^6]

[Mohg's](../demigods/mohg.md) Great Rune is communal -- it blesses summoned spirits with blood, and when those spirits inflict Hemorrhage, the summoner is healed. This reflects Mohg's vision of dynasty: a lord who empowers his subjects and draws strength from their success. His "Mohgwyn Dynasty" is encoded in the rune -- a system of mutual benefit through bloodshed. That the power comes from "Accursed Blood" and the [Formless Mother](the-formless-mother.md) reminds us that this dynasty is built on heresy and suffering.

### Malenia's Great Rune: Tenacity Through Pain

*"This Great Rune reduces the healing power of the Flask of Crimson Tears. And yet, attacks made immediately after taking damage will partially recover HP."*[^7]

[Malenia's](../demigods/malenia.md) Great Rune is the most demanding. It reduces flask healing but grants HP recovery when attacking immediately after being hit. This is Malenia distilled into a mechanic: she endured the [Scarlet Rot](outer-god-of-rot.md) eating her from within, *"refusing all aid, relying only on her own strength"*[^7]. Her rune punishes passivity and rewards aggression through suffering. It is the rune of someone who refused to die and refused to stop fighting, no matter the cost.

### Ranni's Discarded Great Rune

[Ranni](../demigods/ranni.md) explicitly states that she *"cast aside my Two Fingers, and my Great Rune, both"*[^8]. Her Great Rune is never encountered in the game. She voluntarily abandoned her shard of the Elden Ring, rejecting any claim to the existing order. This absence is itself significant: Ranni does not want to reconfigure the current Ring. She wants to replace it entirely.

## Connections & Analysis

### The Runes as Psychological Portraits

The Great Runes reveal what each demigod values most:

| Demigod | Rune Effect | What It Reveals |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| Godrick | All attributes +5 | Covets everything; master of nothing |
| Rennala | Rebirth (respec) | Infinite potential trapped in repetition |
| Radahn | Max HP/FP/Stamina up | Supreme fortitude; the total warrior |
| Morgott | Max HP greatly up | Pure defensive devotion |
| Rykard | HP on kill | Predatory hunger; strength through consumption |
| Mohg | Blood blessing to spirits | Dynasty through shared power and blood |
| Malenia | HP on counter-attack, reduced flasks | Tenacity through suffering |
| Ranni | (Discarded) | Rejection of the existing order |

This psychological mirroring transforms the Great Runes from power-ups into character studies. The Elden Ring, when complete, presumably balanced all these principles. Shattered, each principle runs unchecked, producing the distortions we see in each demigod's domain.

### The Ring's Fragmentation and the Demigods' Stagnation

With only a fragment of the Ring, each demigod embodies only one aspect of reality's law. This fragmentation explains their inability to progress or change: Godrick can only graft, Radahn can only fight, Morgott can only defend, Malenia can only endure. They are stuck in loops defined by their rune's nature. Only by gathering all the runes can someone transcend these limitations and choose a new direction -- which is precisely what the Tarnished does.

### The Divine Towers as Activation Sites

Each Great Rune must be activated at a Divine Tower, atop which sits a set of [Two Fingers](the-fingers.md). This activation ritual binds the rune to the Greater Will's approval system, suggesting that even shattered, the Ring's components remain under some cosmic oversight. The [Two Fingers](the-fingers.md) at each tower may be monitoring which runes are gathered and by whom.

## Unresolved Mysteries

!!! question "What happened to Ranni's Great Rune?"
    **Interpretation A:** Ranni destroyed it, removing a piece of the Elden Ring from existence permanently.
    **Interpretation B:** She discarded it but it persists somewhere, unclaimed. The Miniature Ranni or the Moonlight Altar may hold it.
    **Interpretation C:** When Ranni killed her body, the Great Rune was absorbed into the Cursemark of Death found on her corpse.
    **Gap:** No source describes where the rune went after Ranni cast it aside.

!!! question "Do the Great Runes change their holders, or do the holders change the runes?"
    **Interpretation A:** The runes are neutral fragments that are shaped by whoever claims them. Godrick made his rune boost all stats; it did not originally do that.
    **Interpretation B:** Each rune has inherent properties, and the demigods were drawn to the rune that matched their nature.
    **Gap:** The rune descriptions say the demigods "exerted their will" or "imbued" the runes, suggesting active modification by the holder. But the match between holder and rune is suspiciously perfect.
