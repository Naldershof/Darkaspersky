# The Two Fingers & Three Fingers

> The cosmic intermediaries through which the Outer Gods act upon the Lands Between -- the Two Fingers serving the Greater Will, the Three Fingers channeling the Frenzied Flame. Together they may once have been a single hand.

## Overview

The Fingers are the primary mechanism by which cosmic powers interact with the mortal world. The [Two Fingers](the-greater-will.md) are agents of the Greater Will, seated at the Roundtable Hold and in Divine Towers throughout the Lands Between. They communicate (slowly, cryptically) through the Finger Readers -- crone-like women who interpret their twitching. The [Three Fingers](the-frenzied-flame.md) lurk beneath Leyndell in the Subterranean Shunning-Grounds, sealed behind a door that can only be opened by the unclothed and willing. They offer the Frenzied Flame to any Tarnished who embraces them.

The DLC introduces a third entity: **Metyr, Mother of Fingers**, a vast cosmic organism that suggests the Fingers are not mere appendages of their respective Outer Gods but living beings with their own origins -- possibly fragments of a single, ancient creature.

## Lore Details

### The Two Fingers: Agents of the Greater Will

The Two Fingers appear at the Roundtable Hold as a pair of enormous, gnarled, twitching digits. They do not speak directly; their movements are interpreted by the Finger Reader Enia. When the player first arrives, Enia explains that the Two Fingers have been guiding the Tarnished back to the Lands Between, directing them toward the Erdtree and the restoration of the [Elden Ring](the-elden-ring.md).

The Two Fingers' guidance is the institutional face of the [Golden Order](the-golden-order.md). They selected the Empyreans -- [Malenia](../demigods/malenia.md), [Miquella](../demigods/miquella.md), and [Ranni](../demigods/ranni.md) -- as candidates for the next vessel of the Elden Ring. Each Empyrean was assigned a shadow (Blaidd for Ranni, Maliketh for Marika) to serve as both protector and enforcer. If an Empyrean strays from the Two Fingers' plan, the shadow is meant to destroy them.

Additional Two Fingers exist at the tops of Divine Towers, where the Great Runes are activated. These appear to be separate entities (or extensions) of the same will, each watching over a region. Their universal presence suggests the Greater Will's surveillance network spans the entire Lands Between.

After the Erdtree seals itself with thorns, the Two Fingers at the Roundtable Hold enter a state of prolonged deliberation. They twtch slower and slower as they attempt to communicate with the Greater Will. Enia reveals that the communication may take "thousands, if not tens of thousands of moons." The Greater Will, if it is listening at all, is not in a hurry.

### The Three Fingers: Agents of the Frenzied Flame

The Three Fingers are found in the deepest reaches beneath Leyndell, past the Subterranean Shunning-Grounds and the Cathedral of the Forsaken. The path to them passes through a descent that strips the player of everything: enemies, gravity, and finally armor (the door only opens for one who is completely unclothed). The Three Fingers embrace the Tarnished, burning the Frenzied Flame into their body and irrevocably marking them as the Lord of Frenzied Flame[^1].

The Three Fingers represent the philosophical opposite of the Two Fingers. Where the Two Fingers enforce order, differentiation, and hierarchy, the Three Fingers seek the dissolution of all distinctions -- a return to the One Great, the primordial unity from which all existence was (in this theology) mistakenly separated. Hyetta's words frame this clearly: *"All that there is, came from the One Great. And to the One Great all shall return. The Greater Will made a mistake."*[^2]

The location of the Three Fingers is significant: they are buried beneath the capital of the Golden Order, in the same shunning-grounds where the Omen are imprisoned. The Order literally sits atop the force that would destroy it.

### The Finger Readers

Finger Readers are elderly women found at Sites of Grace and key locations throughout the Lands Between. They can read the player's Great Runes and offer cryptic guidance. Their title indicates they can interpret the Fingers' will, making them the equivalent of priestesses in the Golden Order's hierarchy.

The Finger Readers' function connects to the Great Runes mechanically: the player must bring Remembrances to Finger Reader Enia to exchange them for weapons or incantations. The Finger Readers thus serve as intermediaries not just of divine will but of practical power -- translating the cosmic into the usable.

### Metyr, Mother of Fingers (DLC)

The Shadow of the Erdtree DLC introduces Metyr, Mother of Fingers -- a massive cosmic entity encountered in the [Land of Shadow](../dlc/land-of-shadow.md). Metyr's form is bizarre and unsettling: a vast organism with finger-like appendages, suggesting that all the Fingers in the Lands Between may be offspring or fragments of a single, ancient being.

Metyr's existence reframes the Two Fingers and Three Fingers in a radical way. Rather than being abstract agents of Outer Gods, they may be living creatures -- children of Metyr -- that have been co-opted or commandeered by different cosmic powers. The Greater Will directs the Two Fingers; the [Frenzied Flame](the-frenzied-flame.md) drives the Three Fingers. But their mother exists independently, in the hidden Land of Shadow, separated from both.

This also raises the question of whether the Two Fingers and Three Fingers were once a single Five Fingers -- a single hand, split apart by competing cosmic agendas. The mathematics is suggestive: 2 + 3 = 5, a hand's worth of digits.

## Connections & Analysis

### Ranni's Rejection: Slaying the Fingers

[Ranni's](../demigods/ranni.md) entire questline is built around rejecting the Two Fingers. She declares: *"I have cast aside my Two Fingers, and my Great Rune, both. I would have thee enter my service."*[^3] Her quest for the Fingerslayer Blade -- a weapon forged by the Nox specifically to kill servants of the Fingers -- culminates in her severing the Greater Will's hold on the world.

At the Cathedral of Manus Celes, where Ranni's questline ends, the player finds massive, dead Two Fingers beside Ranni's original body. She killed them. The Fingerslayer Blade was not metaphorical. Ranni physically murdered the cosmic intermediaries assigned to her.

This act has profound implications. If the Fingers can be killed, the Greater Will's reach is not absolute. Its surveillance network has vulnerabilities. And if Ranni can slay her Fingers and survive, other Empyreans could theoretically do the same.

### The Fingers as Communication Bottleneck

A recurring theme is the Fingers' inadequacy as intermediaries. The Two Fingers twitch slowly, are interpreted by elderly women, and enter millennia-long deliberations when faced with unexpected events. They are, fundamentally, terrible at their job. The Greater Will's will is filtered through organisms that may not fully understand it, interpreted by humans who may project their own biases, and acted upon with delays measured in geological time.

This communication failure explains why the Golden Order went wrong. Marika's rebellious actions may have gone unchallenged for so long precisely because the Fingers could not respond quickly enough. The Greater Will may have had opinions about the removal of Destined Death, the Night of the Black Knives, or the Shattering -- but by the time the Fingers processed and communicated those opinions, the damage was done.

### The Frenzied Flame's Origin and the Three Fingers

The [Frenzied Flame](the-frenzied-flame.md) was born from the agony of the merchants buried alive beneath Leyndell. The Three Fingers are its agents, found in the same underground network. This geographic connection suggests that the Three Fingers may have been corrupted by the merchants' suffering -- they were perhaps once neutral (or even aligned with the Greater Will) and were transformed by exposure to the concentrated pain of genocide. Alternatively, the Three Fingers may have already existed as agents of chaos, and the merchants' agony simply gave them power.

### Two Fingers, Three Fingers, Five Fingers

If Metyr is the Mother of Fingers, and the Two Fingers and Three Fingers are her offspring, then the split between them may represent a cosmic schism. Five fingers make a hand; a hand can grasp, create, or destroy. The Greater Will took two fingers for order; the Frenzied Flame took three for dissolution. The hand was broken, and with it, the possibility of unified cosmic governance.

## Unresolved Mysteries

!!! question "Were the Two Fingers and Three Fingers once a single entity?"
    **Interpretation A:** Yes -- they were a single hand of five fingers, split by the conflict between the Greater Will and the Frenzied Flame. The 2+3 split is intentional.
    **Interpretation B:** No -- they were always separate agents of separate powers. The numerical coincidence is just that.
    **Interpretation C:** Metyr's existence suggests they are siblings, not halves. She may have spawned many Fingers, and various Outer Gods claimed different ones.
    **Gap:** No source explicitly states a connection between the Two and Three Fingers beyond their shared morphology.

!!! question "What is Metyr's relationship to the Greater Will?"
    **Interpretation A:** Metyr predates the Greater Will's influence. The Fingers existed before any Outer God claimed them.
    **Interpretation B:** Metyr was sent by the Greater Will alongside the Elden Beast, and the Fingers are purpose-built agents.
    **Interpretation C:** Metyr is from yet another cosmic faction, separate from both the Greater Will and the Frenzied Flame.
    **Gap:** The DLC provides Metyr's existence but almost no context for her cosmic allegiances.

!!! question "Can the Two Fingers actually communicate with the Greater Will, or are they cut off?"
    **Interpretation A:** Communication is real but extremely slow. The Greater Will exists on a cosmic timescale and does not share human urgency.
    **Interpretation B:** The Two Fingers lost contact with the Greater Will long ago and have been operating on autopilot, interpreting a signal that no longer transmits.
    **Gap:** The Fingers' extended deliberation after the thorns appear could indicate either slow communication or no communication at all.
