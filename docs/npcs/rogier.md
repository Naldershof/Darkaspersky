# Rogier

> A spellblade who investigates the Night of the Black Knives and discovers Godwyn's corpse festering beneath Stormveil Castle. His research costs him his life -- death-blight claims him slowly -- but his findings become the player's entry point into the deepest conspiracy in the Lands Between.

## Overview

Rogier is first found in Stormveil Castle, standing on a balcony overlooking the chapel. He is a capable warrior-sorcerer who uses the Rogier's Rapier and glintblade sorcery. After the player defeats Godrick, Rogier relocates to the Roundtable Hold, where his condition visibly deteriorates.

Beneath Stormveil, in a hidden crypt accessible through a jumping puzzle, lies a massive face -- part of Godwyn the Golden's corrupted corpse, growing through the castle's foundations. Rogier discovered this face and touched it. The contact infected him with death-blight, the same corruption that spreads from Godwyn's half-dead body. Over the course of his questline, Rogier weakens, becomes bedridden, and eventually dies, leaving behind his research and his rapier.

## Lore Details

**Godwyn's corpse beneath Stormveil.** The face in the crypt is a manifestation of Godwyn's body, which grows through the Erdtree's roots after his soul-death on the Night of the Black Knives. The same body appears in much larger form in the Deeproot Depths, where [Fia](fia.md) finds it. Stormveil's manifestation is a tendril of the same corruption -- proof that Godwyn's influence reaches far from the Depths.

**Death-blight.** The corruption that kills Rogier. Death-blight is the physical manifestation of incomplete death -- the same force that creates Those Who Live in Death. It spreads from Godwyn's body through the Deathroot, infecting anyone who comes too close. Rogier's gradual decline mirrors the Lands Between's own slow corruption.

**The Black Knife conspiracy.** Rogier pieces together the connection between the Night of the Black Knives, the Rune of Death, and Godwyn's half-death. He shares his findings with the player and directs them toward [Ranni](ranni-questline.md), whom he identifies as a key figure in the conspiracy. His research is incomplete -- he dies before understanding the full picture -- but it is sufficient to set the player on the trail.

**The Black Knifeprint.** Rogier discovers a knifeprint -- an imprint of the Black Knife used to kill Godwyn -- in Stormveil. This physical evidence is what connects the Night of the Black Knives to Destined Death and ultimately to Ranni's theft of the Rune of Death from Maliketh.

## Connections & Analysis

**Rogier is the player's investigative proxy.** In a game that buries its narrative beneath item descriptions and environmental details, Rogier is rare: an NPC who explicitly investigates the plot and shares his findings with the player. He functions as a bridge between the player's ignorance and the game's deepest lore threads. Without Rogier, the connection between Stormveil's crypt, the Night of the Black Knives, and Ranni's questline would be far more obscure.

**His death is the cost of knowledge.** Rogier touches Godwyn's face and contracts death-blight. [Sellen](sellen.md) studies the primeval current and becomes a Graven Mass. [Goldmask](goldmask.md) contemplates the Golden Order and dies in his chair. Knowledge in Elden Ring is not free -- it exacts a physical toll. Rogier's death is the gentlest of these, a slow fading rather than a dramatic transformation, but the pattern is the same: understanding kills.

**The Stormveil face connects the surface to the depths.** Godwyn's body in the Deeproot Depths is the game's most significant underground discovery. But Rogier finds evidence of it on the surface, in a castle the player visits early in the game. This early encounter with the face -- before the player understands what it means -- is a masterful narrative plant. The truth is visible from the beginning; only context reveals it.

**Rogier's research feeds into Fia's questline.** Rogier investigates Godwyn. Fia seeks to complete Godwyn's death. The two questlines are sequential: Rogier's findings explain what happened; Fia's actions attempt to fix it. The player carries knowledge from one NPC to the next, becoming the connective tissue between investigations that neither NPC could complete alone.

**The intersection with Ranni.** Rogier identifies Ranni as central to the Black Knife conspiracy and directs the player toward her. This makes Rogier the narrative hinge between the game's investigative thread (what happened on the Night of the Black Knives?) and its longest questline (Ranni's path to the Age of Stars). He does not live to see where his research leads.

## Unresolved Mysteries

!!! question "Did Rogier know what touching the face would do?"
    His curiosity drove him to investigate Godwyn's corpse, but did he understand the risk of death-blight? His willingness to continue researching after infection suggests he valued the truth more than his life.

!!! question "How did Godwyn's face reach Stormveil?"
    The main body is in the Deeproot Depths, far underground. The face beneath Stormveil suggests Godwyn's corrupted body is growing through the entire root system of the Erdtree, surfacing at multiple points.

!!! question "What was Rogier before the investigation?"
    He is a skilled spellblade with knowledge of glintblade sorcery, suggesting Academy training. His background before arriving at Stormveil is never explained.
