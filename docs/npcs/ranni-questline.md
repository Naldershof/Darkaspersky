# Ranni the Witch -- Questline

> Ranni engineered the single most consequential act of rebellion in the history of the Lands Between: the Night of the Black Knives. Her questline traces the aftermath of that conspiracy and the player's role in completing what she started.

## Overview

Ranni's questline is the longest and most structurally complex in Elden Ring. It spans Liurnia, Nokron, Ainsel River, the Lake of Rot, and the Moonlight Altar. Along the way, the player interacts with her three servants -- [Blaidd](blaidd.md), Iji, and [Seluvis](seluvis.md) -- each of whom meets a tragic end. The quest culminates in the player placing the Dark Moon Ring on Ranni's empyrean body, pledging themselves as her consort and unlocking the [Age of Stars](../endings/age-of-stars.md) ending.

The questline's structure mirrors its theme: layer after layer of descent -- underground cities, subterranean rivers, lakes of poison -- until the player reaches the deepest truth about what Ranni did and why.

## Lore Details

**The Night of the Black Knives.** Ranni stole a fragment of the Rune of Death from [Maliketh](../demigods/godfrey.md), Marika's shadow, and provided it to the Black Knife Assassins -- Numen women with possible ties to Marika's homeland. On that night, the assassins killed Godwyn the Golden's soul while Ranni simultaneously killed her own empyrean body. This "half-death" split the Rune of Death's power: Godwyn's body persisted without a soul, and Ranni's soul persisted without a body.

**The Fingerslayer Blade.** Forged by the Nox civilization, this weapon was designed to sever the Two Fingers' hold on the world. It rests in Nokron, reachable only after Radahn's defeat shatters his gravitational seal on the stars. Ranni needs the blade to complete her severance from the Greater Will.

**The Miniature Ranni.** Found in Ainsel River, this small doll contains a fragment of Ranni's consciousness. She only speaks after the player addresses her three times -- a detail that conveys both her stubborn pride and the vulnerability of her diminished state.

**The Dark Moon Ring.** An engagement ring retrieved from Raya Lucaria's Grand Library. Placing it on Ranni's true body at the Cathedral of Manus Celes completes the pact. The dead Two Fingers beside her corpse confirm that she succeeded in severing their hold.

## Connections & Analysis

**Ranni and Marika are co-conspirators.** This is the questline's deepest implication. The Black Knife Assassins are Numen -- the same race as Marika. Marika later shattered the Elden Ring, an act that only makes sense if the Night of the Black Knives was a coordinated first strike. Ranni stole Death; Marika shattered the Ring. Together, they dismantled the Greater Will's control from two directions. Whether they planned this together or merely exploited each other's rebellions is one of the game's central ambiguities.

**Every servant dies.** [Blaidd](blaidd.md) goes mad because his programming as Ranni's shadow conflicts with her defiance of the Fingers. Iji is killed by Black Knife Assassins -- the same faction Ranni once employed, now turned against her allies. [Seluvis](seluvis.md) is found dead as a puppet, his true nature revealed. Ranni's rebellion has a body count, and her servants pay it. She achieves her freedom, but not without cost.

**The descent structure.** The questline literally takes the player deeper and deeper underground: from the surface towers to Nokron, to Ainsel River, to the Lake of Rot, to the cathedral beneath the moonlight. This mirrors the lore -- the Nox were banished underground for attempting to create a Lord outside the Golden Order's sanction. Ranni follows their path to finish their work.

**Radahn's fall is a prerequisite.** The stars Radahn held back are connected to Ranni's destiny. His gravitational magic was a cage for fate itself. When he falls, a star strikes Limgrave and opens Nokron. Ranni's freedom is built on her brother's destruction -- a cost she accepted in advance.

**The Dark Moon vs. the Greater Will.** Ranni's order replaces the Erdtree with the moon -- but the [Dark Moon](../lore-concepts/the-dark-moon.md) may itself be an Outer God. Her ending frees the Lands Between from the Greater Will only to place them under another cosmic patron. Whether this is liberation or merely a change of management is left unresolved.

## Unresolved Mysteries

!!! question "Did Marika and Ranni coordinate the Night of the Black Knives?"
    The Black Knife Assassins are Numen, like Marika. The timing -- Godwyn's death preceded the Shattering -- suggests a plan. But no dialogue confirms direct communication between them.

!!! question "Is the Dark Moon an Outer God?"
    Ranni claims her order will keep the Outer Gods far from the Lands Between. But her power derives from the Dark Moon. If the Moon is itself an Outer God, her promise is contradictory.

!!! question "Why did Ranni choose to kill her body rather than her soul?"
    The half-death split was deliberate. By destroying her body, she severed the Two Fingers' physical hold on her. But this left Godwyn's body to corrupt the Erdtree from within -- a consequence she may or may not have foreseen.
