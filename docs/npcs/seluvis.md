# Preceptor Seluvis

> A puppet master who turns living people into mindless dolls, schemes to control Ranni herself, and may already be dead when the player meets him -- his "living" form potentially just another puppet operated by the true power behind the curtain.

## Overview

Seluvis is one of [Ranni's](ranni-questline.md) three servants, introduced at Ranni's Rise alongside [Blaidd](blaidd.md) and Iji. He is a sorcerer and "preceptor" -- a title suggesting academic authority -- who sells spirit ashes and sorceries to the player. Beneath this veneer, he operates a hidden puppet gallery where he keeps the bodies of people he has transformed into mindless dolls using a special potion.

His deepest scheme is to administer this potion to Ranni herself, making her his puppet. This plan fails regardless of the player's choices -- if the player brings the potion to Ranni, she sees through it; if the player gives it to the Dung Eater or Nepheli instead, Seluvis gains a puppet but never reaches Ranni. He is eventually found dead, slumped in his chair, his body revealed as a puppet itself.

## Lore Details

**The puppet potion.** Seluvis asks the player to administer a potion to Nepheli Loux. If the player complies, Nepheli becomes a mindless puppet in Seluvis's gallery. Alternatively, the player can give the potion to the [Dung Eater](dungeater.md) in his cell, or bring it to Ranni to expose the plot. The potion strips the victim of will and identity, leaving only an obedient shell.

**The puppet gallery.** Hidden beneath Seluvis's tower, a secret basement contains rows of puppet bodies -- people Seluvis has captured and transformed. Some are recognizable NPCs; others are anonymous victims. The gallery reveals the scale of his operation: this is not a recent scheme but a long-standing practice.

**The Pidia connection.** Pidia, Carian Servant, is found in the Three Sisters area near Ranni's towers. When Seluvis dies, Pidia is attacked and killed by his own puppets. This has led to the theory that Pidia is the true puppeteer -- that Seluvis's body was itself a puppet operated by Pidia, and when the control link broke, Pidia's other puppets turned on him. If true, the "Seluvis" the player interacts with was never a real person.

**Ranni's distrust.** Ranni warns the player about Seluvis: "He is useful, though I trust him not." She tolerates his presence because she needs his sorcery, but she is aware of his nature. Her willingness to keep a known predator in her service speaks to the pragmatism -- or ruthlessness -- underlying her rebellion.

## Connections & Analysis

**Seluvis as a dark mirror of the Two Fingers.** The Two Fingers control through shadows -- embedding obedient agents near the Empyreans. Seluvis controls through puppets -- converting free beings into obedient shells. Both strip agency from their targets. Seluvis's ambition to puppet Ranni is structurally identical to the Fingers' shadow system: place a controller inside the Empyrean's inner circle. The difference is that the Fingers use a living being (Blaidd) who can suffer, while Seluvis removes even the capacity for suffering.

**The question of who is real.** If Seluvis is himself a puppet operated by Pidia, then the player never interacts with a real person during his questline. The arrogance, the scheming, the potion plot -- all performed by a puppet of a puppet master. This creates a recursive horror: the controller is also controlled, and the chain of agency collapses into an infinite regress.

**Agency and consent.** Seluvis's puppets are the game's most explicit depiction of stolen agency. The Golden Order strips choice through doctrine. The Fingers strip it through shadows. Seluvis strips it through sorcery. His method is the most honest -- there is no pretense of divine will or cosmic necessity. He simply takes people and empties them.

**Ranni's tolerance.** Ranni keeps Seluvis in her service despite knowing his nature. This complicates her position as a liberator. She seeks to free the Lands Between from the Greater Will's control, but she is willing to employ a man who enslaves individuals. Her rebellion is against cosmic oppression, not against oppression as a principle. This is a meaningful distinction.

**Connection to the Academy.** Seluvis is a "Preceptor," a rank within the Academy of Raya Lucaria. The Academy is already associated with suppression of knowledge (banning primeval sorcery, sealing itself off). Seluvis represents a different kind of academic corruption -- knowledge used for domination rather than understanding.

## Unresolved Mysteries

!!! question "Is Seluvis a puppet?"
    The Pidia connection strongly implies it. But if so, who created the potion? Who devised the plot against Ranni? Pidia appears to be a minor servant, not a master sorcerer. The power dynamics do not add up.

!!! question "How does the puppet potion work?"
    The potion converts living beings into obedient shells. Is this sorcery, alchemy, or something connected to the primal current? [Sellen's](sellen.md) research into primeval sorcery may be tangentially related.

!!! question "Did Ranni plan Seluvis's death?"
    Seluvis dies during or after Ranni's questline, and his death seems timed to his obsolescence. Did Ranni eliminate him once she no longer needed his services, or did his death follow naturally from the collapse of Pidia's control?
