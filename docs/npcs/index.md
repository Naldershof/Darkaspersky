# NPCs of the Lands Between

> The non-player characters of Elden Ring are not quest-givers in the traditional sense -- they are independent agents pursuing their own philosophies, and the Tarnished is drawn into their orbits rather than the reverse.

## Overview

Every major NPC in Elden Ring embodies a distinct critique of the Golden Order or a response to its collapse. Their questlines do not simply reward the player with items -- they articulate competing visions of what the Lands Between should become. Several NPCs produce Mending Runes that directly determine the game's ending, making their philosophical positions load-bearing elements of the narrative.

The NPCs listed here are grouped by the scope of their ambitions: those who seek to reshape the world, those entangled in the conspiracies that broke it, and those whose personal journeys illuminate the world's deeper themes.

## Thematic Threads

The NPCs collectively explore several questions the game leaves deliberately open:

- **Can the Golden Order be reformed, or must it be replaced?** Goldmask says reform. Ranni says replace. The Dung Eater says defile.
- **What is the proper role of death?** Fia says death belongs in the world. The Golden Order says it does not.
- **Who deserves agency?** Blaidd is denied it. Seluvis denies it to others. Nepheli earns it.
- **What is the cost of knowledge?** Rogier dies for it. Sellen is consumed by it. Goldmask transcends through it.
