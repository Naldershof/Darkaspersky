# Map Fragment Descriptions and Painting Quest Texts

## Map Fragments

### Limgrave, West
> "A map depicting western Limgrave and its surroundings. Marks the location of the Church of Elleh, Gatefront Ruins, and the road leading to Stormveil Castle."

[Context: Found at the Gatefront Ruins, on a stone pillar near the road. The first map fragment most players acquire.]

### Limgrave, East
> "A map depicting eastern Limgrave. Marks Fort Haight, the Mistwood, and the Siofra River Well."

[Context: Found near the road east of Agheel Lake. Reveals the Mistwood and eastern points of interest.]

### Weeping Peninsula
> "A map depicting the Weeping Peninsula, the southern landmass connected to Limgrave. Marks Castle Morne and the Church of Pilgrimage."

[Context: Found along the road south of the Bridge of Sacrifice.]

### Liurnia, East
> "A map depicting eastern Liurnia of the Lakes. Marks the Academy Gate Town, the entrance to the Academy of Raya Lucaria, and the Carian Study Hall."

[Context: Found near the road heading north through Liurnia's lake region.]

### Liurnia, North
> "A map depicting northern Liurnia. Marks Caria Manor, the approach to the Three Sisters, and the road to the Altus Plateau."

[Context: Found along the northern lakeshore path.]

### Liurnia, West (Lakeside)
> "A map depicting western Liurnia and the shores of the great lake. Marks the Village of the Albinaurics and the approach to the Moonlight Altar."

[Context: Found near the western shore, reveals the hidden Albinauric village area.]

### Altus Plateau
> "A map depicting the Altus Plateau, the elevated heartland of the Lands Between. Marks the approach to Leyndell, the Windmill Village, and the roads connecting major sites."

[Context: Found near the Grand Lift of Dectus exit point.]

### Leyndell, Royal Capital
> "A map depicting the Royal Capital and its surrounding walls. Marks the major gates, the avenue leading to the Erdtree, and the capital's tiered districts."

[Context: Found within the capital's outer walls. The capital's complexity necessitates its own fragment.]

### Mt. Gelmir
> "A map depicting Mt. Gelmir and the volcanic region surrounding Volcano Manor. Marks the Ninth Mt. Gelmir Campsite, Fort Laiedd, and the path to the manor."

[Context: Found along the perilous path up Mt. Gelmir's slopes.]

### Caelid
> "A map depicting Caelid, the land blighted by the Scarlet Rot. Marks Redmane Castle, the Swamp of Aeonia, and Sellia, Town of Sorcery."

[Context: Found near the border of Caelid, marking the rot-devastated landscape.]

### Dragonbarrow
> "A map depicting the Dragonbarrow, the elevated region of northeastern Caelid. Marks Lenne's Rise, the Bestial Sanctum, and the domain of ancient dragons."

[Context: Found along the road in Dragonbarrow. This area is significantly higher level than southern Caelid.]

### Mountaintops of the Giants, West
> "A map depicting the western Mountaintops of the Giants. Marks the Zamor Ruins, the ancient battlefield, and the frozen paths toward the Fire Giant's domain."

[Context: Found near the Grand Lift of Rold exit, marking the beginning of the frozen region.]

### Mountaintops of the Giants, East
> "A map depicting the eastern Mountaintops and the approach to Castle Sol. Marks the Stargazer Ruins and the frozen lake leading to the castle."

[Context: Found in the eastern reaches of the Mountaintops.]

### Consecrated Snowfield
> "A map depicting the Consecrated Snowfield, a hidden region of perpetual blizzard. Marks the approach to the Haligtree and Ordina, Liturgical Town."

[Context: Found within the Snowfield. This area is accessed via the secret Haligtree medallion at the Grand Lift of Rold.]

### Siofra River (Underground)
> "A map depicting the Siofra River underground region. Marks the Hallowhorn Grounds, the Ancestral Woods, and the Worshipper's Woods."

[Context: Found in the Siofra River underground area, near the initial elevator landing.]

### Ainsel River (Underground)
> "A map depicting the Ainsel River underground. Marks the Uhl Palace Ruins, the Nokstella waterway approach, and the route to the Lake of Rot."

[Context: Found in the Ainsel River underground region.]

### Lake of Rot (Underground)
> "A map depicting the Lake of Rot, a vast underground expanse of Scarlet Rot. Marks the Grand Cloister and the shores of the toxic lake."

[Context: Found at the edge of the Lake of Rot.]

### Deeproot Depths (Underground)
> "A map depicting Deeproot Depths, where the Erdtree's roots reach their nadir. Marks the Nameless Eternal City ruins and the great waterfall."

[Context: Found in the Deeproot Depths area, marking the region where the Erdtree's roots and Godwyn's corruption converge.]

### Mohgwyn Palace (Underground)
> "A map depicting Mohgwyn Palace, the blood-drenched underground domain of Mohg, Lord of Blood. Marks the blood lake approach and the Dynasty Mausoleum."

[Context: Found in the Mohgwyn Palace region.]

## Painting Quests

Paintings are found at various locations and depict landscapes from specific vantage points. Finding the real-world location depicted in the painting rewards the player with items left behind by the artist's phantom.

### "Homing Instinct"
[Location Found: Stormveil Castle, in a side room near the Liftside Chamber]

Depicts a view of the Stranded Graveyard area from a cliff, with the sea visible in the background. The artist returned to the beginning — to the place where the Tarnished first arrived. The painting's title suggests a longing for origin, a desire to return to the moment before the journey began.

[Painting Location: Near the Stranded Graveyard, overlooking the sea. Reward: Incantation Scarab helm.]

### "Resurrection"
[Location Found: Artist's Shack in Liurnia, near the eastern road]

Depicts the view from a cliff in Limgrave, looking toward the Church of Pilgrimage on the Weeping Peninsula. The title "Resurrection" connects to the church's purpose — a pilgrimage site for those seeking renewal through faith. The artist painted a place of spiritual rebirth.

[Painting Location: Near the Church of Pilgrimage, Weeping Peninsula. Reward: Larval Tear.]

### "Redmane"
[Location Found: Artist's Shack near Redmane Castle, Caelid]

Depicts a view of Redmane Castle from a specific promontory, with the scarlet sky of Caelid above. The artist chose to paint a fortress — a place of martial strength amid a blighted landscape. The painting preserves what Redmane looked like before the rot may eventually consume it.

[Painting Location: A cliff overlooking Redmane Castle from the south. Reward: Rain of Arrows ash of war.]

### "Champion's Song"
[Location Found: A shack near Erdtree-Gazing Hill, Altus Plateau]

Depicts the view from the ramparts of Castle Sol, looking out over the frozen Mountaintops. The title "Champion's Song" evokes glory and lament — the champion who fought for a cause that may be lost. The view from Castle Sol is one of isolation and beauty.

[Painting Location: Atop the ramparts of Castle Sol. Reward: Harp Bow.]

### "Prophecy"
[Location Found: Stormveil Castle, within a tower]

Depicts a view from the Heretical Rise in the Mountaintops of the Giants. The title "Prophecy" connects to the heretical studies conducted in the rise — forbidden knowledge about what is to come. The artist painted a future, or a warning.

[Painting Location: Near Heretical Rise, Mountaintops of the Giants. Reward: Warhawk Ashes.]

### "Flightless Bird"
[Location Found: Fortified Manor in Leyndell (the Roundtable Hold's physical counterpart)]

Depicts a view from the top of the Windmill Village in the Altus Plateau, looking over the dancing celebrants below. The "flightless bird" is a metaphor for aspiration thwarted — the desire to rise above one's circumstances without the means to do so. The Windmill Village's festive horror (the women dance amid flayed corpses) makes this title darkly ironic.

[Painting Location: Near the Windmill Village summit, Altus Plateau. Reward: Fire's Deadly Sin incantation.]

### "Sorcerer"
[Location Found: Artist's Shack on Mt. Gelmir]

Depicts a view from the Hermit Village area of Mt. Gelmir, with the volcanic landscape spread below. The sorcerer of the title is likely the Demi-Human Queen Maggie, who wields sorcery from this isolated peak. The painting captures a place of solitary, dangerous knowledge.

[Painting Location: Hermit Village, Mt. Gelmir. Reward: Greathood helm.]
