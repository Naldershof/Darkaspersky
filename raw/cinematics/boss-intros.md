# Boss Introduction Cinematics

## Margit, the Fell Omen

[Visual: The player approaches the castle gate. A figure drops from above, landing with impossible grace. Margit stands, his cursed staff glowing with golden light, his Omen tail visible beneath his cloak.]

> "Foul Tarnished, in search of the Elden Ring. Emboldened by the flame of ambition. Someone must extinguish thy flame."

> "Let it be Margit the Fell!"

[Visual: He conjures spectral golden weapons — daggers, hammers — from thin air. His movements are elegant, almost regal, belying his cursed Omen nature. He is Morgott in disguise, the capital's secret king personally blocking the first chokepoint.]

---

## Godrick the Grafted

[Visual: A throne room of grafted limbs. Godrick paces, multiple arms twitching. He sees the Tarnished and his expression shifts between rage and insecurity.]

> "A lowly Tarnished... playing as a Lord. I command thee, kneel!"

> "I am the lord of all that is golden!"

[Visual: He extends his many grafted arms in a gesture of dominion that would be impressive if it weren't so pathetic. The arms are mismatched — different sizes, different skin tones, some armored, some bare. They are trophies of the dead.]

### Mid-Fight Cinematic (Phase 2)

[Visual: Godrick seizes a dead dragon's head and hacks off his own arm to graft it in place.]

> "Forefathers one and all... BEAR WITNESS!"

[Visual: He slams the dragon's head onto his stump. The dragon resists — its jaws snap — but Godrick forces it into submission. Fire erupts from the grafted head.]

---

## Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon

[Visual: The Grand Library of Raya Lucaria. Rennala floats in a golden bubble of light, surrounded by her sweetings — crawling students who sing a lullaby. Her expression is vacant, lost. She clutches the amber egg Radagon left her.]

(Rennala does not speak during her first phase. The sweetings sing for her.)

### Phase 2 — Ranni's Projection

[Visual: The amber egg cracks. A projection of Ranni's power fills Rennala with strength. She stands, regal and terrifying, the Full Moon rising behind her.]

> (No spoken dialogue in this phase. The cinematic is visual: the moon rises, Rennala's eyes clear, and she becomes the sorceress-queen she once was.)

[Visual: She summons spectral warriors, dragons, and wolves — fragments of past champions — while hurling lunar sorceries. Ranni protects her mother even now, from beyond her own death.]

---

## Starscourge Radahn — The Festival

[Visual: The player arrives at Redmane Castle for the Radahn Festival. Jerren, the old castellan, addresses the gathered warriors.]

> "The stars have aligned. The festival of combat is nigh. General Radahn, mightiest demigod of them all, awaits in the wastes of Caelid."

[Visual: The warriors charge onto a vast, red-scorched beach. In the distance, an impossibly large figure hunches over the sand. Radahn. He is eating corpses, his mind destroyed by the Scarlet Rot. His tiny horse Leonard struggles beneath his enormous frame.]

> (Radahn does not speak. His intro is his meteor rain — arrows the size of spears arc across the entire battlefield as you approach.)

[Visual: When engaged in melee, Radahn's scale becomes apparent. He dwarfs the player and every summoned ally. His swords, wreathed in gravitational magic, carve craters in the sand.]

### Phase 2

[Visual: Radahn leaps into the sky and returns as a meteor, crashing into the battlefield. The sky burns purple. He blazes with gravitational power, his body wreathed in cosmic energy.]

---

## Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy

[Visual: The lava lake beneath Volcano Manor. The God-Devouring Serpent erupts from the magma, enormous and ancient. In its midsection, Rykard's human body is visible — his face protruding from the serpent's flesh, crowned and grinning.]

> "Now, we can devour the gods together!"

### Phase 2

> "Join the Serpent King, as familyyy..."

[Visual: The serpent opens wider, revealing more of Rykard's body fused within. He draws a blasphemous blade of bone and magma. Skulls of the devoured orbit his body.]

> "Together, we will devour the very gods!"

---

## Morgott, the Omen King

[Visual: The Erdtree Sanctuary. A cloaked figure descends from above the thorned Erdtree entrance. He lands, straightens, and reveals himself — Morgott, horns now fully visible, no longer hiding behind the Margit disguise.]

> "Have it writ upon thy meager grave: Felled by King Morgott! Last of all kings."

[Visual: He conjures the same golden spectral weapons as Margit but with more variety and ferocity. His cursed blood ignites his staff. He fights with the fury of someone defending everything he believes in, knowing he will probably lose.]

> "The Erdtree... wards off all who deign approach."

[Visual: After defeat, Morgott crawls toward the Erdtree, reaching for it. The thorns remain. The tree he spent his life defending does not acknowledge him. He dies reaching for a god that never loved him back.]

---

## Fire Giant

[Visual: A vast snow field. The Fire Giant stands, chained and ancient. He is enormous — the player barely reaches his ankles. His body is covered in scars and red hair. The eye in his torso is closed.]

> (The Fire Giant does not speak. His intro is his emergence from the snow, chains dragging, a creature of deep antiquity forced into combat by the intrusion of yet another Erdtree-aligned warrior.)

### Phase 2

[Visual: The Fire Giant tears off his own leg, shattering his chains. The eye in his torso opens — the Fell God's vestige ignites. Fire erupts from his body. He rolls and thrashes, now powered by something older and more terrible than himself.]

---

## Malenia, Blade of Miquella

[Visual: A golden chamber at the base of the Haligtree. A scarlet lake of rot surrounds a raised platform. Malenia sits on a stone slab, her sword across her knees, head bowed. She waits. She has been waiting.]

> "I am Malenia. Blade of Miquella."

[Visual: She stands. Her prosthetic arm is a work of exquisite craft. Her helmet's wings frame blind, covered eyes. She draws her blade with a single, impossibly fluid motion.]

> "And I have never known defeat."

[Visual: The fight begins. She is fast — faster than anything the player has encountered. Her Waterfowl Dance is a whirlwind of slashes that fills the arena. She heals with every hit she lands, her blade drinking the player's life.]

### Phase 2 — Goddess of Rot

[Visual: Malenia collapses. A Scarlet Aeonia bloom erupts from her body, filling the arena with rot. From within the flower, she rises transformed: naked, winged with scarlet butterfly wings, her body wreathed in rot. She is the Goddess of Rot, the vessel fully given over to the outer god's power.]

> "Let your flesh be consumed. By the Scarlet Rot."

---

## Mohg, Lord of Blood

[Visual: Mohgwyn Palace. The blood-drenched arena. Miquella's cocoon looms in the background, the arm dangling limply. Mohg rises from a pool of blood, his Omen horns enormous, his trident wreathed in bloodflame.]

> "Dearest Miquella. Shall soon become a god."

> "Tres... Duo... Unus! Nihil! NIHIL! NIHIL!"

[Visual: The blood ritual circle ignites. Three curses mark the player (unless the Purifying Crystal Tear was used). Each "Nihil" drains their life, feeding it to Mohg. He ascends, wings of blood unfurling, becoming the Lord of Blood in truth.]

---

## Dragonlord Placidusax

[Visual: The player lies down on a stone platform. Reality shifts. They appear on a vast stone arena surrounded by storm. Placidusax is coiled at the center — two heads, three severed stumps, an ancient being of impossible age.]

> (Placidusax does not speak. He awakens slowly, his two heads rising, red lightning crackling between them. His movements are ponderous at first, then terrifyingly fast. He teleports through storms, breathing red lightning and fire.)

[Visual: In his second phase, he summons clouds that rain red lightning across the entire arena. He vanishes into the storm and reappears from impossible angles. The Dragonlord fights like a god — because he was one.]

---

## Maliketh, the Black Blade

### Phase 1 — Beast Clergyman

[Visual: A circular stone arena atop Farum Azula. The Beast Clergyman hunches, beast-like, hurling stone incantations. He is frantic, feral, guarding something precious.]

### Phase 2

[Visual: At half health, the Clergyman tears away his restraints and reveals the Black Blade — Destined Death itself, sealed in steel. He transforms into Maliketh, an armored black wolf-beast of terrifying agility.]

> "Marika... is this what you wanted? Is this... what you truly desired?"

[Visual: He leaps across pillars, flipping through the air, dragging the blade across reality itself. His attacks reduce the player's maximum HP — the Destined Death effect, temporarily removing the immortality that the Erdtree's grace provides.]

---

## Godfrey, First Elden Lord / Hoarah Loux

### Phase 1 — Godfrey

[Visual: The Erdtree's base in the Ashen Capital. A golden warrior descends, carrying the corpse of a spectral lion (Serosh) on his back. He is composed, regal, and fights with a massive axe in controlled, powerful strokes.]

> "A Tarnished... of no renown. Cross the fog, to the Lands Between, to stand before the Elden Ring. And become the Elden Lord."

> "Brave Tarnished... thy strength befits a crown."

### Phase 2 — Hoarah Loux

[Visual: At half health, Godfrey tears Serosh from his back and kills the lion — killing his own restraint. The golden armor cracks. Beneath is Hoarah Loux, the warrior chieftain: shirtless, scarred, eyes burning with primal fury.]

> "I've given thee courtesy enough."

> "Now I fight as Hoarah Loux! WARRIOR!"

[Visual: He discards his axe and fights bare-handed, grappling the player, slamming them into the ground, stomping craters into the arena. The refined first Elden Lord was always this: a barbarian who learned to play king.]

---

## Radagon of the Golden Order / Elden Beast

### Phase 1 — Radagon

[Visual: The Erdtree interior. Marika hangs crucified on the Fractured Marika statue, broken and suspended. As the player approaches, her body transforms — the feminine form shifts, cracks with golden light, and becomes Radagon: red-haired, muscular, wielding a golden hammer. He pulls himself from the crucifixion and attacks.]

> (Radagon does not speak. The silence is the statement. He is Marika's other half, and the fact that he fights to defend what she shattered is the game's central paradox made flesh.)

[Visual: He fights with holy incantations and his golden hammer, slamming the ground with blows that leave golden rune-scars. He is trying to repair the Elden Ring even as the player seeks to claim it.]

### Phase 2 — Elden Beast

[Visual: Radagon falls. Behind him, the Elden Ring itself writhes, and from it emerges the Elden Beast: a massive, ethereal, star-filled creature — part serpent, part whale, part nebula. It is the living incarnation of the Elden Ring, a vassal beast of the Greater Will.]

> (The Elden Beast does not speak. It sings. Its attacks are golden arcs, rings of light, and cosmic projections. It flees across a vast, water-floored arena beneath a sky of stars, forcing the player to chase it.)

[Visual: The fight takes place in a liminal space — a flat plane of shallow water under starlight. This is the space within the Erdtree, or the space where the Elden Ring exists. The beast's body contains a galaxy. Killing it means shattering whatever remains of the old order's divine framework.]
