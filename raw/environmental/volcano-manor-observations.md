# Volcano Manor — Environmental Observations

## Rykard's Domain

Volcano Manor sits atop Mt. Gelmir, an active volcanic mountain in northwestern Altus. The manor itself is a grand estate, surprisingly intact and even comfortable in its upper floors, where Tanith and the Recusant Tarnished operate. The contrast between the manor's civilized interior (furnished rooms, a dining area, portraits on walls) and the hellscape of lava and death below is the central environmental tension of this location.

Rykard, Praetor Rykard, one of Radagon and Rennala's children, chose to feed himself to the God-Devouring Serpent rather than accept the Golden Order's authority. His decision was framed as a rebellion: by becoming one with the serpent, he would grow powerful enough to devour the gods themselves. The manor serves as his court, and the Recusants (Tarnished who have rejected the Two Fingers' guidance) are his agents.

The manor operates as a functioning political faction, unique among demigod seats. Tanith acts as its steward, providing assassination targets (all of whom are aligned with the Erdtree or oppose Rykard) and rewarding completion. The assassination system reveals the manor's true purpose: it is a machine for weakening the Golden Order from within, using Tarnished as disposable killers.

[Context: The upper floors of Volcano Manor, Mt. Gelmir. The faction hub for the Recusant questline.]

## The God-Devouring Serpent

The boss encounter with Rykard occurs in a lava lake deep beneath the manor. The God-Devouring Serpent is a massive, ancient serpent (possibly predating the Erdtree era) that dwells in the volcanic depths. Rykard's body is visible protruding from the serpent's midsection: his face emerges from the snake's body, still wearing his crown, his expression somewhere between agony and ecstasy. He speaks from within the serpent, his voice distorted, offering the player a place within the serpent alongside him ("Join the Serpent King as familyyy").

The implication is that being devoured by the serpent does not mean death but absorption. Rykard lives within the serpent, controlling it (or being controlled by it, or some merger of both). The serpent grows by consuming powerful beings, and Rykard fed himself to it deliberately as a strategy. His Great Rune (the Rune of Blasphemy) allows him to restore health by killing enemies, mechanically reflecting the serpent's devouring nature.

The Serpent-Hunter great spear is found at the entrance to the arena, a weapon specifically designed to fight this creature. Its presence implies that the serpent has been fought before, that previous warriors came prepared for this exact battle. The serpent is an ancient, recurring threat, not something that appeared with Rykard.

[Context: The deepest lava chamber beneath Volcano Manor, accessible via a teleporter behind a sealed room in the manor.]

## Prison Town

Beneath the manor's refined upper floors lies Prison Town, a settlement built into the volcanic cavern. This area is a nightmare of lava flows, iron cages, and suffering. The inhabitants are prisoners, man-serpents, and tortured souls strung up on crosses or locked in cells. Iron maidens (the torture-device enemies) patrol the corridors.

Prison Town reveals the reality beneath Volcano Manor's genteel surface. The Recusants drink wine and accept assassination contracts in comfortable rooms while beneath them, prisoners are tormented and transformed. The town appears to serve as both a prison for the manor's enemies and a processing center where humans are converted into man-serpent hybrids.

The architecture of Prison Town is older and rougher than the manor above, suggesting it predates the current structure. The volcanic cavern was used for these purposes before Rykard established his court. The presence of Abductor Virgins (the iron maiden enemies) that can teleport captured players to this area from distant locations implies a kidnapping network feeding the prison system.

The descent from manor to Prison Town to lava temple mirrors the descent in Leyndell from golden city to sewers: every seat of power in the Lands Between hides atrocity beneath elegance.

[Context: The settlement area beneath Volcano Manor's main building, inside the volcanic cavern of Mt. Gelmir.]

## The Man-Serpents

The man-serpents of Volcano Manor are hybrid creatures with human torsos and serpentine features (or vice versa). They carry weapons, wear fragments of armor, and demonstrate intelligence. Some wield sorcery. Their presence throughout the manor's lower areas and the path to Rykard suggests they are either Rykard's creations, products of the God-Devouring Serpent's influence, or members of an older serpent cult.

The gradation of man-serpent forms is telling. Some appear mostly human with serpentine features; others are mostly serpent with vestigial human elements. This spectrum suggests an ongoing process of transformation, with individuals at various stages of conversion. The most serpentine forms are found closest to Rykard's chamber, implying proximity to the serpent accelerates the change.

Man-serpent sorcerers use magma-based spells, indicating that the volcanic environment has been incorporated into their magical tradition. This adaptation suggests the man-serpents have existed in this volcanic environment for a long time, long enough to develop their own school of magic.

[Context: Found throughout Volcano Manor's lower areas, Prison Town, and the path to the God-Devouring Serpent.]

## The Lava Area Below and Mt. Gelmir's Exterior

Mt. Gelmir's exterior is a brutal volcanic landscape of lava flows, hardened magma platforms, and toxic fumaroles. The Ninth Mt. Gelmir Campsite and other Sites of Grace mark a path through an environment that is actively trying to kill the player through terrain damage. Full-Grown Fallingstar Beasts, magma wyrms, and other creatures inhabit the slopes.

The volcano's exterior features the remains of military fortifications (Fort Laiedd, various towers) that have been partially consumed by lava. These outposts were once part of the manor's defensive perimeter but have since been overwhelmed by volcanic activity. The mountain is not dormant; it is actively destructive, and the fact that Rykard chose to merge with a creature dwelling in its most dangerous depths speaks to his willingness to embrace annihilation as a strategy.

The Seethewater area on the mountain's slopes contains geysers and boiling water, and the Hermit Village nearby is populated by hostile demi-humans and a Demi-Human Queen who wields a staff. The isolation and hostility of every settlement on Mt. Gelmir reinforces the mountain as a place where all social order has broken down, replaced by raw power dynamics.

Patches appears on the slopes of Mt. Gelmir, attempting to push the player off a cliff. Even the game's recurring trickster figure is operating at the margins here, preying on those drawn to the manor's promises.

[Context: The exterior slopes of Mt. Gelmir, leading up to and surrounding Volcano Manor.]

## After Rykard's Defeat

After killing Rykard, the manor's inhabitants react with despair or resignation. Tanith goes to Rykard's corpse and begins eating it, consuming his remains to continue his vision of devouring the gods. A crucible knight appears to stop her (or to stop anyone from interfering). This act of cannibalistic devotion is the endpoint of the manor's ideology taken to its logical conclusion: if the serpent devours the divine to grow stronger, and Rykard devoured the serpent, then devouring Rykard continues the chain.

The Recusant NPCs leave or die. The manor's purpose dies with its lord, or transforms into something more personal and disturbing through Tanith's act.

[Context: Post-boss observations in the Rykard arena and the manor's upper floors.]
