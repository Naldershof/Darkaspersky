# Altus Plateau — Environmental Observations

## The Erdtree Approaches

The Altus Plateau represents the transition from the outer regions of the Lands Between to the inner sanctum of the Golden Order. Upon ascending via the Grand Lift of Dectus (or the Ruin-Strewn Precipice alternate path), the player is immediately struck by the Erdtree's proximity. It dominates the skyline, its golden canopy stretching overhead, and the land beneath is bathed in a warm golden light. The contrast with the grey, embattled regions below is stark.

The plateau is dotted with Minor Erdtrees, functioning churches, and road systems that suggest this was once the prosperous heartland of the Erdtree civilization. The roads are paved, flanked by golden markers, and lead in clear paths toward Leyndell. Caravans (large treasure chests pulled by trolls along fixed routes) still travel these roads, guarded by soldiers, suggesting commerce and logistics continue even in the post-Shattering world. That the caravans are guarded against bandits rather than monsters implies a semblance of civil order persists here.

However, the plateau is not untouched. Battlefields with siege equipment and scattered corpses indicate that armies have fought here. The outskirts of Leyndell are fortified against approach, and multiple hero's graves and catacombs suggest significant death even in this golden heartland. The beauty of the Altus Plateau serves the same narrative function as a gilded surface over rot: it looks like everything is fine until you look closely.

[Context: The elevated central region of the Lands Between, accessed via the Grand Lift of Dectus or the Ruin-Strewn Precipice. Between Liurnia/Limgrave below and Leyndell above.]

## Windmill Village (Dominula) — The Dancing Women and Flaying

The Dominula Windmill Village is one of the most disturbing locations in the Altus Plateau. Perched on a hillside among functioning windmills, the village is populated entirely by dancing women in festive clothing. They twirl and celebrate among the windmill blades with apparent joy. Their dance is accompanied by cheerful music.

Closer inspection reveals the horror: the women are dancing around flayed bodies. Skinned corpses hang from poles and are strewn across the village. Some of the dancing women carry weapons and will attack on sight, their festive demeanor switching instantly to violence. The flayed bodies appear to be men, and the celebration has a ritual quality, not random violence but an organized, joyful ceremony of slaughter.

The Godskin Apostle boss at the top of the village connects the flaying to the Godskin tradition. The Godskins are an ancient order that wears the skin of gods (or those with divine heritage), and their presence in this village implies the women's ritual is either a Godskin practice or a folk derivation of one. The flaying is not torture; it is worship, or harvest. The skins of the divine are the sacred material.

The windmill setting adds to the dissonance. Windmills are pastoral, agricultural symbols. Their blades turn peacefully while corpses hang below. The entire village is a masterclass in environmental horror through contrast: the cheerful and the ghastly occupying the same space with no acknowledgment of the contradiction.

[Context: Northeastern Altus Plateau, an elevated hillside village accessible from the main plateau roads. Contains a Godskin Apostle boss encounter.]

## Shaded Castle

The Shaded Castle sits in a poison-choked valley in the northern Altus Plateau, surrounded by toxic moats and mists. The castle itself is crumbling, its halls filled with Cleanrot Knights and poison-spewing Servants of Rot. The boss, Elemer of the Briar (the Bell Bearing Hunter), wields a sword telekinetically.

The castle's most significant lore element is the body found at its highest point: a corpse wearing the Valkyrie's Prosthesis, a mechanical arm designed for Malenia. The presence of Malenia's prosthesis here (rather than on her person at the Haligtree) and the Cleanrot Knights defending the castle suggest this location was connected to Malenia's forces during their march through the Lands Between. The castle may have served as a staging point or refuge during the campaign against Radahn.

The poison that fills the castle differs from the Scarlet Rot; this is mundane toxicity, not divine affliction. The Servants of Rot here are lesser beings who worship rot, not its true avatars. This distinction matters: the rot has inspired a cult following even among those not directly touched by the Outer God of Rot.

The Shaded Castle's remote, poisoned valley feels like a place of exile or quarantine. Its position between the Altus Plateau and Mt. Gelmir suggests it guards (or blocks) the passage between these regions, and its hostility to all comers indicates no faction currently claims it.

[Context: Northern Altus Plateau, in a valley between the main plateau and the path toward Mt. Gelmir and Volcano Manor.]

## The Sealed Tunnel and the Golden Order Infrastructure

The Altus Plateau contains several sealed tunnels (mining operations) that reveal the Golden Order's industrial side. These tunnels harvest Smithing Stones and other materials from the earth, operated by miners (some human, some transformed into crystal-encrusted workers). The tunnels are functional but their workers are hostile, suggesting either automation through magic or a breakdown in the labor system.

The Old Altus Tunnel and the Altus Tunnel proper show an infrastructure dedicated to maintaining the Golden Order's military power. Weapons, armor, and fortifications all require materials, and these mines supplied them. The continued operation of the mines even after the Shattering suggests that the resource extraction was so deeply embedded in the landscape that it persists without central direction.

[Context: Various mining tunnel entrances across the Altus Plateau, typically found at cliff faces or hillsides.]

## Lux Ruins and the Second Church of Marika

The Second Church of Marika on the Altus Plateau contains a statue of Marika and provides one of the map's Marika-associated Sites of Grace. The church is largely intact, unlike the ruined churches in Limgrave and elsewhere, reflecting the Altus Plateau's closer relationship to the Golden Order's maintained territory.

The Lux Ruins nearby are overgrown but contain a boss cellar, continuing the pattern of underground chambers beneath surface ruins. "Lux" (light) in the name connects to the golden/light imagery of the Erdtree civilization. These ruins may predate the current structures, representing an earlier phase of settlement on the plateau.

[Context: Central-western Altus Plateau, along the roads leading toward Leyndell.]

## The Battlefield Before Leyndell

The approaches to Leyndell from the Altus Plateau pass through extensive battlefields. Siege weaponry, broken barricades, and masses of corpses indicate that multiple assaults have been launched against the capital. The outer walls of Leyndell are garrisoned with soldiers and Tree Sentinels who actively prevent approach.

A Draconic Tree Sentinel guards the primary entrance, a golden-armored knight on a horse wreathed in lightning. This guardian is significantly more powerful than anything else on the plateau, creating a sharp difficulty spike that mechanically communicates the capital's defensive threshold. You are not welcome.

The Erdtree's roots are visible here, breaking through the ground in massive golden tendrils. They serve as both terrain features and symbolic barriers: the Erdtree itself is physically blocking approach to the capital. As the player gets closer to the center of power, the tree becomes not just a background element but an active presence in the landscape.

[Context: Eastern Altus Plateau, the transitional zone between the open plateau and the capital city of Leyndell.]

## Sainted Hero's Grave and the Minor Erdtrees

The Altus Plateau contains multiple hero's graves (burial catacombs for honored dead) and Minor Erdtrees. These hero's graves are more elaborate than those in Limgrave, featuring chariot traps, complex layouts, and more powerful guardian spirits. The dead honored here were of higher status, and their resting places reflect it.

The Minor Erdtrees on the plateau are healthier and more vibrant than those in outer regions, their golden canopies fuller and their surrounding areas more lush. This gradient of Erdtree health (vibrant near the center, withered at the periphery) suggests the Erdtree's power radiates outward with diminishing strength, or that the Shattering's effects were felt more acutely at greater distances from the source.

[Context: Scattered across the Altus Plateau, with multiple Minor Erdtrees and at least two hero's graves.]
