# Millicent — Complete Dialogue

## Caelid — Church of the Plague

> "I am... Millicent. Though I scarce remember even that."

> "The rot... it has taken so much from me. My arm. My memories. My strength."

> "I was born in the Swamp of Aeonia. At least, I think I was. That place... where the Scarlet Rot blooms most fiercely."

[Context: Millicent is found at the Church of the Plague in Caelid, gravely afflicted by Scarlet Rot. She has lost her right arm to the disease. She is barely conscious and struggling to survive. Gowry, the sage at his shack nearby, sends the player to find the Unalloyed Gold Needle to treat her.]

### After Receiving the Unalloyed Gold Needle

> "The needle... it eases the rot. I can feel it. The burning, the decay — it recedes."

> "Thank you. You have given me back myself."

> "Gowry told you to bring this to me? That old man... he has his own reasons for everything. But I am grateful nonetheless."

> "I must go. There is something I need to do. Something I need to find out."

> "Who I am. Where I came from. And why the rot chose me."

[Context: The Unalloyed Gold Needle — originally crafted by Miquella to ward off Outer God influence — suppresses the Scarlet Rot within Millicent. She regains her clarity and sets off on her own journey.]

## Altus Plateau — The Erdtree-Gazing Hill

> "We meet again. I have been travelling."

> "The needle helps, but the rot is not gone. It sleeps within me. Waiting."

> "I am seeking the Haligtree. Miquella's Haligtree. I believe that is where I will find my answers."

> "Malenia... the Blade of Miquella. The Goddess of Rot. I think... I think she is connected to me somehow."

> "I have her rot. Her curse. And sometimes, in my dreams, I see her. A woman with red hair, wielding a blade of pure light."

[Context: Millicent hints at her connection to Malenia. She is one of Malenia's daughters — or more precisely, one of several buds born from Malenia's Scarlet Aeonia bloom during her battle with Radahn in Caelid.]

### On the Journey

> "The world is vast. And cruel. But also beautiful."

> "Before the needle, I knew only rot. Pain. Decay. Now I can see flowers. Feel the wind. Taste clean water."

> "Whatever happens, I am grateful for this time."

## Miquella's Haligtree — Before the Choice

> "I made it. The Haligtree. Miquella's sanctuary."

> "This is where it all began. Where Malenia resided. Where the Scarlet Aeonia bloomed."

> "I am one of Malenia's buds. Born of the Scarlet Bloom. I understand that now."

> "The rot is my inheritance. My birthright. And I must decide what to do with it."

[Context: Millicent reaches the Haligtree and comes to terms with her origin. She is a being born from Malenia's Scarlet Rot — one of several sisters created when Malenia bloomed during her battle with Radahn.]

## The Prayer Room — The Final Choice

### If the Player Helps Millicent (Fighting Her Sisters)

> "They came for me. My sisters. They want me to embrace the rot. To become one with it."

> "But I refuse. The rot is not who I am. It is what was done to me."

> "Thank you for standing with me. Against them. Against what I was born to be."

[Context: Millicent's sisters — other Scarlet Aeonia buds — attack her, trying to force her back into the rot. The player can choose to help Millicent fight them off.]

### After Defeating Her Sisters

> "It is done. They are gone."

> "But the fight... it has weakened me. The needle can no longer hold the rot at bay."

> "I must remove the needle. Let the rot take me. But on MY terms."

> "I will not become a goddess of rot. I will not bloom. I choose to rot and die as myself. Millicent. Not as Malenia's heir."

> "This is my defiance. My freedom."

> "Take the needle. It served its purpose. And perhaps it will serve yours."

> "Thank you... for everything. For giving me the chance to choose."

[Context: Millicent removes the Unalloyed Gold Needle and succumbs to the Scarlet Rot, dying on her own terms. She refuses to bloom into a Scarlet Aeonia — refuses to perpetuate the cycle. The player receives the Unalloyed Gold Needle back, which can be used in the Scarlet Aeonia flower after defeating Malenia to obtain Miquella's Needle — the item that can reverse the Frenzied Flame ending.]

### If the Player Betrays Millicent (Fighting Against Her)

> "You... why? After everything..."

> "So this is how it ends. Betrayed by the one person I trusted."

[Context: The player can choose to invade Millicent instead, siding with her sisters. Defeating her yields the Millicent's Prosthesis talisman but locks out the Unalloyed Gold Needle's recovery.]

## Gowry — On Millicent

> "Millicent is special. A bud of the Scarlet Aeonia. Born from Malenia's bloom."

> "She carries within her the potential to become the next Goddess of Rot. To bloom, as Malenia bloomed."

> "That is why I sent you to save her. Not out of kindness — out of necessity. The Scarlet Rot must have its goddess."

> "If she blooms, the cycle continues. The rot spreads. And our mistress... Malenia... is reborn."

[Context: Gowry reveals his true motivation: he serves the Scarlet Rot (and by extension, the Outer God of Rot). He wanted Millicent alive not to cure her, but to ensure she would eventually bloom and become the next vessel for the Rot Goddess. Millicent's choice to die without blooming thwarts his plan.]

### Gowry — If Millicent Dies Without Blooming

> "No... she refused? She chose death over the bloom?"

> "Foolish girl. She had the power of a goddess within her, and she threw it away."

> "All my work. All my planning. For nothing."

[Context: Gowry is devastated if Millicent dies on her own terms. His reaction confirms that his apparent kindness was manipulation all along.]

## Notes on Millicent's Lore Significance

- Millicent is one of Malenia's "daughters" — buds born from the Scarlet Aeonia bloom during Malenia's fight with Radahn in Caelid.
- Her quest parallels the player's ability to resist the influence of Outer Gods. She is destined to become a vessel for the Outer God of Rot, but she CHOOSES to die rather than submit.
- The Unalloyed Gold Needle is Miquella's creation, designed to ward off Outer God influence. Its journey through Millicent's quest leads to obtaining Miquella's Needle, which can cure the Frenzied Flame.
- Gowry's manipulation of Millicent mirrors the Outer Gods' manipulation of the Lands Between — using mortal beings as vessels for cosmic power, regardless of their consent.
- Millicent's choice to die as herself rather than bloom as a goddess is one of the game's most powerful statements about free will and identity.
- Her prosthetic arm (a replacement for the one lost to rot) mirrors Malenia's own prosthetic limbs, reinforcing their connection.
- The Scarlet Aeonia flower left by Malenia's defeat is where the needle can be used — connecting Millicent's sacrifice directly to the final resolution of the rot's influence.
