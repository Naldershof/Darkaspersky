# Millicent — Complete Dialogue

## Caelid — Church of the Plague (First Meeting)

> (Millicent is found gravely ill, barely conscious, in the Church of the Plague in Caelid. She cannot speak coherently until the player provides the Unalloyed Gold Needle.)

### Before the Needle

> "The rot... it burns... I cannot..."

> "Who... am I? I cannot remember..."

[Context: Millicent is afflicted with the Scarlet Rot and has lost her memory. She is found in Gowry's care — the old man at the shack on the road to Sellia, Town of Sorcery. Gowry sends the player to find the Unalloyed Gold Needle (dropped by Commander O'Neil) to treat her.]

### After Receiving the Unalloyed Gold Needle

> "The needle... I can feel it. The rot recedes. If only a little."

> "I remember now. My name is Millicent. I was born in the Swamp of Aeonia. Where the Scarlet Rot blooms."

> "I have been afflicted with the rot since birth. It is in my blood. In my very being."

> "But I will not succumb. Not yet. I must find the truth of who I am."

[Context: The Unalloyed Gold Needle suppresses the Scarlet Rot. This is the same type of needle that Miquella crafted to help Malenia — the "unalloyed" gold resists Outer God corruption.]

## Gowry's Shack — Gowry on Millicent

> "Millicent is special. She is no ordinary girl."

> "She was born in the Swamp of Aeonia — the very spot where Malenia's Scarlet Rot first bloomed. Where Malenia's great flower opened during her battle with Radahn."

> "I believe Millicent is a daughter of Malenia. Or a bud. A child born of the rot itself."

> "The Scarlet Rot chose her. As it chose Malenia. Their fates are intertwined."

[Context: Gowry is a mysterious old man who serves the Scarlet Rot's interests. He believes Millicent is connected to Malenia — possibly one of several "buds" or offspring born from Malenia's Scarlet Aeonia bloom.]

## Altus Plateau — Erdtree-Gazing Hill

> "I have left Caelid behind. The needle holds the rot at bay, for now."

> "I must find my way to the Haligtree. Where Malenia waits."

> "If I am truly connected to her... if I am her daughter, her bud, her remnant... then I must know."

> "But I have lost my arm. The rot took it. I can barely fight."

[Context: Millicent has traveled from Caelid to the Altus Plateau. She has lost her right arm to the Scarlet Rot.]

### If Given the Valkyrie's Prosthetic Arm

> "A prosthetic arm? For me?"

> "It moves... as though it were my own. The craftsmanship is extraordinary."

> "This was made for a warrior. A valkyrie. Could it have been... Malenia's?"

> "Thank you. With this, I can fight again. I can continue my journey."

[Context: The Valkyrie's Prosthetic Arm is found in the Shaded Castle on the Altus Plateau. It connects to Malenia's title as the "Blade of Miquella" — she famously lost her arm and used a prosthetic in her battles.]

## Mountaintops of the Giants — Ancient Snow Valley Ruins

> "I can feel it still. The rot, deep inside me. The needle suppresses it, but it is always there. Waiting."

> "I think about Malenia often. She lived with this curse her entire life. She became the greatest swordswoman who ever lived, despite it."

> "And yet she could not defeat it. She surrendered to it, in the end. Let it bloom."

> "I will not make the same mistake. I will face my rot. And I will not surrender."

## Elphael, Brace of the Haligtree — Before the Final Choice

> "We have come to the Haligtree. Malenia's domain."

> "I can feel her presence. The rot that binds us. She is close."

> "This is where my journey ends. One way or another."

### On Her Identity

> "I know now what I am. A bud. Born of the Scarlet Rot. Of Malenia."

> "I am not her daughter in the way of flesh and blood. I am a part of her. A fragment of the rot that took root and became a person."

> "But I am also myself. Millicent. Not Malenia. Not the rot. Myself."

> "And I choose to resist."

## The Prayer Room — The Final Choice

### If the Player Helps Millicent (Assists Against the Sisters)

> (At the Prayer Room in Elphael, Millicent is attacked by her "sisters" — other buds of Malenia. The player can choose to help Millicent fight them off or to join them in killing Millicent.)

> "Thank you. They were my sisters. Other buds, like me. Born of the rot."

> "But they chose to surrender to it. To become the rot. I will not."

> "I remove the needle now. Of my own will. Not because the rot has won — but because I will not let Miquella's needle define me."

> "Let the rot take this body. I have lived as myself. Millicent. Not a puppet of the rot, nor of the needle."

> "This is my choice. My agency. The one thing the rot could never take."

> "Take the needle. It is unalloyed gold — Miquella's work. It may yet serve a purpose."

> "Goodbye. And... thank you. For everything."

> (Millicent removes the Unalloyed Gold Needle and succumbs to the Scarlet Rot. She dies, blooming into a scarlet flower.)

[Context: Millicent's death is an act of defiance and autonomy. She removes the needle not because she gives up, but because she refuses to exist as a vessel for anyone's purpose — not the rot's, not Miquella's. The needle she returns can be used in Malenia's boss arena to obtain Miquella's Needle — the item that can reverse the Frenzied Flame ending.]

### If the Player Kills Millicent (Joins the Sisters)

> "You... too? Why?"

> "I thought... we were friends..."

> "So this is my fate. Consumed. Like all the others."

[Context: If the player chooses to help the sisters kill Millicent, she dies and drops the Millicent's Prosthesis talisman. This is the cruel path — and it prevents obtaining the Unalloyed Gold Needle for Miquella's Needle.]

## Gowry — After Millicent's Death

### If Millicent Died Resisting the Rot

> "She... resisted? To the very end?"

> "No. NO. She was supposed to bloom. To become the next vessel of the Scarlet Rot. To carry the rot forward!"

> "That foolish girl. She has ruined everything."

> "The Scarlet Rot needed a new host. Malenia's bloom was not enough. Millicent was to be the continuation. The next flower."

> "And she threw it away. For what? Pride? Autonomy? FOOLISHNESS."

[Context: Gowry reveals his true nature — he was not helping Millicent out of kindness. He was cultivating her as the next vessel for the Scarlet Rot's Outer God. Her resistance ruins his plan.]

### If Millicent Died Consumed by Rot (Player Killed Her)

> "The flower blooms. As it should."

> "Millicent has become the rot. A new bud, blossoming. The cycle continues."

> "You have done well, Tarnished. The Scarlet Rot endures."

## Notes on Millicent's Lore Significance

- Millicent is a "bud" of Malenia — one of several beings born from the Scarlet Aeonia bloom. She is not Malenia's daughter in a traditional sense but a fragment of the rot given form.
- The Scarlet Rot is a curse from an Outer God (distinct from the Greater Will). Malenia was afflicted from birth, as was Millicent.
- Miquella's Unalloyed Gold Needle suppresses Outer God influence. It was originally crafted to help Malenia. In Millicent's possession, it serves the same purpose.
- Millicent's choice to remove the needle and die on her own terms is the game's strongest statement on autonomy: she refuses to be defined by the rot, by the needle, or by anyone's plan.
- Gowry is an agent of the Scarlet Rot's Outer God, disguised as a kindly old man. His entire purpose was to cultivate Millicent as the rot's next vessel.
- The Unalloyed Gold Needle, if returned after Millicent's death, can be used in Malenia's boss arena (after defeating her) to obtain Miquella's Needle — the only item that can reverse the Three Fingers' embrace and undo the Frenzied Flame.
- Millicent's sisters — the other buds — chose to embrace the rot. They represent the path Millicent rejected.
- The Valkyrie's Prosthetic mirrors Malenia's own prosthetic arm, further connecting them. Malenia lost her arm (and later her legs) to the rot.
