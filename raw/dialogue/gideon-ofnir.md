# Sir Gideon Ofnir, the All-Knowing — Complete Dialogue

## Roundtable Hold — First Meeting

> "Ahh, you must be one of the new Tarnished. I am Sir Gideon Ofnir. The All-Knowing. I lead the Roundtable Hold in the pursuit of the Elden Ring."

> "The Elden Ring has been shattered. Its shards, the Great Runes, are held by the demigods. If you would be Elden Lord, you must claim at least two Great Runes and present yourself before the Erdtree."

> "I, too, seek the Elden Ring. But unlike many, I seek knowledge above all else. To understand the demigods, their strengths, their weaknesses, their secrets. Knowledge is the truest form of power."

[Context: Gideon is found in his study at the Roundtable Hold, behind a closed door. He is the most learned of the Tarnished and has been studying the demigods extensively.]

---

## Gideon's Knowledge of the Demigods

### On Godrick the Grafted

> "Godrick the Grafted. Lord of Stormveil Castle. He is the weakest of the demigod shardbearers. A wretched creature who grafts the limbs of others onto himself to compensate for his own frailty."

> "He is of the Golden Lineage, descended from Godfrey and Marika. But his blood has thinned. He is a pale shadow of his ancestors."

> "A dragon was felled at Stormveil, and he grafted its head onto his arm. Pitiful, really. But do not underestimate desperation."

### On Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon

> "Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon. She holds court in the Grand Library of Raya Lucaria. But she is no longer herself."

> "When Radagon left her to become Queen Marika's consort, Rennala was devastated. She retreated into madness, clutching the great rune Radagon left her — an amber egg, a parting gift."

> "She uses it to attempt rebirth. Over and over. But the rebirths are imperfect. Larval. Her students protect her out of loyalty, or perhaps pity."

> "The Great Rune she holds is not truly hers. It was given by Radagon. A rune of rebirth."

### On General Radahn

> "Starscourge Radahn. The mightiest of the demigods, in terms of sheer martial prowess. He holds the stars in place with his gravitational magic. The very sky is his prisoner."

> "But the Scarlet Rot has claimed him. He fought Malenia to a standstill at the Battle of Aeonia, but the Rot she unleashed consumed him. Now he wanders the sands of Caelid, mindless. A husk."

> "His festival is held at Redmane Castle. Warriors gather to grant him an honorable death. He loved his steed so much that he learned gravity magic to reduce his own weight, so as not to crush it."

### On Morgott, the Omen King

> "Morgott, the Omen King. He who rules Leyndell as its hidden sovereign. He is cursed — born an Omen, with horns and a tail. A thing to be shunned, even by his own royal family."

> "Yet he is the most devoted of Marika's children. The most loyal to the Erdtree. He defends Leyndell against all Tarnished, despite being born a thing the Golden Order would have expunged."

> "His brother, Mohg, shares the Omen curse. But where Morgott fights for the Order that despises him, Mohg has abandoned it entirely."

### On Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy

> "Rykard, Praetor Rykard. Once a demigod of the Golden Order, now... something else entirely."

> "He allowed himself to be devoured by the God-Devouring Serpent of Mt. Gelmir. Now he and the serpent are one. He seeks to devour the gods themselves."

> "His consort, Lady Tanith, leads Volcano Manor — a den of assassins who hunt Tarnished in his name. She calls it 'recusancy' — defiance of the Erdtree."

> "Together, they amass power. Champion after champion, fed to the serpent. All to fuel Rykard's blasphemous ambition."

### On Malenia, Blade of Miquella

> "Malenia. The Blade of Miquella. The most dangerous of all the demigods. Perhaps the most dangerous being in all the Lands Between."

> "She was born afflicted by the Scarlet Rot — a curse from an Outer God. Yet she became the greatest swordswoman who ever lived. She has never known defeat."

> "She clashed with Radahn at the Battle of Aeonia. When she could not defeat him by the blade alone, she unleashed the Rot. It consumed Caelid. And Radahn's mind."

> "Now she waits at the Haligtree, in an endless dream. Waiting for her brother, Miquella, to return."

### On Miquella

> "Miquella, the Unalloyed. Twin of Malenia. The most fearsome Empyrean of all."

> "He is cursed with eternal youth — he cannot age, cannot grow. And so he attempted to grow the Haligtree, a new Erdtree, using his own blood as nourishment."

> "But he was stolen. Ripped from the Haligtree's cocoon by Mohg, Lord of Blood. Mohg seeks to make Miquella his consort, to become a god."

> "Miquella's fate is... uncertain. His power of compulsion — the ability to make others love him — may yet be the key."

### On Mohg, Lord of Blood

> "Mohg. Twin brother to Morgott. Both born Omen. But where Morgott clung to the Golden Order, Mohg embraced an Outer God — the Formless Mother."

> "He rules the Mohgwyn Dynasty, deep underground. A palace built on blood. He has stolen Miquella from the Haligtree and seeks to establish a new dynasty with Miquella as its god."

> "The blood ritual he performs... it is meant to elevate Miquella. To make him a god of blood. Whether Miquella consents is another matter entirely."

## Gideon — Responding to the Player's Progress

### After Defeating Multiple Demigods

> "You are proving yourself capable. More capable than I had anticipated."

> "Two Great Runes. That is the threshold. With two Great Runes, you may present yourself before the Erdtree."

### After the Player Gains Significant Power

> "Hmm. You have come far. Farther than any other Tarnished in recent memory."

> "But tell me — do you truly intend to become Elden Lord? Do you understand what that means? What it costs?"

### On the Erdtree's Thorns

> "The Erdtree rejects you? Of course it does. The thorns are a ward. A seal placed by... well. That is the question, isn't it?"

> "The Two Fingers are silent. They have not spoken in some time. Perhaps they cannot guide us any further."

## Gideon — On Specific Lore Topics

### On the Night of the Black Knives

> "The Night of the Black Knives. The night Godwyn the Golden was slain. The first death of a demigod since the Rune of Death was sealed away."

> "The Black Knife Assassins were Numen women — of the same stock as Queen Marika herself. Their blades were imbued with a fragment of the Rune of Death, stolen from Maliketh."

> "Who orchestrated the plot? That is the great mystery. But consider: who benefited? And who lost the most?"

### On the Tarnished

> "We are Tarnished. Stripped of grace by Queen Marika herself, and cast out of the Lands Between. We fought wars in distant lands. We died. And then... we were called back."

> "The guidance of grace led us here. But to what end? Are we tools of the Greater Will? Or of Marika? Or something else entirely?"

### On the Greater Will

> "The Greater Will. The Outer God that sent the Elden Beast to the Lands Between. The beast became the Elden Ring — the embodiment of Order."

> "The Greater Will speaks through the Two Fingers. Or it did. The Fingers have been silent for some time now."

## Gideon — Late Game (After the Erdtree Burns)

> "The Erdtree burns. The Golden Order crumbles. And still you press forward."

> "I have learned a great deal, Tarnished. More than any other. And what I have learned is this: no one can become Elden Lord. It is not meant to be."

> "The gods do not want a new Lord. Marika does not want a new Lord. The Greater Will does not want a new Lord. It is all a farce."

> "And yet you persist. How very... Tarnished of you."

[Context: Gideon's disillusionment. His obsession with knowledge has led him to the conclusion that the quest for the Elden Ring is futile. This sets up his boss fight.]

## Gideon Ofnir — Boss Fight (Erdtree Sanctuary)

> "Ahh, I knew you'd come. The final obstacle before the Erdtree."

> "A Tarnished cannot become a Lord. Not even you."

> "I am Sir Gideon Ofnir, the All-Knowing. And I know... that the age of the Erdtree is over."

> "No one will be Elden Lord. The ring is better left shattered."

> "Give up, Tarnished. There is no hope. Not for you. Not for any of us."

[Context: Gideon blocks the path to the Erdtree as a boss fight. He uses incantations based on the demigods' powers — proof of his vast knowledge. He has concluded that becoming Elden Lord is impossible and seeks to prevent the player from trying. His All-Knowing nature is his downfall: he knows too much to have faith.]

### During the Fight

> "I know... the secret of Malenia."

> "I know... the blasphemy of Rykard."

> "I know... how Miquella was stolen."

[Context: He uses demigod-themed abilities during the fight, demonstrating that his knowledge is not merely academic — he has learned to wield their powers.]

### Upon Defeat

> "I know... in my bones... A Tarnished cannot become a Lord. Not even you..."

> "Marika... Queen Marika..."

[Context: Even in death, Gideon clings to his belief. His dying words invoking Marika suggest he understood something about her role in the futility of the quest.]

## Gideon — Miscellaneous Dialogue

### When Asked for Information

> "You wish to know more? Very well. Knowledge is meant to be shared. Within reason."

### On Ensha

> "Ensha is my servant. Pay him no mind. He is... zealous in his duties."

[Context: Ensha, who stands silently outside Gideon's study, eventually attacks the player after they obtain a piece of the Haligtree Secret Medallion. Gideon disavows this.]

### After Ensha's Attack

> "Ensha acted on his own. I assure you. He has been dealt with."

> "The Haligtree medallion, was it? Yes, I know of it. It leads to a hidden path. To the Consecrated Snowfield. And from there, to Miquella's Haligtree."

### On Nepheli Loux

> "Nepheli is my adopted daughter. I took her in as a child. She is fierce, but naive."

> "She lacks the capacity for the difficult decisions a leader must make. The world is not so simple as she believes."

[Context: Gideon adopted Nepheli but treats her as a tool. Their relationship deteriorates as Nepheli discovers his willingness to sacrifice innocents for knowledge.]

### After Learning of Nepheli's Disillusionment

> "Nepheli has left my service? So be it. She was always too sentimental."

> "The pursuit of knowledge requires sacrifice. She could not understand that."

## Notes on Gideon Ofnir's Lore Significance

- Gideon is the "All-Knowing" — a title that proves to be his curse. His knowledge leads him to nihilism rather than action.
- He is the leader of the Roundtable Hold, which mirrors the Roundtable of Elden Lords — Godfrey's war council.
- His boss fight uses abilities themed after the demigods — Malenia's Scarlet Rot, Rykard's blasphemous flames, and others. This proves he has studied each demigod to the point of replicating their power.
- He concludes that no Tarnished can become Elden Lord because the gods themselves do not want it. He may be right — but the player proves that will can overcome fate.
- His relationship with Nepheli mirrors the Golden Order's treatment of people as tools — used when convenient, discarded when not.
- Ensha's attack on the player suggests that Gideon, despite his claims of sharing knowledge, hoards the most valuable secrets and will resort to violence to protect them.
- His dying invocation of Marika suggests he understood her role in the Shattering — and possibly that her plan was never to have a successor at all.
