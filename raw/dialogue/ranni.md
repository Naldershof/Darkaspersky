# Ranni the Witch — Complete Dialogue

## Church of Elleh — First Meeting (Night)

> "I am known as Renna. I have a gift for you. I'd like you to have this spirit calling bell. And these ashes, for your first summoning."

> "Should you ever find yourself in need, pray summon the spirits with this bell. They are your warriors. Your shields. Your companions."

> "I bid thee take care, Tarnished."

[Context: Ranni appears under the alias "Renna" at the Church of Elleh at night, but only if the player has already acquired Torrent. She gives the Spirit Calling Bell and Lone Wolf Ashes. She vanishes and does not reappear here. If the player misses this, the bell can be bought from the Twin Maiden Husks at Roundtable Hold.]

---

## Ranni's Rise — Three Towers of Liurnia

### Initial Audience

> "Speak. I am Ranni the Witch. I stole Death long ago, and search now for the dark path. That is all thou need know."

> "I am Ranni, daughter of Rennala and Radagon. An Empyrean chosen by the Two Fingers. I have cast aside my Two Fingers, and my Great Rune, both. I would have thee enter my service."

> "I have no desire to be controlled by the Two Fingers. Nor do I wish to be the vessel of the Elden Ring. My purpose is my own. I act of my own volition."

[Context: At Ranni's Rise, the player finds Ranni on the upper floor with three servants: Blaidd, Iji, and Seluvis. She invites the player into her service.]

### Explaining Her Goal

> "Mine will be an order not of gold, but of the stars and moon. I would keep them far from this land. Mine order will not be of this world. Let the old ways die. I will not inherit the Erdtree or the fingers."

> "I stole a fragment of the Rune of Death. And used it to forge the Fingerslayer Blade. To commit a cardinal sin."

> "On the night of the plot, I slew mine own empyrean flesh. Casting aside my Two Fingers. And my Great Rune."

[Context: Ranni reveals the Night of the Black Knives. She orchestrated the theft of a fragment of the Rune of Death from Maliketh and used it to slay her own body, freeing herself from the Two Fingers' control. Her soul now inhabits a doll's body.]

### On Her Servants

> "Blaidd is my shadow. An assassin, bestowed upon me by the Two Fingers. Iji is my war counselor. And Seluvis... is a preceptor in my service. They are my steadfast allies."

[Context: Each servant has a role. Blaidd was assigned by the Fingers to watch Ranni, but his loyalty to her superseded his purpose.]

## Ranni's Questline — Sending the Player to Nokron

> "I would have thee enter Nokron, the Eternal City. Beneath these lands. There, you will find the Fingerslayer Blade. Bring it to me."

> "The Fingerslayer Blade was forged to slay the servants of the Two Fingers. With it, I will sever the hold the Fingers have upon this world."

[Context: This is the first major task. Reaching Nokron requires defeating General Radahn, whose gravitational magic holds the stars in place. When Radahn falls, a star crashes into Limgrave, opening the path to Nokron.]

---

## After Bringing the Fingerslayer Blade

> "Thou hast done well. The blade of the Nox. Fingerslayer Blade. With this, the deed will be done."

> "Now, I entrust this to thee. The Carian Inverted Statue. Use it at the Carian Study Hall. It will invert the rise, and reveal the path to the Devine Tower of Liurnia."

> "Atop that tower, thou shalt find the cursemark of my discarded flesh. Bring it to me."

[Context: She gives the Carian Inverted Statue, which inverts the Carian Study Hall, allowing access to the Divine Tower of Liurnia where her Cursemark of Death — the death rune imprint left on her corpse — awaits.]

---

## After Bringing the Cursemark of Death

> "The cursemark of my discarded flesh. At last. With this, and the blade, I can sever the hold of the Fingers."

> "I thank thee. Truly. Thou art a warrior of considerable strength. But the path ahead is perilous. More perilous still."

> "I will send thee to the lake of rot. Deep below this world. There, mine other body awaits. In the form of a doll."

[Context: She sends the player beneath Renna's Rise, through a coffin portal that leads to Ainsel River Main and eventually the Lake of Rot.]

## Miniature Ranni (Ainsel River)

### First Interaction (Picked Up)

> "..."

[Context: The player finds Miniature Ranni — a small doll — at a Site of Grace in Ainsel River Main. When first spoken to, she says nothing.]

### Second Interaction

> "..."

[Context: Still silent.]

### Third Interaction

> "Thou art... persistent. Very well. What is it thou wishest to say to a mere doll?"

> "I am known as Ranni the Witch. I would have thee do something for me. Head south. Deeper into the river. And when thou findest a Baleful Shadow, slay it. For me."

[Context: After speaking to the doll at the Site of Grace multiple times, Ranni finally responds. The Baleful Shadow is an assassin sent by the Two Fingers to destroy Ranni.]

### After Defeating the Baleful Shadow

> "Thou hast done well. The Baleful Shadow... an assassin sent by the Fingers. It was a danger to mine life."

> "I thank thee. Take this key. It will open a sealed chest, in the grand library of Raya Lucaria."

> "There, thou shalt find something precious to me."

[Context: She gives the Discarded Palace Key. The chest in Raya Lucaria's Grand Library contains the Dark Moon Ring, an engagement ring.]

## Lake of Rot — The Grand Cathedral

> (At the far end of the Lake of Rot, the player finds a coffin that leads to the Astel arena. After defeating Astel, Naturalborn of the Void, the path leads to the Moonlight Altar.)

[Context: No direct dialogue here, but the progression is critical to the quest.]

---

## Moonlight Altar — Cathedral of Manus Celes

> (At the end of the underground path, the player finds Ranni's original body — her true empyrean body — slumped beneath the cathedral. The Two Fingers, massive and dead, lie beside her.)

> (The player places the Dark Moon Ring on her finger.)

> "Thou... Thou wouldst be my Lord?"

> "How long I have waited. For this moment."

> "My dear consort, eternal. Let us go, together. To the dark path that lieth ahead."

[Context: This is the culmination of Ranni's questline. By placing the ring on her empyrean body's finger, the player pledges themselves as her consort. Her doll body absorbs her true body, and she rises in a new form — spectral, blue, empowered. This unlocks the Age of the Stars ending.]

---

## Age of the Stars Ending

> "Order, lay in ruin. Uncertainty, and the maddening taint of the flame of frenzy. The world needed no such things."

> "Mine will be an order not of gold, but the stars and moon of the chill night. I would keep them far from this land."

> "Here beginneth the chill night that encompasses all, reaching the great beyond. Into fear, doubt, and loneliness. As the path stretcheth into darkness."

> "Well then. Shall we? My dear consort, eternal."

[Context: Ranni's ending monologue. She establishes a new order — one of stars and moon, not the Golden Order. She takes the Elden Ring and her consort into the stars, removing the influence of the Outer Gods from the Lands Between. The "chill night" is her order — distant, unknowable, but liberating.]

## Ranni — Optional and Miscellaneous Dialogue

### If Spoken to Without Progressing the Quest

> "Is there something else? Then leave me be."

> "I have given thee thy task. See it done."

### On Blaidd

> "Blaidd is my shadow. He was given to me by the Fingers, when I was young. But he is my brother, more than their spy. He has fought for me, always."

### On Iji

> "Iji is my war counselor. He has served House Carian since before I was born. He is wise, and steadfast. I trust him above all others."

### On Seluvis

> "Seluvis is... a preceptor in my service. He is useful, though I trust him not. Keep a wary eye on him."

### If the Player Gives Seluvis's Potion to Ranni (Alternate Path)

> "... What is this? A potion? From Seluvis?"

> "Hmph. The wretch. He schemes against me, does he?"

> "Very well. I will deal with Seluvis in my own time."

[Context: If the player brings Seluvis's puppet potion to Ranni instead of administering it, she sees through the scheme. This leads to Seluvis's downfall.]

## Notes on Ranni's Lore Significance

- Ranni orchestrated the Night of the Black Knives alongside the Black Knife Assassins (Numen women, possibly connected to Marika's homeland). She provided the stolen fragment of the Rune of Death; the assassins used it to kill Godwyn the Golden's soul.
- Godwyn's body survived but his soul died. Ranni's body died but her soul survived. This is the "half-death" — a corruption of the natural order that created Deathroot and Those Who Live in Death.
- Ranni is the daughter of Rennala and Radagon. Since Radagon is Marika, Ranni is technically both a Carian princess and a child of Marika.
- Her doll body was crafted by the snow witch of the Carian Manor, her mentor.
- Her order removes the Outer Gods' influence — neither the Greater Will, nor the Frenzied Flame, nor any other. The stars represent freedom and the unknown.
- Blaidd is her shadow, a wolfman created by the Two Fingers to watch her. When Ranni defies the Fingers, Blaidd is driven mad — his purpose conflicts with his love for Ranni.
- The Miniature Ranni doll is a smaller copy of her current form. It may have been crafted as a backup or vessel.
