# Smithing Master Hewg — Complete Dialogue

## Roundtable Hold — First Meeting

> "What do you want? I'm a smithing master. I strengthen weapons."

> "Bring me your weapons and your stones. That's all I need."

> "Don't bother me with idle chatter. I have work to do."

[Context: Hewg is found at the smithy in the Roundtable Hold. He is chained to his anvil — literally shackled. He is the only blacksmith capable of strengthening weapons at the Hold.]

---

## On His Imprisonment

> "These chains? Don't concern yourself. I've been here longer than I can remember."

> "I was imprisoned. Bound to this table. Forced to smith. For all eternity."

> "Who chained me? I... I cannot remember. A woman. A queen, perhaps. She said I had a duty."

> "To forge a weapon. A weapon capable of slaying a god."

[Context: Hewg was chained to the Roundtable Hold by Queen Marika herself. His purpose: to forge a god-slaying weapon for the Tarnished who would eventually challenge the Elden Beast/Marika/Radagon. This implies Marika planned the Tarnished's return and armed them for the final battle.]

### On the God-Slaying Weapon

> "A weapon to slay a god. That was my charge. My purpose."

> "I have been working at it... for so long. I cannot say how long. Years. Decades. Centuries?"

> "Every weapon I strengthen, every blade I hone — it is all in service of that purpose."

> "But I have not yet succeeded. Not truly. The weapon eludes me."

> "Perhaps... you will bring me the right one. The blade that can bear the burden."

---

## On Roderika

### After Roderika Arrives at the Hold

> "That girl. The spirit tuner. She sits there all day, talking to her spirits."

> "Don't bother her. She's... she's doing important work."

> "What? No, I don't... I don't care about her. I'm just saying. She's useful."

### As Their Relationship Develops

> "Roderika? She's... fine. A good worker. Dedicated to her craft."

> "She reminds me of... someone. I can't remember who."

> "The spirits respond to her. She has a gift. I'd be a fool to deny that."

### Later

> "She asked me to teach her smithing. I told her no. I'm a weapons smith, not a nursemaid."

> "But she insisted. Sat right there and watched me work. For hours."

> "Stubborn girl. Reminds me of myself."

### Much Later

> "I suppose she's grown on me. The spirit tuner."

> "It's... nice. Having someone here. Someone who talks to me like I'm a person. Not just a tool."

> "Most Tarnished come here for what I can give them. She comes here for... me."

> "I won't say I'm grateful. But I won't say I'm not."

[Context: The relationship between Hewg and Roderika is one of the game's most tender subplots. The gruff, chained blacksmith slowly opens up to the gentle spirit tuner. Their bond is that of a father and daughter, or a master and apprentice.]

---

## On the Roundtable Hold

> "The Roundtable Hold. A gathering place for Tarnished. Built in the image of the original — Godfrey's war council."

> "This place is... not truly real. It exists outside the Lands Between. A pocket of grace, maintained by the Two Fingers."

> "When the Erdtree burns, this place may not endure. The grace that sustains it will falter."

---

## As the Erdtree Burns — Hewg's Fading Memory

### Early Signs

> "I... what was I saying? Forgive me. My memory is not what it was."

> "The chains grow heavier. Or perhaps I grow weaker."

> "The weapon... the god-slaying weapon... I must not forget. It is my purpose."

### Progressive Deterioration

> "Who are you? A Tarnished? Come for smithing, I expect."

> "I've been at this anvil for... how long? I cannot say."

> "There was a woman. She gave me a purpose. What was it?"

> "A weapon. I must forge a weapon. To slay... something. Someone."

> "Forgive me. The details escape me."

### Near the End

> "Roderika... that's her name, isn't it? The girl who sits beside me."

> "She speaks of spirits. Of warmth. I like the sound of her voice."

> "I don't want to forget. Not her. Not the weapon. Not... anything."

> "But the fire consumes. The world changes. And I am chained to this table."

### Final Dialogue (As the Hold Crumbles)

> "Who... where am I?"

> "This anvil. These chains. I've been here... always?"

> "A weapon. I must make a weapon. I promised... someone."

> "Please. Let me keep working. It's all I have left."

> "Roderika... I... I remember warmth. Beside me."

> "Don't let the fire take that. Please."

[Context: As the Erdtree burns and the Roundtable Hold begins to fade, Hewg's memory deteriorates completely. He forgets his purpose, his history, even Roderika. His final dialogues are heartbreaking — a man clinging to the last fragments of identity while everything slips away.]

## Notes on Hewg's Lore Significance

- Hewg was chained to the Roundtable Hold by Marika. His purpose — to forge a god-slaying weapon — reveals that Marika planned for the Tarnished to challenge and potentially destroy the Elden Beast, the Greater Will's emissary.
- This means Marika was working AGAINST the Greater Will while ostensibly serving it. The god-slaying weapon is intended to destroy the very entity that empowered her.
- Hewg is an Omen (or at least has Omen-like features — horns can be seen on his model). Like Morgott and Mohg, he was likely persecuted by the Golden Order.
- His fading memory parallels the Roundtable Hold's decay: as the Erdtree burns, the magic sustaining the Hold weakens, and Hewg — bound to it — decays with it.
- His relationship with Roderika humanizes both characters: Hewg, the eternal prisoner, finds companionship; Roderika, the traumatized maiden, finds purpose and family.
- The "god-slaying weapon" is never explicitly identified. Every weapon the player upgrades could be the one Hewg was meant to forge. The implication is that any weapon, sufficiently strengthened, can slay a god — it is the wielder, not the blade, that matters.
- Hewg's amnesia in the late game is one of the most emotionally devastating arcs. He is a prisoner who found happiness despite his chains, only to have even the memory of that happiness stripped away.
