# Blaidd the Half-Wolf — Complete Dialogue

## Mistwood Ruins — First Encounter

> (A howl echoes through the Mistwood. Looking up, the player can see a figure perched atop the ruins.)

[Context: Blaidd is first seen (but not spoken to) at the Mistwood Ruins in Limgrave. The player hears his wolf howl. Speaking to Merchant Kale about the howling leads to receiving the Finger Snap gesture to call him down.]

### After Using the Finger Snap

> "Well, you're a friendly one, aren't you? The name's Blaidd. I'm looking for a man who goes by Darriwil."

> "He fled to a gaol in this area. An evergaol. If you find him, let me know. I owe him a debt."

[Context: Blaidd is hunting Bloodhound Knight Darriwil, an enemy who betrayed Ranni. The evergaol is the Forlorn Hound Evergaol in southern Limgrave.]

### After Defeating Darriwil

> "You took care of Darriwil? I owe you one. Here, take this. A token of my gratitude."

> "The name's Blaidd. I serve Ranni the Witch. If you're ever in Liurnia, near the Three Sisters... seek out Ranni's Rise. Tell her Blaidd sent you."

[Context: Blaidd rewards the player with the Somber Smithing Stone [2] and introduces himself properly as Ranni's servant.]

---

## Ranni's Rise — In Ranni's Service

> "You came. Good. Ranni has need of strong warriors."

> "I am her shadow. Her blade. Where she wills, I go. What she commands, I do."

> "But more than that — she is my friend. My sister, if you'll forgive the sentiment. I've known her since she was young."

[Context: At Ranni's Rise, Blaidd is one of three servants present alongside War Counselor Iji and Preceptor Seluvis.]

### On the Task Ahead

> "Ranni wants us to find the entrance to Nokron. The Eternal City, deep underground."

> "There's supposed to be something down there. The Fingerslayer Blade. Ranni needs it."

> "But the path to Nokron is sealed. We'll need to find another way in."

---

## Siofra River — Investigating the Path to Nokron

> "I've been searching down here. The Siofra River. Thought maybe there'd be a way through to Nokron."

> "But no luck. The path is sealed tight. Ancient magic. Nox magic, maybe."

> "Iji might know something. The old war counselor. He's wise — wiser than me, at any rate."

[Context: Blaidd has descended into the Siofra River Well to search for a path to Nokron but has been unsuccessful. He suggests consulting Iji.]

### On Radahn

> "Iji says the stars are the key. The stars that Radahn holds in place with his gravity magic."

> "If Radahn falls, the stars will move again. And one of them will crash down and open the way to Nokron."

> "I know what you're thinking. Radahn is... formidable. But there's a festival at Redmane Castle. A gathering of warriors, all intent on granting the Starscourge an honorable death."

> "I'll be there. And I'd welcome you at my side."

---

## Redmane Castle — Festival of Radahn

### Before the Battle

> "This is it. The Festival of Radahn. Warriors from across the Lands Between, gathered to challenge the mightiest of the demigods."

> "Radahn... he was a great warrior once. The greatest, some say. But the Rot has taken his mind. He's nothing but a beast now. A beast that holds the stars hostage."

> "Let's give him the death he deserves. A warrior's death."

### During the Battle (Summon Dialogue)

> "For General Radahn!"

> "Have at you, Radahn!"

> "This ends now!"

[Context: Blaidd can be summoned during the Radahn boss fight, along with many other NPCs. The festival is a communal effort to bring down the Starscourge.]

### After Radahn's Defeat

> "We did it. Radahn is fallen."

> "Look — the stars. They're moving again."

> "One of them is falling... toward Limgrave. That must be it. The path to Nokron."

> "I'll meet you there. At the crater."

## After Nokron Opens

> "The way is open. Nokron, the Eternal City. Let's find what Ranni needs."

> "The Fingerslayer Blade. It's down there somewhere. Be careful — the Nox are ancient, and their creations are dangerous."

[Context: After Radahn's defeat, a star crashes into the Mistwood of Limgrave, creating a massive crater that leads to Nokron.]

---

## Blaidd — Forlorn Hound Evergaol (Imprisoned)

> "Iji... He locked me in here. In this evergaol."

> "He said I'd lose my mind. That as Ranni's shadow, given to her by the Two Fingers, I'd be compelled to turn on her when she defied the Fingers."

> "He said it was for Ranni's protection. To keep me from becoming a danger to her."

> "But I won't. I WON'T turn on Ranni. I am her blade. Her friend. I would die before I'd harm her."

> "Let me out. Please. I need to see this through."

[Context: After certain quest triggers, Iji traps Blaidd in the Forlorn Hound Evergaol to protect Ranni. Iji knows that shadows are bound to the Two Fingers' will and will inevitably turn against their Empyreans if those Empyreans defy the Fingers. The player can free Blaidd.]

---

## Blaidd — Outside Ranni's Rise (Endgame)

> (Blaidd is found outside Ranni's Rise, surrounded by the bodies of Black Knife Assassins he has slain. He is shaking, growling, no longer himself.)

> "Ranni... Ranni..."

> "I am her blade... Her shield... Her..."

> "GRAAAAAGH!"

> (He attacks the player on sight.)

[Context: After Ranni's quest is completed, Blaidd loses his mind. Iji's warning was correct — the Two Fingers' hold on him has driven him to madness. He fought off the Black Knife Assassins sent to kill Ranni, but in doing so, his mind shattered. He now attacks anyone who approaches.]

### Upon Defeat

> "Ranni... forgive me..."

> "I was... your blade... to the end..."

[Context: Blaidd dies fighting. He never turned on Ranni — he fought the assassins to protect her, even as his mind was being torn apart by the Fingers' compulsion. His death is one of the game's greatest tragedies. He drops the Royal Greatsword and his armor set.]

---

## Iji's Response to Blaidd's Fate

> "So Blaidd has... I see. I feared as much."

> "He was her shadow. Given by the Fingers. He was meant to be her watcher — her jailer, should she defy the Fingers' will."

> "But he loved her. More than any compulsion could overcome. He loved her until the very end."

> "I imprisoned him to spare him that fate. But it seems... fate cannot be escaped."

> "Forgive me, Blaidd."

[Context: Iji mourns Blaidd. Soon after, Iji himself is found dead outside his smithy, killed by Black Knife Assassins — the same ones Blaidd had been fighting off.]

## Notes on Blaidd's Lore Significance

- Blaidd is a "shadow" — a half-wolf being created by the Two Fingers and assigned to an Empyrean. His purpose was to watch Ranni and, if necessary, restrain or kill her should she defy the Fingers.
- Maliketh is another shadow — assigned to Marika. The parallel is deliberate: both shadows are ultimately at odds with their masters' desires.
- Blaidd's love for Ranni overrides his programming but cannot override it entirely. His mind shatters under the contradictions — he wants to protect Ranni, but the Fingers compel him to stop her.
- Iji's decision to imprison Blaidd is an act of mercy — he knows what's coming. But it's also tragic: it denies Blaidd the chance to fight alongside Ranni to the end.
- The Black Knife Assassins found outside Ranni's Rise were sent by the Two Fingers to eliminate Ranni. Blaidd killed them all before succumbing to madness. Even in his final moments, he protected her.
- Blaidd's wolf howl in the Mistwood is reminiscent of the Great Grey Wolf Sif from Dark Souls — another loyal companion driven to fight against the player to protect their master's legacy.
- His Royal Greatsword channels the chill magic of the dark moon — Ranni's power. Even his weapon reflects his devotion to her.
