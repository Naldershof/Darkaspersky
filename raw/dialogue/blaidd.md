# Blaidd — Complete Dialogue

## Mistwood Ruins — The Howling

> "..."

[Context: Before speaking to Blaidd, the player hears a wolf's howl echoing through the Mistwood Ruins. Merchant Kale recognizes the howl and teaches the player the Finger Snap gesture to get Blaidd's attention.]

### After Using Finger Snap

> "Hey. You. Down there. I'm Blaidd. A friend of Ranni's."

> "I'm looking for a man. Goes by the name Darriwil. He fled somewhere nearby. You seen him?"

[Context: Blaidd drops down from the ruins when the player uses Finger Snap. He is searching for Bloodhound Knight Darriwil, a traitor.]

### After Defeating Darriwil

> "You did it, did you? Good. Darriwil was a traitor. He needed to be put down."

> "I owe you for this. Here, take this. And if you ever find yourself in Ranni's service... give me a shout."

[Context: Blaidd gives the player the Somber Smithing Stone [2] as thanks. He mentions Ranni, foreshadowing the connection.]

## Siofra River — Searching for Nokron

> "Ah, you. Fancy meeting you here, underground."

> "I'm searching for the eternal city of Nokron. Ranni has need of a treasure that sleeps there."

> "But the way is barred. I've looked everywhere, and I can't find the bloody entrance."

> "...Wait. I know someone. Seluvis. That conniving little weasel might know something. Or Sellen — the sorceress. She studied the stars. Maybe she can tell us how to reach Nokron."

[Context: Blaidd is found in the Siofra River Well area, searching for a path to Nokron, Eternal City. Despite his thorough searching, the entrance only opens after Radahn's defeat sends a star crashing into the earth.]

### If Spoken to Again

> "The way forward... It's connected to the stars somehow. The stars are held in place by Radahn's power. His gravity magic keeps the cosmos still."

> "If we could defeat Radahn... the stars would move again. And perhaps one of them would show us the way to Nokron."

## Redmane Castle — Radahn Festival

### Before the Battle

> "Tarnished! You came. Good."

> "General Radahn. The mightiest demigod to remain in the Lands Between. Scarlet Rot has driven him mad, but his power is undimmed."

> "The festival is a celebration — and a mercy. Warriors from across the land, come to grant Radahn an honourable death."

> "I wouldn't miss this for the world. Fighting alongside you, against the mightiest of them all."

> "Let's go. Let's give Radahn the send-off he deserves."

[Context: The Radahn Festival gathers warriors to challenge the now-maddened General Radahn in the Wailing Dunes. Blaidd relishes the challenge.]

### After Defeating Radahn

> "We did it... General Radahn. He fought to the last."

> "A star fell. Did you see? It smashed into the earth, somewhere in Limgrave. That must be it — the way to Nokron."

> "I need to go. To Nokron. For Ranni."

> "This was a battle I'll never forget. Thank you, truly."

[Context: With Radahn defeated, a star crashes into the Lands Between near Fort Haight, opening the path to Nokron. Blaidd is exhilarated but focused on his mission for Ranni.]

## Ranni's Rise — In Service

> "So you've joined Ranni's service too, have you? Good. The more, the better."

> "I've served Ranni since before I could remember. She is my purpose. My reason."

> "Whatever she needs, I will provide. Without question."

[Context: Blaidd is found at Ranni's Rise as part of her entourage, alongside Iji and Seluvis. He is devoted absolutely to Ranni.]

### On Iji

> "Iji. The old smith. He's been with Ranni even longer than I have. A wise one, that. If a little overcautious."

### On Seluvis

> "Seluvis... I don't trust him. Never have. He's got his own agenda, that one. But Ranni keeps him around, so I hold my tongue."

## Forlorn Hound Evergaol — Imprisoned

> "...Let me out. Let me OUT!"

> "I am Ranni's blade! Her sworn shadow! Why have they done this to me?!"

> "Iji... did Iji do this? He said... he said the Two Fingers would send me mad. That I was made to betray Ranni."

> "That's a lie! I would NEVER betray her! Never!"

[Context: After progressing Ranni's quest far enough, Blaidd is found imprisoned in the Forlorn Hound Evergaol by Iji. Iji imprisoned him as a precaution — the Two Fingers created Blaidd as a shadow assigned to an Empyrean, and if that Empyrean defies the Greater Will, the shadow is meant to turn against them. Iji feared this failsafe would activate.]

### If Released

> "...Thank you. I don't know why Iji did this. But I know one thing — I will never turn on Ranni. Never."

> "I am her blade. That is all I am. All I need to be."

## Ranni's Rise — After Ranni's Quest Completion (Madness)

> "Ranni... Ranni..."

> "I'm... Ranni's..."

> "Grraaagh!"

[Context: After Ranni departs following the completion of her quest, Blaidd is found outside Ranni's Rise, hostile and maddened. The Two Fingers' failsafe has activated — Blaidd's programming as an Empyrean's shadow has overridden his will, driving him to madness because Ranni defied her fate. Despite his absolute devotion, his very nature has been turned against him. He attacks the player on sight.]

### After Defeating Blaidd

> "Ranni... my purpose... my very reason..."

> "I would have... given anything... to see her..."

> "...safe."

[Context: These are Blaidd's dying words. Even in madness, even with the Two Fingers' curse driving him to violence, his devotion to Ranni persists. He dies wishing for her safety. The player receives the Royal Greatsword and Blaidd's armor set.]

## Iji — On Blaidd's Fate

> "Blaidd was a gift to Ranni, from the Two Fingers. A loyal shadow to serve an Empyrean. But that loyalty was designed as a chain."

> "Should the Empyrean defy the Two Fingers, the shadow is destined to turn. To become a weapon against the one they love."

> "I imprisoned him to spare him that fate. And to spare Ranni the pain of facing him."

> "But it seems... he was freed. And the fate I feared came to pass. Poor Blaidd."

[Context: Iji explains why he imprisoned Blaidd. Iji is later found dead outside his smithy, killed by Black Knife Assassins — likely sent by the Two Fingers in retribution for aiding Ranni's defiance.]

## Notes on Blaidd's Lore Significance

- Blaidd is a half-wolf shadow, created by the Two Fingers and assigned to the Empyrean Ranni. His entire existence was designed as both servant and failsafe.
- His devotion to Ranni is genuine and absolute — but his nature as a shadow means he was literally built to betray her if she defied the Greater Will.
- The tragedy of Blaidd is that free will and destiny are at war within him. He CHOOSES Ranni, but his body and mind are MADE to turn against her.
- His imprisonment by Iji and eventual madness parallel the game's broader themes: the Greater Will's control systems are embedded in the very fabric of the world.
- Blaidd's Royal Greatsword deals magic damage and has a frost-based skill — reflecting his connection to Ranni and the cold of the Dark Moon.
- His howl in the Mistwood is one of the game's most atmospheric introductions, and Kale's recognition of it hints at deeper connections between the merchants and those who operate outside the Golden Order.
