# Minor NPCs — Key Dialogue

## D, Hunter of the Dead

> "Those Who Live in Death are a blight on the Erdtree. They defy the Golden Order's most fundamental tenet — that souls return to the Erdtree upon death. The Deathroot spreads their corruption."

[Context: D at Roundtable Hold, explaining his mission]

> "That woman... that Deathbed Companion. She harbors Those Who Live in Death. She is an enemy of the Golden Order."

[Context: D about Fia, foreshadowing their conflict]

> "Brother... forgive me..."

[Context: D's twin brother, found later, relating to D's death at Fia's hands]

---

## Gurranq, Beast Clergyman / Maliketh

> "Bring me more Deathroot. I hunger. The beast within... it demands more. I must consume it. All of it. The death that spreads... it is my failure. My sin."

[Context: Gurranq at the Bestial Sanctum in Dragonbarrow. He is Maliketh in disguise, consuming Deathroot in penance for failing to prevent the theft of Destined Death]

> "Traaitor! Thou... thou wilt betray me? As she did?"

[Context: After feeding him several Deathroot, Gurranq becomes aggressive — the beast within takes over. "She" likely refers to Ranni, who orchestrated the theft of the Rune of Death he was sworn to protect]

---

## Latenna the Albinauric

> "Please... take me to the Haligtree. Miquella promised us — the Albinaurics — a place free from persecution. The Golden Order hunts us because we were not born of the Erdtree. But Miquella said there was another way."

[Context: Latenna at Slumbering Wolf's Shack, after her wolf companion has died]

> "The Haligtree... it was supposed to be our salvation. But look at it. The rot has consumed it. Miquella is gone. Our sanctuary is a ruin."

[Context: If brought near the Haligtree area]

---

## White Mask Varre

> "You don't have a maiden, do you? How sad. A Tarnished without a maiden — like a knight without a lord. But I can offer you something better. The blood of the Lord of Blood. Come, join the communion."

[Context: Varre at the First Step, then later at Rose Church]

> "Luminary Mohg, the Lord of Blood, awaits at the Mohgwyn Dynasty. He shall usher in a new age — a dynasty of blood, with the young Miquella at its heart."

[Context: Varre when offering access to Mohgwyn Palace]

---

## Kenneth Haight

> "I am Kenneth Haight, next in line to be lord of Limgrave. Godrick, that craven coward, has claimed this land by force. He is no true lord. A true lord rules by right and virtue, not by grafting."

[Context: Kenneth on his tower in Limgrave]

---

## Thops

> "I was a student at the Academy, but they locked me out. I need a Glintstone Key to return. The sorcerers within... they've become insular. They study the stars no longer — only their own reflections."

[Context: Thops at the Church of Irith. His quest reveals the Academy's stagnation under Rennala's grief]

---

## Recusant Bernahl

> "The Golden Order must fall. Rykard showed us the way — through blasphemy, through the devouring of gods. Join Volcano Manor. Hunt the Tarnished who serve the Erdtree."

[Context: Bernahl at Volcano Manor, offering recusant missions]

---

## Ensha of the Royal Remains

> (Ensha does not speak — he attacks the player silently upon returning to the Roundtable Hold with a piece of the Haligtree Secret Medallion)

[Context: Ensha ambushes the player on Gideon's orders, seeking the medallion that leads to Miquella's Haligtree. His silence makes him one of the most unsettling encounters in the game]

---

## Blackguard Big Boggart

> "I'm a thief, I'll admit it. Stole that necklace right off the girl. But I've turned over a new leaf — now I sell boiled crab. Best in the Lands Between."

[Context: Blackguard at the Boilprawn Shack, then Altus Plateau moat]

> "Those Dung Eater... monsters... they got me... curse... spreading..."

[Context: If the Dung Eater questline progresses, Blackguard is found dying from the Omen curse]
