# Rogier — Complete Dialogue

## Stormveil Castle — Chapel

> "Oh, hello. A fellow Tarnished, I see. I am Rogier. A sorcerer, of sorts."

> "I've been investigating this castle. There's something beneath it. Something... unsettling."

> "Have you seen the roots? The Erdtree's roots extend deep below this castle. And there, at the base... I found something terrible."

[Context: Rogier is first encountered in the chapel area of Stormveil Castle. He serves as a summon for the Margit fight. He is investigating a mystery beneath the castle.]

### On What Lies Below

> "Beneath this castle, at the base of the Erdtree's roots, there is a great face. A vast, distorted face, growing from the roots. A face of death."

> "It is Godwyn. Or what remains of him. The Prince of Death. His body lives on, growing, fusing with the roots of the Erdtree."

> "I touched it. And now... this."

[Context: Rogier found Godwyn's corrupted body beneath Stormveil Castle. The face — a massive, fish-eyed, lifeless visage — is Godwyn's corpse fused with the Erdtree roots. By touching it, Rogier has been afflicted with Deathblight.]

## Roundtable Hold — After Stormveil

### Initial Conversations

> "Ah, we meet again. I've made my way to the Roundtable Hold. My legs... they no longer work as they should."

> "The Deathblight. It spreads from the wound. From where I touched Godwyn's remains."

> "But I cannot stop now. The mystery of the Night of the Black Knives — I must unravel it. Even if it costs me everything."

[Context: Rogier appears at the Roundtable Hold in a wheelchair or seated position, his legs paralyzed by Deathblight. Despite his condition, he continues his investigation.]

### On the Night of the Black Knives

> "The Night of the Black Knives. That is the key. The night everything changed."

> "Godwyn the Golden was the first demigod to die. Slain by the Black Knife Assassins. But it was not a true death. His soul was killed, but his body persisted."

> "At the same time, someone else died that night. But only their body — their soul survived. Two half-deaths, on the same night. One soul killed, one body killed."

> "A half-death each. The Rune of Death was split in two, you see. The cursemark, divided. One half for each victim."

[Context: This is critical lore. The Night of the Black Knives involved TWO deaths — Godwyn's soul and Ranni's body. The stolen fragment of the Rune of Death was split, creating a shared curse.]

### On Ranni's Involvement

> "The other victim... the one whose body was killed... was Ranni. Ranni the Witch."

> "She orchestrated it all. She stole the fragment of the Rune of Death from Maliketh. She gave it to the Black Knife Assassins. And she used it to slay her own flesh."

> "Her body died, but her soul persisted. Transferred to a doll. And Godwyn's soul died, but his body persisted. Corrupting the Erdtree from within."

> "The two deaths were linked. Inseparable. That is the nature of the cursemark."

### On the Black Knife Print

> "I found this. A Black Knife print. Evidence of the conspiracy."

> "The imprint of a Black Knife — the weapons used on the Night of the Black Knives. Infused with the Rune of Death."

> "Take it to Fia. The Deathbed Companion. She may know more. She has... a connection to these matters."

[Context: Rogier gives the player the Black Knifeprint, which can be shown to Fia to advance her questline and unlock further lore about the Night of the Black Knives.]

### On D, Hunter of the Dead

> "D. He hunts Those Who Live in Death. Agents of the Golden Order."

> "He and I do not see eye to eye. He believes Those Who Live in Death are abominations. I believe they are victims — symptoms of a greater corruption."

> "The corruption begins with Godwyn. His half-death spawned the Deathroot, which spawns the living dead. Cure the disease, not the symptom."

## Rogier — Later Conversations (Deterioration)

### As the Deathblight Spreads

> "The blight grows worse. I can feel it... spreading. My legs are gone. Soon, my arms."

> "But the investigation is not yet complete. There are still answers to be found."

> "The cursemark... the other half. Ranni has it. Or knows where it is."

### His Final Request

> "Tarnished... I have a request. When I am gone... continue the investigation."

> "Find the truth of the Night of the Black Knives. Find the cursemark. And understand... why Ranni did what she did."

> "Was it to free herself from the Two Fingers? To defy the Greater Will? Or something else?"

> "I believe Ranni acted for a greater purpose. To break the hold of the Outer Gods upon this world. But I may be wrong."

### Final Words

> "I am glad... to have met you. A fellow seeker of truth."

> "The Deathblight will take me soon. But death holds no fear for me now. I have seen it. Godwyn's death. A fate worse than any end."

> "At least mine will be... complete."

[Context: Rogier succumbs to the Deathblight and dies in his chair at the Roundtable Hold. His body can be found there afterward. He leaves behind his rapier and his Bell Bearing.]

## Notes on Rogier's Lore Significance

- Rogier is one of the game's primary lore exposition characters. He connects the Stormveil mystery (Godwyn's corpse) to the broader Night of the Black Knives conspiracy.
- His discovery that TWO half-deaths occurred on the Night of the Black Knives is crucial: Godwyn's soul + Ranni's body. The Rune of Death was split between them.
- The Black Knifeprint he provides is a key item that connects his quest to Fia's and eventually to Ranni's.
- His Deathblight affliction demonstrates the danger of the Deathroot corruption — merely touching Godwyn's remains is enough to inflict a fatal curse.
- His investigation parallels D's but from a sympathetic perspective: where D hunts the living dead, Rogier seeks to understand their origin and cure the root cause.
- He is a Carian sorcerer, connecting him to Ranni's faction (House Carian).
- His death at the Roundtable Hold is one of the game's quieter tragedies — a scholar who got too close to a terrible truth and paid with his life.
- The rapier he leaves behind, Rogier's Rapier, is infused with Deathblight — proof of his contact with Godwyn's remains.
