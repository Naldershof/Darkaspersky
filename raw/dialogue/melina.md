# Melina — Complete Dialogue

## First Encounter — Third Site of Grace (Gatefront or Similar)

> "Tarnished, are you ready? To commit a cardinal sin?"

[Context: Brief line heard as she materializes. Only appears in some versions of the encounter.]

> "I can play the role of maiden. Granting you the ability to turn runes into strength. To aid you in your search for the Elden Ring. You need only take me with you. To the foot of the Erdtree."

[Context: Melina appears automatically once the player has rested at three Sites of Grace in the open world. She offers her accord — the player must accept to level up.]

> "Then it's settled. I will stay with you, as a companion, for the time being."

> "Ah, I should say. My purpose was given to me by my mother inside the Erdtree, long ago. For the purpose of a journey. I was told... to head for the foot of the Erdtree."

[Context: She mentions her mother without naming her. Strongly implied to be Queen Marika.]

### If the Player Declines the Accord

> "I understand. But should you change your mind, I will be here. At the next site of grace."

[Context: The player can refuse Melina's accord. She reappears at subsequent Sites of Grace until accepted.]

[Context: She gives the player the Spectral Steed Whistle to summon Torrent. This happens shortly after accepting the accord.]

> "May I please have a word? There is something I'd like to say."

> "I was born at the foot of the Erdtree. But my mother's purpose for me was unfulfilled. My body... I was born without a body. I am naught but a spirit. A wandering, bodiless spirit."

> "Should I not be able to offer you guidance to the foot of the Erdtree, I ask that you still take me with you. To the foot of the Erdtree."

[Context: At the Stormhill Shack Site of Grace, Melina reveals more about her nature — bodiless, spiritual. This is optional and easily missed.]

## Melina at Various Churches — Queen Marika's Words

### Church of Pilgrimage (Weeping Peninsula)

> "Spoken echoes of Queen Marika linger here, as well. Shall I share them with you?"

> "In Marika's own words. I declare mine intent, to search the depths of the Golden Order. Through understanding of the proper way, our faith, our Golden Order, shall be made whole again."

[Context: These "In Marika's own words" segments appear when Melina reads Marika's dialogue from specific churches. They are critical lore. Marika sought to reform the Golden Order from within.]

### Third Church of Marika (Limgrave)

> "Spoken echoes of Queen Marika linger here, as well. Shall I share them with you?"

> "In Marika's own words. My Lord, and thy warriors. I divest each of thee of thy grace. With thine eyes dimmed, ye will be driven from the Lands Between. Ye will wage war in a land afar, where ye will live, and die."

> "Then, after thy death, I will give back what I once claimed. Return to the Lands Between, and have thy deathbed companion."

[Context: The crucial revelation that Marika herself was responsible for stripping grace from the Tarnished and sending them away, with the plan that they would return. The "deathbed companion" likely refers to the Finger Maiden.]

### Church of Irith (Liurnia)

> "Spoken echoes of Queen Marika linger here, as well. Shall I share them with you?"

> "In Marika's own words. O Radagon, leal hound of the Golden Order. Thou'rt yet to become me, thou'rt yet to become a god. Let us both be shattered, mine other self."

[Context: One of the most critical lore revelations — Marika and Radagon are one being, or two halves of the same being. Marika commanded the Shattering.]

### Second Church of Marika (Altus Plateau)

> "Spoken echoes of Queen Marika linger here, as well. Shall I share them with you?"

> "In Marika's own words. Hear me, Demigods. My children beloved. Make of thyselves that which ye desire. Be it a Lord. Be it a God. But should ye fail to become aught at all, ye will be forsaken. No different from the beasts."

[Context: Marika addressing her demigod children, revealing her expectations and ultimatums.]

### Church of the Crusade (Altus Plateau)

> "Spoken echoes of Queen Marika linger here, as well. Shall I share them with you?"

> "In Marika's own words. The Erdtree governs all. The choice is thine. Become one with the Order. Or resist it to the last."

[Context: A statement about the nature of the Golden Order's dominion and the binary choice it offers.]

### First Church of Marika (Mountaintops of the Giants)

> "Spoken echoes of Queen Marika linger here, as well. Shall I share them with you?"

> "In Marika's own words. Hark, brave warriors. Hark, my lord Godfrey. We commend yourستance. But the battle is over. I bid you, warriors. Put down your arms. And rest in grace."

[Context: Marika dismissing Godfrey and his warriors after the war against the Giants. This is the moment the Tarnished lost their grace and were exiled.]

### Church of Repose (Mountaintops of the Giants)

> "Spoken echoes of Queen Marika linger here, as well. Shall I share them with you?"

> "In Marika's own words. Brandish the Elden Ring, for all the Lands Between. The seat of the Elden Ring, and I, are one. And what is one... cannot be broken."

[Context: Marika's assertion that the Elden Ring and she are inseparable — making her later Shattering all the more profound as an act of self-destruction.]

> "The Erdtree is warded by thorns. To reach the foot of the Erdtree, the thorns must be burned away."

## Notes on Melina's Identity and Lore Significance

- Melina's closed left eye, when opened, burns with dark flame. The Gloam-Eyed Queen wielded Destined Death and had the power to slay gods. The connection is strongly implied.
- Her mother is Marika, making Melina a possible Empyrean and sibling to the demigods.
- She can read Marika's words at churches, suggesting a unique spiritual connection or shared consciousness with Marika.
- Her name shares etymology with Malenia (both from the root "Mel-"), suggesting a sibling connection.
- She is bodiless — she exists as a spirit, visible only at Sites of Grace. This is unusual and unexplained.
- The Frenzied Flame path is the only route where Melina survives, but she becomes an antagonist.
- She is distinct from a true Finger Maiden — she says she can "play the role" of one, but she is not one. This implies the player's original Finger Maiden died (seen at the Chapel of Anticipation).
- Her self-sacrifice mirrors the fate of many maidens in the Lands Between — used as kindling, fuel, or vessels.
