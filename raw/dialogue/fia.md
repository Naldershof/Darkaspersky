# Fia, Deathbed Companion — Complete Dialogue

## Roundtable Hold — First Meeting

> "Tarnished, are you? Come, lay your weary head upon my lap. I will hold you, for a time."

> "I am Fia. A deathbed companion. I offer my embrace, and my warmth. In return, I would ask but a small thing of you. Your vitality."

[Context: Fia is found in a bedchamber at the Roundtable Hold. Her embrace grants the Baldachin's Blessing consumable but also reduces the player's HP by a small percentage until the Blessing is used. She is a Deathbed Companion — one who lies with champions of the Roundtable before they die, absorbing their vitality to animate a Rune of Death.]

### On Her Nature

> "I am a Deathbed Companion. I lay with the remains of the exalted. Taking their vitality into myself. Their dying warmth."

> "This is my purpose. To give life to Those Who Live in Death. To restore the natural cycle. For death is not a thing to be feared. It is a thing to be shared."

> "The Golden Order decrees that death is not to be. That Those Who Live in Death are abominations. But I would give them their rightful place."

[Context: Fia reveals her purpose — she collects the vitality of champions to eventually birth a new Rune of Death. She opposes the Golden Order's prohibition on death.]

---

## Roundtable Hold — Continued Conversations

### On the Champions

> "Champion after champion has lain with me, and shared their dying warmth. But none has proven suitable. None has been strong enough to father a proper Death."

> "Please, Tarnished. Continue to gain strength. And continue to share your warmth with me."

### On D, Hunter of the Dead

> "That man... D. He hunts Those Who Live in Death. He calls them wretched, abominations. But they are not abominations. They are merely incomplete. They need only a proper Death."

> "The Golden Order's hunters would deny them even that."

[Context: Fia and D are ideological enemies. D hunts Those Who Live in Death as part of the Golden Order. Fia seeks to give them Death — a completion of their existence.]

### After Several Embraces

> "You have been most generous. Your warmth is... exceptional. I feel it. The stirrings of something great."

> "In time, I will birth a great rune. The Mending Rune of the Death-Prince. A rune to mend the Elden Ring. To restore Death to the world."

### On the Weathered Dagger

> "I have a favor to ask. Would you deliver this dagger to its rightful owner? It belongs to a man at this hold. He will know it when he sees it."

[Context: Fia gives the player the Weathered Dagger to return to D. This sets up D's murder — when D confronts Fia, she kills him with this very dagger, using the vitality she has gathered.]

---

## Roundtable Hold — After D's Death

> (Fia is found standing over D's body in the room beyond the bedchamber.)

> "I have taken the life of D. With the warmth you and the other champions gave me. Forgive me, if you can."

> "But this was necessary. For the sake of Those Who Live in Death. For the sake of the Death-Prince."

> "I go now. To the Deeproot Depths. To find Godwyn. The Prince of Death."

[Context: Fia murders D and flees the Roundtable Hold. She descends to the Deeproot Depths to find Godwyn's body — the first demigod to die during the Night of the Black Knives. His soul was killed, but his body persists, corrupted, at the roots of the Erdtree.]

---

## Deeproot Depths — The Prince of Death's Throne

### First Encounter

> "You found me. I knew you would."

> "This is the Deeproot Depths. The roots of the Erdtree, entwined with the body of Godwyn. The first of the demigods to perish."

> "Godwyn the Golden. His body was slain, but his soul remained. No... rather, his soul was slain, but his body remained. A half-death. An abomination of the Golden Order."

> "But not to me. To me, he is the Death-Prince. And I will give him a proper death."

### On the Rune of Death

> "The Rune of Death was plucked from the Elden Ring by Maliketh, on Queen Marika's orders. Death was sealed away. And so the demigods became truly immortal."

> "But on the Night of the Black Knives, a fragment of the Rune of Death was stolen. Used to slay Godwyn. But only half of him."

> "His body lives on, growing, fusing with the Erdtree's roots. Spreading Deathroot. Birthing Those Who Live in Death. All because he was denied a complete Death."

[Context: This is critical lore exposition. The removal of the Rune of Death from the Elden Ring by Maliketh made true death impossible. The Black Knives could only achieve a half-death. Godwyn's soulless body persists and corrupts the Erdtree from within.]

### On Her Goal

> "I will lay with Godwyn. Take his warmth — what remains of it — into myself. And birth the Mending Rune of the Death-Prince."

> "A rune to restore Death to the Elden Ring. To give Those Who Live in Death a proper end. And a proper beginning."

> "Will you help me? I need but one more thing. I need you to enter the Deathbed Dream. And there, retrieve the hallowbrand of the cursemark."

---

## Deeproot Depths — Entering the Deathbed Dream

> "Lay with me. As a Deathbed Companion, I ask this of you."

> "Within the dream, you will face Fortissax. The great dragon, Lichdragon Fortissax. He was Godwyn's companion, once. But now he is lost in the death dream. Corrupted by it."

> "Defeat him, and the cursemark shall be yours."

[Context: The player lies with Fia to enter the Deathbed Dream, a boss arena where they fight Lichdragon Fortissax — an ancient dragon who loved Godwyn and entered his death dream to try to save him, becoming corrupted in the process.]

---

## After Defeating Lichdragon Fortissax

> "It is done. The cursemark... You have it."

> "Now, I can complete my purpose. I will birth the Mending Rune of the Death-Prince. And give the Death-Prince his proper death."

> "Thank you. You have given me... and all Those Who Live in Death... a great gift."

> "I am weary now. Let me rest. Beside Godwyn."

[Context: Fia falls into a deep sleep, lying beside Godwyn's body. The Mending Rune of the Death-Prince appears. This rune can be used in the Elden Lord ending to create the Age of the Duskborn — a world where death is restored to the natural order.]

---

## Fia — If D's Brother Appears

> (D's twin brother, who was found sleeping in Nokron, may appear at the Deeproot Depths after Fia falls asleep. He kills Fia to avenge his brother. She does not resist.)

> (D's Brother, standing over Fia's body:) "For my brother... D. He who would extinguish Those Who Live in Death. I have avenged him."

[Context: D's twin brother, also named D, dons his brother's armor and hunts Fia to the Deeproot Depths. He murders her in her sleep. The Mending Rune remains obtainable regardless.]

## Notes on Fia's Lore Significance

- Fia is a Deathbed Companion — a role that existed in the Golden Order to offer comfort to dying champions. She has perverted (or restored) this role to serve Those Who Live in Death.
- The vitality she collects through embraces is literally the life force of champions. She uses this to fuel the creation of a Rune of Death.
- Her murder of D is a necessary step: she needs the accumulated vitality AND the dagger (which may be a Black Knife or similar Destined Death weapon).
- Godwyn the Golden was the first demigod to die. His soul was killed by the Black Knives (using stolen Destined Death), but his body survived. This "half-death" created the deathroot corruption and Those Who Live in Death.
- Lichdragon Fortissax was a dragon who loved Godwyn. When Godwyn was soul-killed, Fortissax entered his death dream to fight the corruption — and lost, becoming a lich dragon.
- The Mending Rune of the Death-Prince restores the concept of Death to the Elden Ring. The Age of the Duskborn is arguably the most "natural" ending — life and death in balance, as they should be.
- Fia's questline intersects with D, Rogier (who investigates Godwyn's corpse beneath Stormveil), and the broader Black Knife conspiracy.
