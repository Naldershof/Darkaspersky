# Rya / Zoraya — Complete Dialogue

## Liurnia — Scenic Isle Area (First Meeting)

> "Oh! Hello! I am Rya. A scout for Volcano Manor."

> "Lady Tanith has asked me to extend an invitation. To the Manor, on Mt. Gelmir."

> "Volcano Manor is a home for those who oppose the Erdtree. Recusants, we are called."

> "If you would come, I can show you the way. But first... I have a small favor to ask."

### The Necklace

> "A man stole my necklace. A precious keepsake. He fled to the Boilprawn Shack."

> "Could you retrieve it for me? I would be... most grateful."

[Context: Rya's necklace was taken by Blackguard Big Boggart at the Boilprawn Shack. The player can buy it back or take it by force. Returning the necklace earns Rya's trust and the invitation to Volcano Manor.]

### After Returning the Necklace

> "You found it! Oh, thank you! You are most kind."

> "As promised, I will guide you to Volcano Manor. Here — take this."

> "Use it when you're ready, and I will bring you to the Manor."

## Volcano Manor — As a Resident

> "Welcome to Volcano Manor! I'm so glad you came."

> "Lady Tanith is our leader. She serves Lord Rykard, the lord of this manor."

> "I serve as a scout. Finding Tarnished who might be sympathetic to our cause."

> "Please, make yourself at home. We're all family here."

### On the Manor's Residents

> "The recusants here... they've all chosen to defy the Erdtree. Each for their own reasons."

> "Some seek power. Some seek revenge. Some simply want a place to belong."

> "Lady Tanith welcomes them all. As she welcomed me."

### On Tanith

> "Lady Tanith is... wonderful. She took me in when no one else would."

> "She is like a mother to me. The only mother I've ever known."

## Rya's Discovery — Her True Nature

### Finding the Serpent's Amnion

> "I found something. In the manor. A room I wasn't meant to see."

> "There were... things inside. Horrible things. And a serpent's amnion."

> "An amnion... a birthing sac. For a serpent."

> "I think... I think it was mine."

### Confronting the Truth

> "Lady Tanith... she told me the truth. When I pressed her."

> "I am not human. I was born from a serpent. A man-serpent of Volcano Manor."

> "My human form... it is a disguise. A skin I wear. Beneath it, I am a serpent."

> "Tanith raised me. Loved me. Despite what I am."

> "But I... I am a monster. How can I live, knowing this?"

[Context: Rya discovers that she is a Man-Serpent — one of the serpentine creatures found throughout Volcano Manor. Her human form is a facade. This revelation devastates her.]

### Her Despair

> "I am a snake. Wearing the skin of a woman."

> "Everything I believed about myself... a lie."

> "The other Man-Serpents in the manor... they are mindless. Violent. Is that what I truly am?"

> "Perhaps it would be better if I simply... ceased to exist."

> "Please. I am asking you. End me. Kill me, and end this farce."

## The Player's Choices

### If the Player Gives Rya the Tonic of Forgetfulness (From Tanith)

> "A tonic? From Lady Tanith?"

> "She... she wants me to forget? To go back to how I was?"

> "Perhaps that is a kindness. To forget the truth. To live in ignorance."

> "I... I will drink it. Thank you."

> (Rya drinks the tonic and her memories of the discovery are erased. She returns to her cheerful demeanor.)

> "Hello! Welcome to Volcano Manor! I'm Rya!"

[Context: Tanith provides the Tonic of Forgetfulness, which erases Rya's memory of her discovery. This is a mercy — but also a form of control. Rya returns to ignorant happiness.]

### If the Player Refuses to Kill Rya and Does Not Give the Tonic

> "You... you won't kill me? Even knowing what I am?"

> "I am a serpent. A monster."

> "...But you see me as more than that?"

> "Perhaps... perhaps I am more than my blood. More than the skin I wear."

> "I need time. To think. To decide who I want to be."

### After Time Passes (If Spared Without Tonic)

> "I have thought about it. For a long time."

> "I am Rya. Or Zoraya. Or whatever name you choose."

> "I am a serpent. But I am also a person. And I choose to be a person."

> "I am leaving the manor. I cannot stay here, surrounded by reminders of what I am."

> "But I will not hide from it either. I will find my own path."

> "Thank you. For seeing me. For believing I could be more."

> "Take this. A keepsake. The Daedicar's Woe talisman. It was with me when I was born."

> "Goodbye, friend."

[Context: If spared and not made to forget, Rya accepts her dual nature and leaves to find her own purpose. This is arguably the best outcome — she retains her agency and self-knowledge.]

### If the Player Kills Rya

> "Thank you... I knew you were kind..."

> "Goodbye... Mother..."

[Context: If the player kills Rya at her request, she dies peacefully. She calls Tanith "Mother" in her final breath. Tanith does not react well if she learns of this.]

## Notes on Rya/Zoraya's Lore Significance

- Rya's true name is Zoraya. "Rya" is the name she uses in her human form.
- Man-Serpents are created through the influence of the God-Devouring Serpent. They are humans who have been partially transformed by the serpent's power.
- Rya is unique among Man-Serpents: she has a complete human consciousness and personality. This may be because Tanith raised her with love and care.
- The Tonic of Forgetfulness parallels the Golden Order's approach to uncomfortable truths: erase them. Tanith's love for Rya is genuine, but her solution is to deny Rya the truth.
- Rya's quest is about identity: can you be more than what you were born as? Can a serpent be a person? The game allows the player to affirm or deny this.
- Her departure from Volcano Manor (if spared with knowledge intact) is one of the few genuinely hopeful outcomes in the game.
- The Daedicar's Woe talisman she gives the player increases damage taken — a reflection of her self-perception as cursed.
- Rya's human form being a "disguise" mirrors the broader theme of hidden identities in Elden Ring: Radagon is Marika, Ranni inhabits a doll, Margit is Morgott.
