# Godskin & Black Flame — Item Descriptions

> All items related to the Godskin Apostles, the Gloam-Eyed Queen, and the black flame tradition. These are the primary evidence for reconstructing the Gloam-Eyed Queen's identity and mission.

---

## Godslayer's Greatsword

> "The black flames wielded by the apostles are the remnant fires of the Gloam-Eyed Queen, sole survivor of the godskin cult. The colossal sword of the Gloam-Eyed Queen, leader of the Godskin Apostles. The Gloam-Eyed Queen once led the apostles to challenge the Greater Will."

[Source: Godslayer's Greatsword, Colossal Sword]
[Context: Found in the Divine Tower of Caelid basement, behind a Godskin Apostle. The sword is described as belonging to the Gloam-Eyed Queen herself.]
[Key detail: "sole survivor" — she survived the destruction of her own cult. This implies Maliketh destroyed the Godskin order but spared (or failed to kill) the Queen.]

## Scouring Black Flame

[Source: Scouring Black Flame, Incantation]

## Godskin Encounter Locations — Mapped Evidence

The Godskins appear at specific, lore-significant locations:

1. **Divine Tower of Caelid** — Guarding the Godslayer's Greatsword (the Queen's weapon)
2. **Windmill Village** — Where women dance and flay in ecstatic ritual (a surviving Godskin practice?)
3. **Volcano Manor / Mt. Gelmir** — Near Rykard's anti-Erdtree rebellion
4. **Divine Tower of Liurnia** — Near the tower where Ranni's Great Rune was discarded
5. **Spiritcaller Cave** — Underground, near the Eternal Cities
6. **Crumbling Farum Azula** — Near Maliketh (their nemesis), guarding the approach to Destined Death

[Context: The Godskins' placement tells a story: they cluster around Divine Towers (where Great Runes are activated), near anti-Golden Order sites, and near Maliketh. They are still, even after their queen's defeat, positioned to threaten the divine order.]

## Beast Claw (Incantation)

> "Incantation of the beast clergy. The beast clergy worship the Crucible, the primordial form of the Erdtree. Gurranq, the Beast Clergyman, is their chief — and his hunger for Deathroot betrays a connection to death that runs deeper than piety."

[Source: Beast Claw, Incantation]
[Context: Gurranq IS Maliketh. He hungers for Deathroot because he was the one who sealed Destined Death — and the sealing left him bound to it. The beast clergy's Crucible worship connects the pre-Erdtree age to the Gloam-Eyed Queen's power.]
