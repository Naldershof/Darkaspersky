# Demigod & Story Weapons — Item Descriptions

## Grafted Dragon

> "Dragon head encountered at the foot of the Erdtree. Formed by grafting many fingers and toes to the hand that grips it. Grafting is more than mere imitation of the Crucible. It is an attempt to embody its primordial form."

[Source: Grafted Dragon, Fist weapon]
[Context: Remembrance of the Grafted (Godrick)]

---

## Axe of Godrick

> "Axe used by Godrick the Grafted. Once a golden halberd bestowed upon heroes of the Erdtree, this weapon was later grafted with a dragon head, and an arm to wield it."

[Source: Axe of Godrick, Greataxe]
[Context: Remembrance of the Grafted]

---

## Carian Regal Scepter

> "Scepter of the Carian queen. A masterwork of the Lazuli sorcerers, who served the royal Carian family. The Carian royals were most devoted to the art of the Full Moon."

[Source: Carian Regal Scepter, Glintstone Staff]
[Context: Remembrance of the Full Moon Queen]

---

## Starscourge Greatsword

> "Starscourge Radahn's greatswords, befitting his enormous stature. These colossal swords, along with the gravitational technique used to wield them, were a gift from an Alabaster Lord he met in his youth."

[Source: Starscourge Greatsword, Colossal Sword]
[Context: Remembrance of the Starscourge]

---

## Lion Greatbow

> "Greatbow used by General Radahn. This bow was crafted using the technique of a race of giants. Its gravity-imbued arrows fly in a long arc, much like falling stars."

[Source: Lion Greatbow, Greatbow]
[Context: Remembrance of the Starscourge]

---

## Blasphemous Blade

> "Sacred sword of Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy. It was once the sword of a hero, but was corrupted when fed to the serpent along with its owner. It is said the Lord of Blasphemy still lurks within."

[Source: Blasphemous Blade, Greatsword]
[Context: Remembrance of the Blasphemous]

---

## Morgott's Cursed Sword

> "Curved sword held by Morgott, the Omen King. An accursed bloody blade. All the Omen are born of the crucible's blessing — that primordial matter from which all life was once blended together."

[Source: Morgott's Cursed Sword, Curved Greatsword]
[Context: Remembrance of the Omen King]

---

## Mohgwyn's Sacred Spear

> "Trident of Mohg, Lord of Blood. A sacred spear that creates bloodflame. As the Lord of Blood, Mohg wished to establish a new dynasty with the Empyrean Miquella as its monarch."

[Source: Mohgwyn's Sacred Spear, Great Spear]
[Context: Remembrance of the Blood Lord]

---

## Hand of Malenia

> "Blade of Malenia, Goddess of Rot. She waited at the foot of the Haligtree, blade in hand, until the day Miquella returned."

[Source: Hand of Malenia, Katana]
[Context: Remembrance of the Rot Goddess]

---

## Sacred Relic Sword

> "Sword wrought from the remains of a god who should have been left alone. Hewn from the very Elden Ring, this sword is the relic of a god — and the god's husk."

[Source: Sacred Relic Sword, Greatsword]
[Context: Elden Remembrance]

---

## Maliketh's Black Blade

> "The black blade wielded by Maliketh, Marika's loyal shadow-bound beast. Maliketh was entrusted with the fragment of the Rune of Death by his Empyrean, and upon it is carved the Destined Death of the demigods."

[Source: Maliketh's Black Blade, Colossal Sword]
[Context: Remembrance of the Black Blade]

---

## Bolt of Gransax

> "Spear wrought in the image of the bolt of lightning hurled by the ancient dragon Gransax at Leyndell. A legendary armament of the capital. The dead dragon, whose corpse drapes the city walls, once assaulted the capital in a frenzy."

[Source: Bolt of Gransax, Spear]
[Context: Found in Leyndell on a balcony near the dead dragon]

---

## Coded Sword

> "A sword with a cypher-inscribed blade made of light. This weapon, bestowed by the Two Fingers, is one of the Golden Order's armaments. The blade is composed of runes of the Golden Order. The glow of its blade is something only the righteous will see."

[Source: Coded Sword, Straight Sword]
[Context: Found in Roundtable Hold]

---

## Dark Moon Greatsword

> "Greatsword of Ranni the Witch. A Moon Greatsword, bestowed by a Carian queen upon her spouse at the ceremony of the pledge. A frigid greatsword wrought of the Dark Moon itself."

[Source: Dark Moon Greatsword, Greatsword]
[Context: Given at the end of Ranni's questline]

---

## Rivers of Blood

> "Weapon of Okina, swordsman from the Land of Reeds. This is a cursed weapon that has felled countless men. When Mohg, the Lord of Blood, first felt his blood boil, he had visions of a coming dynasty of blood."

[Source: Rivers of Blood, Katana]
[Context: Dropped by Bloody Finger Okina at Church of Repose]

---

## Godslayer's Greatsword

> "The black flames wielded by the apostles are the remnant fires of the Gloam-Eyed Queen, sole survivor of the godskin cult. The colossal sword of the Gloam-Eyed Queen, leader of the Godskin Apostles. The Gloam-Eyed Queen once led the apostles to challenge the Greater Will."

[Source: Godslayer's Greatsword, Colossal Sword]
[Context: Found in Divine Tower of Caelid]

---

## Black Knife

> "Dagger of the Black Knife Assassins, who on the Night of the Black Knives, stole a fragment of the Rune of Death. The assassins' blades were imbued with the power of the stolen rune, enchanted with Destined Death."

[Source: Black Knife, Dagger]
[Context: Dropped by Black Knife Assassin at Sainted Hero's Grave]

---

## Sword of Night and Flame

> "Legendary sword once wielded by a swordsman who served two masters. One, the Carian queen of the stars, and the other, the fire monks who worshipped the flame of the Fell God."

[Source: Sword of Night and Flame, Straight Sword]
[Context: Found in Carian Manor]

---

## Ruins Greatsword

> "Greatsword made from the rubble of a ruin that fell from the sky. One end is fashioned as a blade to hew, while the other is blunt to bludgeon. Gravity magic is said to be an ancient creation of the Alabaster Lords."

[Source: Ruins Greatsword, Colossal Sword]
[Context: Dropped by Misbegotten Warrior and Crucible Knight in Redmane Castle]

---

## Winged Scythe

> "Blade of gold once wielded by an angel of the Lands Between. The Winged Scythe inflicts holy damage and prevents the use of Flask of Crimson Tears for a time."

[Source: Winged Scythe, Reaper]
[Context: Found in Weeping Peninsula]

---

## Eclipse Shotel

> "Shotel forged by the sorcerers of Castle Sol. Imbued with the power of the eclipse, which was said to be an ancient rite practiced by those who wished to contact the dead."

[Source: Eclipse Shotel, Curved Sword]
[Context: Found in Castle Sol]
