# Lore-Significant Ashes of War

## Hoarah Loux's Earthshaker

> "Skill of Hoarah Loux, warrior and first Elden Lord. Godfrey suppressed his warrior's instinct to become a lord. When at last he cast aside his lordly guise, the ferocity of Hoarah Loux erupted."

[Source: Hoarah Loux's Earthshaker, Ash of War]
[Context: Remembrance of Hoarah Loux]

---

## Carian Grandeur

> "Skill of the Carian royal family. Performs a sweeping strike with a conjured greatsword. The Carian royals developed sorceries that manifested as spectral weapons — swords, greatshields, and phalanx blades."

[Source: Carian Grandeur, Ash of War]
[Context: Found in Carian Manor]

---

## Black Flame Tornado

> "Skill of the Godskin Apostles. Creates a raging tornado of black flame. The Godskin Apostles wielded the black flame given to them by the Gloam-Eyed Queen in their crusade against the Greater Will."

[Source: Black Flame Tornado, Ash of War]
[Context: Remembrance of the Godskin Apostle]

---

## Lifesteal Fist

> "Skill granted by the Roundtable Knight Ensha. Grabs a foe and drains their life force. Ensha served the Roundtable Hold but harbored dark ambitions."

[Source: Lifesteal Fist, Ash of War]
[Context: Dropped by Ensha after he attacks the player]

---

## Flames of the Redmanes

> "Skill of the Redmane Knights who served General Radahn. Unleashes a burst of flame. The Redmane Knights were devoted to their general, and many fought alongside him until the rot claimed their minds."

[Source: Flames of the Redmanes, Ash of War]
[Context: Found at Fort Gael in Caelid]

---

## Sacred Blade

> "Skill of the Erdtree's sacred knights. Sheathes blade in holy light. The sacred knights were the most devoted of the Erdtree's protectors, sworn to defend the Golden Order."

[Source: Sacred Blade, Ash of War]
[Context: Found at the Third Church of Marika]

---

## Prayerful Strike

> "Skill of the Erdtree Avatars. A solemn strike that heals the wielder. The Erdtree Avatars are guardians born of the Minor Erdtrees, manifestations of the tree's will to protect itself."

[Source: Prayerful Strike, Ash of War]
[Context: Dropped by Erdtree Avatar enemies]

---

## Stamp (Upward Cut)

> "Skill of the champion warriors. Stamps the ground and follows with an upward cut. Godfrey's warriors were known for their raw physical prowess."

[Source: Stamp, Ash of War]
[Context: Various locations]

---

## Glintstone Pebble (Ash)

> "Skill used by Raya Lucaria sorcerer soldiers. Fires a glintstone projectile before closing with a thrust. The rank-and-file soldiers of the Academy used this technique in its defense."

[Source: Glintstone Pebble, Ash of War]
[Context: Dropped by Academy soldiers]
