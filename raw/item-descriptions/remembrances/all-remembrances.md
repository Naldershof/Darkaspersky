# All Boss Remembrances — Elden Ring

Remembrances are key items obtained by defeating major bosses (demigods and other legendary foes). They can be exchanged at the Roundtable Hold for unique weapons, spells, or incantations, or consumed for runes. Their descriptions contain critical lore about each boss.

---

## Remembrance of the Grafted

> "Remembrance of Godrick the Grafted, hewn into the Erdtree.
> The power of its namesake can be unlocked by the Finger Reader.
> Alternatively, it can be used to gain a great bounty of runes.
>
> Godrick was the last of the golden lineage, and the weakest of the demigod children.
> When the gods fled the Lands Between during the Shattering, he alone remained,
> grafting the limbs of his fallen soldiers to his own body."

[Source: Remembrance of the Grafted, Key Item — Remembrance]
[Context: Obtained by defeating Godrick the Grafted in Stormveil Castle]
