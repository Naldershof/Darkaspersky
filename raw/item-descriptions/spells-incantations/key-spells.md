# Lore-Significant Spells & Incantations

## Scarlet Aeonia

> "The most potent manifestation of the Scarlet Rot. When Malenia unleashed the power of the Scarlet Rot, she rotted from within and bloomed into the Goddess of Rot. The flower of the rot goddess bloomed three times. With each bloom, the land around her was devastated."

[Source: Scarlet Aeonia, Incantation]
[Context: Remembrance of the Rot Goddess]

---

## Elden Stars

> "Incantation that creates a stream of gold shooting stars. This incantation is a vestige of the Elden Beast, the vassal of the Greater Will that descended upon the Lands Between, carrying with it the Elden Ring."

[Source: Elden Stars, Incantation]
[Context: Found in Deeproot Depths]

---

## Flame of Frenzy

> "The flame of frenzy incantation of the Three Fingers. Emits a burst of the yellow flame of frenzy from the eyes. This incantation was discovered at the base of the Erdtree, where the Three Fingers dwell."

[Source: Flame of Frenzy, Incantation]
[Context: Found in Callu Baptismal Church]

---

## Howl of Shabriri

> "An incantation of the Three Fingers. Shabriri, most reviled of all, is said to have been the first to be afflicted with the madness of the flame of frenzy. He now wanders, seeking to spread the flame."

[Source: Howl of Shabriri, Incantation]
[Context: Found in Subterranean Shunning-Grounds]

---

## Unendurable Frenzy

> "Superior incantation of the Three Fingers. When the land was afflicted by the madness of the flame of frenzy, there were those who surrendered to it willingly. They became vessels of the flame."

[Source: Unendurable Frenzy, Incantation]
[Context: Found in Subterranean Shunning-Grounds]

---

## Ranni's Dark Moon

> "Legendary sorcery of Ranni the Witch. Uses the caster as a vessel to incarnate a cold, dark moon. The moon was encountered by a young Ranni, who was taught by the snowy crone the cold sorceries of the dark side of the moon."

[Source: Ranni's Dark Moon, Sorcery]
[Context: Found at the end of Chelona's Rise puzzle]

---

## Rennala's Full Moon

> "Legendary sorcery used by the Full Moon Queen. Incarnates a full moon and fires it at foes. Queen Rennala encountered the full moon in person, and from that moment, her fate was forever altered."

[Source: Rennala's Full Moon, Sorcery]
[Context: Remembrance of the Full Moon Queen]

---

## Comet Azur

> "Legendary sorcery devised by the primeval sorcerer Azur. Fires a tremendous comet. Azur was one of the founding sorcerers who studied the primeval current — the stars themselves. He gave his entire being to the stars, and in return, they gave him this."

[Source: Comet Azur, Sorcery]
[Context: Given by Primeval Sorcerer Azur at Mt. Gelmir]

---

## Stars of Ruin

> "Legendary sorcery of the primeval sorcerer Lusat. Fires a volley of twelve dark shooting stars. Lusat, who peered into the primeval current, gained power beyond his control. In the end, he was consumed by the very stars he revered."

[Source: Stars of Ruin, Sorcery]
[Context: Given by Master Lusat in Sellia Hideaway]

---

## Destined Death (Black Blade)

> "Superior incantation channeling the power of Maliketh. Empowers the blade with the power of Destined Death. The blade of death was sealed away by Maliketh, but a fragment was stolen on the night of the dire plot."

[Source: Black Blade, Incantation]
[Context: Remembrance of the Black Blade]

---

## Law of Regression

> "A Golden Order incantation. All things in the world yearn to converge. The principle of regression, as elucidated by the Golden Order, is the pull of meaning. The tendency for all things to want to return to their original form."

[Source: Law of Regression, Incantation]
[Context: Given by Goldmask after demonstrating it on the Radagon statue]

---

## Law of Causality

> "A Golden Order incantation. All things in the world are connected by cause and effect. The principle of causality holds that every phenomenon is connected to something prior."

[Source: Law of Causality, Incantation]
[Context: Found on the Altus Plateau]

---

## Radagon's Rings of Light

> "One of the incantations of the Golden Order, casting golden rings of light. Radagon's dedication to the Golden Order was absolute. His body was golden, and radiated the Order's light."

[Source: Radagon's Rings of Light, Incantation]
[Context: Found in a chest at the Altus Plateau]

---

## Litany of Proper Death

> "Incantation taught by D, Hunter of the Dead. Those Who Live in Death are abominations that should not exist. This incantation casts a purifying light that destroys them."

[Source: Litany of Proper Death, Incantation]
[Context: Given by D, Hunter of the Dead]

---

## Founding Rain of Stars

> "Legendary sorcery cast by the founding members of the academy. Calls down a rain of star showers. The Academy of Raya Lucaria was founded by sorcerers who studied the stars, before the Erdtree's dominion."

[Source: Founding Rain of Stars, Sorcery]
[Context: Found in Heretical Rise]

---

## Ancient Death Rancor

> "Sorcery of the servants of Death. Summons the vengeful spirits of the dead. Those afflicted by Deathroot transform into the living dead, servants of the Prince of Death."

[Source: Ancient Death Rancor, Sorcery]
[Context: Found in Deeproot Depths near Godwyn]

---

## Flame, Grant Me Strength

> "Incantation of the Fire Monks. Raises physical and fire-affinity attack power. The fire monks guard the flame of the Fell God, the ancient fire that once burned atop the Mountaintops of the Giants."

[Source: Flame, Grant Me Strength, Incantation]
[Context: Found behind Fort Gael in Caelid]

---

## Flame of the Fell God

> "The incantation of the one-eyed Fire Giant. Creates a massive ball of fire. The Fire Giants borrowed the flames of the Fell God to burn the Erdtree, an act that earned them the wrath of the Golden Order."

[Source: Flame of the Fell God, Incantation]
[Context: Dropped by Adan in Malefactor's Evergaol]

---

## Dragon Communion Incantations (Dragonfire, Dragonclaw, etc.)

> "Incantation of the Dragon Communion. The Dragon Communion is an ancient practice of consuming dragon hearts to gain their power. This art was practiced in the Cathedral of Dragon Communion on the Caelid coast."

[Source: Various Dragon Communion incantations]
[Context: Purchased at Cathedral/Church of Dragon Communion]

---

## Placidusax's Ruin

> "Incantation of the Dragonlord Placidusax. The Dragonlord Placidusax once served as Elden Lord, before the Erdtree's age. In that lost era, his god had fled. The Dragonlord has waited, for an eternity."

[Source: Placidusax's Ruin, Incantation]
[Context: Remembrance of the Dragonlord]
