# Lore-Significant Talismans — Item Descriptions

## Radagon's Scarseal

> "A talisman with the seal of Radagon, found in a stonecoffin within the Raya Lucaria Academy. The seal is of the Golden Order and was once given to those who devoted themselves to the study of its incantations."

[Source: Radagon's Scarseal, Talisman]
[Context: Found in Raya Lucaria]

---

## Radagon's Soreseal

> "A talisman with the seal of Radagon. Greatly raises attributes at the cost of taking more damage. The seal is the mark of the Golden Order, an oath to uphold its teachings."

[Source: Radagon's Soreseal, Talisman]
[Context: Found in Fort Faroth, Caelid]

---

## Marika's Scarseal

> "A talisman bearing the seal of Marika. Raises mind, intelligence, faith, and arcane at the cost of increasing damage taken. Queen Marika's seal — a reminder of her grace and her purpose."

[Source: Marika's Scarseal, Talisman]
[Context: Found in Siofra River]

---

## Marika's Soreseal

> "A talisman bearing the seal of Marika. Greatly raises mind, intelligence, faith, and arcane at the cost of greatly increasing damage taken. In Marika's own words: 'O Radagon, leal hound of the Golden Order. Thou'rt yet to become me. Thou'rt yet to become a god.'"

[Source: Marika's Soreseal, Talisman]
[Context: Found in Elphael, Brace of the Haligtree]

---

## Erdtree's Favor

> "A talisman that increases maximum HP, stamina, and equip load. A vestige of the Erdtree's blessing. The Erdtree once graced all who lived in the Lands Between."

[Source: Erdtree's Favor, Talisman]
[Context: Various locations, +1 and +2 versions in later areas]

---

## Two Fingers Heirloom

> "A talisman depicting the Two Fingers and the Three Fingers locked in conflict. An ancient relic that shows that the Two and Three Fingers were once one entity."

[Source: Two Fingers Heirloom, Talisman]
[Context: Found in Purified Ruins, Liurnia]

---

## Crucible Knot Talisman

> "Stone talisman depicting the crucible of the Erdtree. Reduces the holy damage component of critical hits. In the beginning, everything was in a state of perfect wholeness. An age of abundance, where everything existed in an unalloyed state."

[Source: Crucible Knot Talisman]
[Context: Found on Crucible Knight in Auriza Hero's Grave]

---

## Crucible Feather Talisman

> "Stone talisman depicting feathered wings around the crucible. Enhances dodge rolling. An aspect of the primordial crucible, the wings represent a vestige of the ancient age of plenty."

[Source: Crucible Feather Talisman]
[Context: Dropped by Crucible Knight]

---

## Millicent's Prosthesis

> "A mechanical prosthesis designed for Millicent. The prosthesis allows her to fight despite losing her arm to the rot. It was crafted by the master engineer of the Haligtree."

[Source: Millicent's Prosthesis, Talisman]
[Context: Given by Millicent / found during her questline]

---

## Rotten Winged Sword Insignia

> "A talisman depicting a one-armed valkyrie. Greatly raises attack power with successive attacks. Millicent chose to fight the rot that consumed her. In doing so, she won a battle against the god within."

[Source: Rotten Winged Sword Insignia, Talisman]
[Context: Reward for helping Millicent at the Haligtree]

---

## Godfrey Icon

> "An icon of the legendary Godfrey, First Elden Lord. Enhances charged spells and skills. Godfrey was the first to be granted grace and the first to have it taken away."

[Source: Godfrey Icon, Talisman]
[Context: Dropped by Godefroy the Grafted in Golden Lineage Evergaol]

---

## Moon of Nokstella

> "A talisman representing the moon of Nokstella. Increases memory slots. The Eternal City of Nokstella lay in worship of a different moon — not the full moon of Raya Lucaria, but the dark moon of the Nox."

[Source: Moon of Nokstella, Talisman]
[Context: Found in Nokstella, Eternal City]

---

## Ancestral Spirit's Horn

> "Horn of the ancestor spirit. Restores FP upon defeating enemies. The ancestor spirits are the spiritual manifestations of a long-dead civilization's reverence for the natural world."

[Source: Ancestral Spirit's Horn, Talisman]
[Context: Dropped by Regal Ancestor Spirit in Nokron]

---

## Stargazer Heirloom

> "An heirloom of a stargazer from the Eternal Cities. Raises intelligence. The stargazers of the Eternal Cities mapped the movements of the stars, seeking to understand the will of the cosmos."

[Source: Stargazer Heirloom, Talisman]
[Context: Found at the Divine Tower of Liurnia]

---

## Blessed Dew Talisman

> "A talisman depicting a dewdrop blessed by the Erdtree. Slowly restores HP. When the Erdtree was in full bloom, its dew healed all manner of ailment. A remnant of those blessed days."

[Source: Blessed Dew Talisman]
[Context: Found in Leyndell]
