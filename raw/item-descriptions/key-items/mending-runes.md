# Mending Runes and Endings

Items and approaches that determine the nature of the new Order when the Elden Ring is mended. Each represents a different philosophy for the future of the Lands Between.

---

## Mending Rune of the Fell Curse

> "Mending rune acquired from the Dung Eater's corporeal body.
> Used to restore the Elden Ring when brandished by the Elden Lord.
> The Dung Eater was once a Tarnished, but was cursed by the fell omen. In the end, he chose to defile the very roots of the Erdtree itself, so that all might share in the curse.
> The blessing of the Erdtree is the Golden Order's greatest grace, and its defiling will mean an end to all things sacred."

[Source: Mending Rune of the Fell Curse, Key Item / Mending Rune]
[Context: Obtained by completing the Dung Eater's questline. The player must feed him multiple Seedbed Curses while he is imprisoned in the Roundtable Hold's underground gaol, or give his puppet form the curses]

---

## Mending Rune of the Death-Prince

> "Mending rune found within the lifeless demigod body in the Deeproot Depths.
> Used to restore the Elden Ring when brandished by the Elden Lord.
> Fia, the Deathbed Companion, sought to mend the Elden Ring by returning the principle of Destined Death into it.
> The Golden Order was founded upon the separation of life from death. The Death-Prince would undo that separation, allowing those who live in death to find a rightful place."

[Source: Mending Rune of the Death-Prince, Key Item / Mending Rune]
[Context: Obtained by completing Fia's questline. After defeating the Lichdragon Fortissax in Fia's dream, the mending rune is found on Fia's body in the Deeproot Depths]

---

## Mending Rune of Perfect Order

> "Mending rune discovered by the noble Goldmask.
> Used to restore the Elden Ring when brandished by the Elden Lord.
> What was shattered need not remain broken forever.
> The current imperfection of the Golden Order is the result of the fickleness of the gods, no better than the madness of men.
> The Mending Rune of Perfect Order is the answer to that madness - an Order immutable, perfected, and eternal."

[Source: Mending Rune of Perfect Order, Key Item / Mending Rune]
[Context: Obtained by completing Goldmask and Brother Corhyn's questline. Found on Goldmask's body on the Erdtree Capital ramparts after the city becomes the Ashen Capital]

## The Frenzied Flame Approach (Lord of Frenzied Flame Ending)

> *Note: This ending does not use a Mending Rune. Instead, the player inherits the Frenzied Flame from the Three Fingers.*

### Flame of Frenzy (Incantation)

> "Incantation of the Three Fingers' flame of frenzy.
> Produces a burst of the yellow flame of frenzy from the caster's eyes.
> The flame of frenzy deals damage that could not be healed.
> Those who are touched by the Three Fingers are forever changed.
> The Three Fingers seek an audience with the Tarnished. Should they find one worthy, the Lord of Frenzied Flame will be born."

[Source: Flame of Frenzy, Incantation]
[Context: The Three Fingers are found beneath the Subterranean Shunning-Grounds, below Leyndell. The player must remove all armor and open the door to be embraced]

### Shabriri's Woe (Talisman)

> "Talisman depicting the flame of frenzy.
> Constantly attracts enemies' aggression.
> Shabriri, the most reviled man in all history, was said to have had his eyes gouged out as punishment.
> Through his empty sockets burned a flame of frenzy, which spread across the land, setting all ablaze."

[Source: Shabriri's Woe, Talisman]
[Context: Found in the Frenzied Flame Village, Liurnia of the Lakes]

### Hyetta's Dialogue (Contextual)

> "I can see it now, in the light of the flame of frenzy.
> All that there is, came from the One Great.
> And to the One Great all shall return.
> The Greater Will made a mistake. Torment, despair, affliction...
> Every sin, every curse, every one, born of a mistake.
> And so, what was borrowed must be returned.
> Melt it all away, with the kindling of the Frenzied Flame.
> Every last order. Every last grace. Every trace of their existence."

[Source: Hyetta, NPC Dialogue]
[Context: Hyetta's final words after the player inherits the Frenzied Flame beneath the Subterranean Shunning-Grounds]

## Ranni's Dark Moon Approach (Age of Stars Ending)

> *Note: This ending does not use a Mending Rune. Instead, the player completes Ranni's questline and summons her at the conclusion.*

### Dark Moon Ring

> "Ring of the dark moon, given by Ranni the Witch.
> When the Tarnished first met Ranni, she introduced herself as Renna.
> This ring is a cold, dark blue, like the moon that guides the chill night.
> O Tarnished, I would have thee be my Lord Consort.
> Let us go, together, to the night sky."

[Source: Dark Moon Ring, Key Item]
[Context: Given to the player by the miniature Ranni doll during the Lake of Rot section of Ranni's questline. Used at the conclusion of the game to summon Ranni]

### Ranni's Dialogue at Ending

> "Mine will be an order not of gold, but the stars and moon of the chill night.
> I would keep them far from the earth beneath our feet.
> As I have learned from my mistakes, the fingers and the greater will are not to be trusted.
> I bid thee travel the path of the Lord.
> And I shall depart upon my own path - together in spirit, if not in flesh."

[Source: Ranni, NPC Dialogue]
[Context: Spoken by Ranni if summoned at the end of the game after completing her questline]

## Notes on Endings and Mending

### The Six Main Endings
1. **Elden Lord (default)** - Mend the Elden Ring as-is, becoming Elden Lord
2. **Age of Fracture** - Same as default (some consider this distinct if you have no mending rune)
3. **Blessing of Despair (Dung Eater)** - Mend with Fell Curse rune; curse spreads to all
4. **Age of Duskborn (Fia)** - Mend with Death-Prince rune; Destined Death returns to the Ring
5. **Age of Order (Goldmask)** - Mend with Perfect Order rune; remove the gods' fickleness
6. **Age of Stars (Ranni)** - Remove the influence of the Greater Will; Ranni becomes the new god of a moonlit order
7. **Lord of Frenzied Flame** - Burn everything; return all to the One Great

### Lore Significance
- The Mending Runes represent different critiques of the Golden Order:
  - **Fell Curse**: The omen curse should be shared by all, not just the reviled few
  - **Death-Prince**: Destined Death should be part of the natural order, not sealed away
  - **Perfect Order**: The gods themselves are flawed; remove their influence from the Order
  - **Frenzied Flame**: All existence is a mistake; return to primordial oneness
  - **Age of Stars**: The Greater Will and its Fingers should be kept far from the world
