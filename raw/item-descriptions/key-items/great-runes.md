# Great Runes — Elden Ring

Great Runes are the shards of the Elden Ring, held by the demigod shardbearers after the Shattering. Each Great Rune's power reflects the values and nature of the demigod who held it. They must be activated at a Divine Tower and then empowered using a Rune Arc.

---

## Godrick's Great Rune

> "A Great Rune of the shardbearer Godrick.
> Its strength was once that of the golden lineage,
> but Godrick the Grafted exerted his will upon it,
> bending its strength to bolster all attributes.
>
> A shard of the Elden Ring, this Great Rune is the weakest among them,
> and yet even so, its power is a boon from the Erdtree.
> Use a Rune Arc to increase the power of this Great Rune."

[Source: Godrick's Great Rune, Key Item — Great Rune]
[Context: Obtained from Godrick the Grafted. Activated at the Divine Tower of Limgrave. Raises all attributes when empowered.]

---

## Great Rune of the Unborn

> "Great Rune of the shardbearer Rennala.
> This Great Rune is the amber egg that was given to
> Rennala by her beloved Radagon, upon their union.
> Contained within the egg is the Great Rune of rebirth,
> an unborn and formless Great Rune.
>
> It holds the power of rebirth, and can be used at the
> Academy of Raya Lucaria, to be reborn anew."

[Source: Great Rune of the Unborn, Key Item — Great Rune]
[Context: Obtained after the Rennala encounter. Does not require activation at a Divine Tower. Allows stat reallocation (respec) at Rennala.]

---

## Radahn's Great Rune

> "A Great Rune of the shardbearer Radahn.
> Its strength was once that of the golden lineage,
> but Radahn the Starscourge imbued it with the force of the stars.
>
> Raises maximum HP, FP, and Stamina.
> Conqueror of the Stars, Radahn was the mightiest of the
> demigod children, and his Great Rune reflects his vast
> fortitude and ambition.
> Use a Rune Arc to increase the power of this Great Rune."

[Source: Radahn's Great Rune, Key Item — Great Rune]
[Context: Obtained from Starscourge Radahn. Activated at the Divine Tower of Caelid. Raises maximum HP, FP, and Stamina when empowered.]

---

## Morgott's Great Rune

> "A Great Rune of the shardbearer Morgott.
> Its strength was once that of the golden lineage,
> and though Morgott was an Omen, and therefore
> unblessed by the Erdtree, his devotion to the
> Erdtree was unwavering.
>
> This Great Rune greatly raises maximum HP.
> Morgott the Omen King was the last to stand in defense
> of the capital and the Erdtree.
> Use a Rune Arc to increase the power of this Great Rune."

[Source: Morgott's Great Rune, Key Item — Great Rune]
[Context: Obtained from Morgott, the Omen King. Activated at the Divine Tower of East Altus. Greatly raises maximum HP when empowered.]

---

## Rykard's Great Rune

> "A Great Rune of the shardbearer Rykard.
> Its strength was once that of the golden lineage,
> but Rykard the Blasphemous fed it to the serpent,
> granting the power to restore HP upon defeating enemies.
>
> The serpent devours all, and so too does this rune
> offer sustenance from the death of foes.
> Use a Rune Arc to increase the power of this Great Rune."

[Source: Rykard's Great Rune, Key Item — Great Rune]
[Context: Obtained from Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy. Activated at the Divine Tower of West Altus. Restores HP upon defeating enemies when empowered.]

---

## Mohg's Great Rune

> "A Great Rune of the shardbearer Mohg.
> Its power was once that of the golden lineage,
> but Lord Mohg imbued it with his own Accursed Blood,
> granting the ability to bestow a blessing of blood
> upon summoned spirits.
>
> When summoned spirits inflict Hemorrhage on foes,
> the summoner's HP is also restored.
> An Omen, Mohg sought power from the Formless Mother of blood
> and rot, hoping to become the lord of a new dynasty.
> Use a Rune Arc to increase the power of this Great Rune."

[Source: Mohg's Great Rune, Key Item — Great Rune]
[Context: Obtained from Mohg, Lord of Blood. Activated at the Divine Tower of East Altus (accessible via a special coffin). Grants a Blessing of Blood to summoned spirits when empowered.]

---

## Malenia's Great Rune

> "A Great Rune of the shardbearer Malenia.
> The blessing of this Great Rune reduces the healing power of
> the Flask of Crimson Tears. And yet, attacks made immediately
> after taking damage will partially recover HP.
>
> Malenia was born with the scarlet rot eating away at her from
> within. She endured, undefeated, refusing all aid, relying only
> on her own strength. Her Great Rune reflects that tenacity,
> demanding that the bearer press forward through pain."

[Source: Malenia's Great Rune, Key Item — Great Rune]
[Context: Obtained from Malenia, Blade of Miquella. Activated at the isolated Divine Tower at the base of the Haligtree. HP recovery upon striking enemies shortly after taking damage, but reduces flask healing when empowered.]
