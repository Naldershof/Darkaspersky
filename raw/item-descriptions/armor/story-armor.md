# Story Armor Sets — Item Descriptions

## Radahn's Lion Armor

> "Armor worn by General Radahn. The lion is the symbol of Radahn, the mightiest hero of the demigods. In his youth, Radahn looked up to the hero Godfrey. He wished to be a great warrior like him."

[Source: Radahn's Lion Armor, Chest Armor]
[Context: Dropped/purchased after defeating Radahn]

---

## Malenia's Armor (Cleanrot Armor)

> "Armor donned by Malenia, Blade of Miquella. A suit of plate armor intricately crafted with a lotus motif. The Cleanrot Knights were Malenia's devoted knights, who swore to fight alongside her despite the scarlet rot that had claimed their bodies."

[Source: Malenia's Set pieces]
[Context: Enia at Roundtable Hold after Malenia's defeat]

---

## Snow Witch Set (Ranni's)

> "Hat/Robe once worn by the snowy crone who taught the young Ranni. She was a witch, and taught Ranni sorcery. But more than that, she was the one who put Ranni on the path to the dark side of the moon."

[Source: Snow Witch Set]
[Context: Found in Renna's Rise]

---

## Black Knife Set

> "Armor worn by the Black Knife Assassins. The assassins that carried out the plot were all women, and Numen who had close ties with Marika herself. It is said their leader was none other than Alecto."

[Source: Black Knife Set]
[Context: Found in Ordina, Liturgical Town]

---

## Crucible Axe Set

> "Armor of the Crucible Knights, ancient heroes who served Godfrey, the first Elden Lord. The Crucible Knights were champions of the Erdtree in a bygone era, when its primordial form was still a common sight. Their armor is engraved with a pattern of the Crucible of life."

[Source: Crucible Axe Set]
[Context: Dropped by Crucible Knight in Stormhill Evergaol]

---

## Crucible Tree Set

> "Armor of a Crucible Knight. The Crucible is the primordial form of the Erdtree. It is said that the Crucible existed before the establishment of the Golden Order, and the Crucible Knights were among the earliest devotees of the Erdtree."

[Source: Crucible Tree Set]
[Context: Dropped by Crucible Knight Siluria in Deeproot Depths]

---

## Cleanrot Knight Set

> "Armor of the Cleanrot Knights. The Cleanrot Knights continued to serve Malenia even as they rotted from within. Their dignity, evident in the fine crafting of their arms, was all they had left. Soldiers who had succumbed to the Scarlet Rot."

[Source: Cleanrot Knight Set]
[Context: Dropped by Cleanrot Knights]

---

## Tree Sentinel Set

> "Armor worn by Tree Sentinels, the heavily-armored horsemen who serve as guardians of the Erdtree. Sentinels stood watch at the Minor Erdtrees before the Shattering, and some still remain at their posts."

[Source: Tree Sentinel Set]
[Context: Dropped by Tree Sentinel enemies]

## Fia's Deathbed Dress

> "Dress of the Deathbed Companion. The warmth of a Deathbed Companion's embrace soothes the afflicted, and those fortunate enough to die in her arms experience true peace. But she takes from them something in return."

[Source: Fia's Deathbed Dress]
[Context: Found on Fia's body in Deeproot Depths]

## Goldmask's Rags

> "Ragged clothing once worn by Goldmask. It is said he was once an erudite scholar, but cast aside his material possessions in pursuit of the Golden Order's fundamental principle."

[Source: Goldmask's Rags]
[Context: Found on Goldmask's body on the Erdtree Capital ramparts]

## Omen Set (Dung Eater's)

[Context: Available after Dung Eater questline]
