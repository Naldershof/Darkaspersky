# Lore-Significant Spirit Ashes

## Black Knife Tiche

> "Spirit of Black Knife Tiche, daughter of the Black Knife Ringleader, Alecto. Tiche met her end in the plot to assassinate Godwyn the Golden, but her skill with the Black Knife endures."

[Source: Black Knife Tiche, Spirit Ash]
[Context: Dropped by Alecto in Ringleader's Evergaol]

---

## Cleanrot Knight Finlay

> "Spirit of Cleanrot Knight Finlay. Finlay is one of the few who witnessed the Battle of Aeonia. When Malenia's scarlet flower scattered and she could fight no more, Finlay carried her all the way back to the Haligtree, alone."

[Source: Cleanrot Knight Finlay, Spirit Ash]
[Context: Found in Elphael, Brace of the Haligtree]

---

## Lhutel the Headless

> "Spirit of Lhutel the Headless. An ancient hero who gave her own head in pledge to Destined Death, so that the Death Rite Bird might not claim her lord."

[Source: Lhutel the Headless, Spirit Ash]
[Context: Tombsward Catacombs]

---

## Banished Knight Oleg

> "Spirit of Banished Knight Oleg. The banished knights swore allegiance to the Erdtree before being stripped of their grace and driven out. Oleg was considered the greatest among them."

[Source: Banished Knight Oleg, Spirit Ash]
[Context: Fringefolk Hero's Grave]

---

## Banished Knight Engvall

> "Spirit of Banished Knight Engvall. Engvall served among the first knights, before the establishment of the Round Table. His faith never wavered, even after banishment."

[Source: Banished Knight Engvall, Spirit Ash]
[Context: Murkwater Catacombs]

---

## Mimic Tear

> "Spirit of the Mimic Tear, an artificial being created in the image of its summoner. The Mimic Tear was fashioned by the Eternal City of Nokron as an attempt to forge a lord. A silver tear that takes the form of the summoner."

[Source: Mimic Tear, Spirit Ash]
[Context: Found in Nokron, Eternal City]

---

## Ancestral Follower

> "Spirit of an ancestral follower. The ancestors dwell in the underground rivers, worshipping the starlight that filters down from above. They are relics of a civilization that predates the Erdtree."

[Source: Ancestral Follower, Spirit Ash]
[Context: Found in Siofra River]

---

## Omenkiller Rollo

> "Spirit of Omenkiller Rollo. The omenkillers were surgeons tasked with removing the horns and cursed blood of the Omen. Their 'treatment' was closer to butchery. Rollo was driven mad by his work."

[Source: Omenkiller Rollo, Spirit Ash]
[Context: Sainted Hero's Grave]

---

## Redmane Knight Ogha

> "Spirit of Redmane Knight Ogha, who served General Radahn. Ogha was taught the gravity-infused archery of his lord, and fought at his side until the Battle of Aeonia claimed his sanity."

[Source: Redmane Knight Ogha, Spirit Ash]
[Context: War-Dead Catacombs]

---

## Ancient Dragon Knight Kristoff

> "Spirit of Ancient Dragon Knight Kristoff. Kristoff earned his title in battle at the capital, when he single-handedly defeated an ancient dragon. He was one of the great heroes of the era before the Shattering."

[Source: Ancient Dragon Knight Kristoff, Spirit Ash]
[Context: Sainted Hero's Grave]

---

## Dolores the Sleeping Arrow

> "Spirit of Dolores the Sleeping Arrow. Dolores was a devotee of St. Trina, who is said to bring gentle sleep. St. Trina's nature remains a mystery — some say she is a child, others claim she is connected to Miquella."

[Source: Dolores the Sleeping Arrow, Spirit Ash]
[Context: Sellia Evergaol]

---

## Latenna the Albinauric

> "Spirit of Latenna, an Albinauric archer. Latenna sought to reach the Haligtree, a sanctuary built by Miquella for those rejected by the Golden Order — including the Albinaurics."

[Source: Latenna the Albinauric, Spirit Ash]
[Context: Given by Latenna at Slumbering Wolf's Shack]
