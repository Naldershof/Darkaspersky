# Lore-Significant Consumables

## Baldachin's Blessing

> "A blessing bestowed by Fia, the Deathbed Companion. Temporarily boosts poise. By embracing champions, the Deathbed Companion takes their vitality into herself. The blessing is the warmth of her embrace — but it comes at a cost."

[Source: Baldachin's Blessing, Consumable]
[Context: Given by Fia at Roundtable Hold after her embrace; the HP reduction is the "vitality" she takes]

---

## Rune Arc

> "A shard of the shattered Elden Ring. Activates the power of a Great Rune when used. The Great Runes are vestiges of the Elden Ring, each held by a demigod. Their power can only be activated by a Rune Arc."

[Source: Rune Arc, Consumable]
[Context: Various locations and rat drops; activates equipped Great Rune]

---

## Shabiri Grape

> "An eyeball with a strange film over it. Given to Hyetta as she searches for the distant light. These grapes are the eyes of those afflicted by the madness of the Three Fingers. The film that covers them is the mark of Shabriri's curse."

[Source: Shabiri Grape, Key Item/Consumable]
[Context: Found on bodies; given to Hyetta on her path to the Three Fingers]

---

## Fingerprint Grape

> "An eyeball marked with a fingerprint. The final grape sought by Hyetta. This grape bears the mark of the Three Fingers themselves, and the one who consumes it will see the distant light of the Frenzied Flame."

[Source: Fingerprint Grape, Key Item]
[Context: Found in the Church of Inhibition; completes Hyetta's quest]

---

## Seedbed Curse

> "A curse that defiles the very soul. The Dung Eater collects these from his victims. The curse propagates the Omen affliction — horns, fell blood, and ostracism. The Dung Eater's goal is to curse every living soul."

[Source: Seedbed Curse, Key Item]
[Context: Found on corpses violated by the Dung Eater throughout the game]

---

## Celestial Dew

> "Shimmering dewdrop of ethereal light. Used at the Church of Vows to atone for sins. Miriel, Pastor of Vows, says that the absolution granted by celestial dew reflects the original purpose of the Erdtree — forgiveness."

[Source: Celestial Dew, Consumable]
[Context: Found in Eternal City areas; used at Church of Vows]

---

## Starlight Shards

> "Amber crystal formed by starlight. Slowly restores FP. These crystals form naturally in places where starlight gathers — primarily in the underground rivers of the Eternal Cities."

[Source: Starlight Shards, Consumable]
[Context: Found throughout the game; connected to the star-worship of the Nox]

---

## Deathroot

> "A root of the death that spreads from the corpse of Godwyn the Golden. The deathroot spreads beneath the Lands Between, and those who consume it are afflicted with a terrible hunger. Gurranq, Beast Clergyman, consumes these in penance."

[Source: Deathroot, Key Item]
[Context: Found in catacombs; given to Gurranq/Maliketh in the Bestial Sanctum]

---

## Larval Tear

> "Material used for rebirth at the Academy. A silver tear hardened into a larval form. The Mimic Tears of the Eternal Cities were attempts to forge a lord. The larval tears are their shed husks."

[Source: Larval Tear, Consumable/Key Item]
[Context: Various locations; used at Rennala for rebirth/respec]

---

## Boiled Crab / Boiled Prawn

> "Crab boiled by Blackguard Big Boggart. The Blackguard is a rough man, but his cooking is unmatched. He was once a thief who stole Rya's necklace — but found an honest trade at the Altus moat."

[Source: Boiled Crab/Prawn, Consumable]
[Context: Purchased from Blackguard Big Boggart at various locations]

---

## Gold-Pickled Fowl Foot

> "A good-luck charm made by pickling the foot of a golden fowl. Boosts rune acquisition. The gold-tinged fowl are said to be blessed by the Erdtree."

[Source: Gold-Pickled Fowl Foot, Consumable]
[Context: Crafted; connected to Erdtree blessing]
