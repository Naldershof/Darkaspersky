# DLC Remembrances and Key Weapons — Lore Descriptions

---

## Remembrance of the Impaler

> "Remembrance of Messmer the Impaler, hewn into the Erdtree.
> Messmer's scarlet flame was gifted to him by his mother, Queen Marika.
> Yet even as he led her armies and purged the land of shadow in her name,
> he could not earn the love a child should rightly know.
> In time, he was hidden away, and all record of his existence was effaced."

[Source: Remembrance item]
[Context: Obtained after defeating Messmer the Impaler in the Shadow Keep]

---

## Remembrance of the Dancing Lion

> "Remembrance of the Divine Beast Dancing Lion, hewn into the Erdtree.
> The lion was once a guardian spirit of the shamans who lived upon the grassy plains.
> When the hornsent came and trampled their villages, they bound the beast in ritual garb,
> compelling it to serve forevermore as protector of Belurat, Tower Settlement."

[Source: Remembrance item]
[Context: Obtained after defeating the Divine Beast Dancing Lion in Belurat, Tower Settlement]

---

## Remembrance of the Saint of the Bud

> "Remembrance of Romina, Saint of the Bud, hewn into the Erdtree.
> Romina was an ancient priestess who turned to the scarlet rot,
> becoming its saint and apostle. She awaited the coming of a new god
> at the Church of the Bud, guarding the passage to Enir-Ilim,
> the threshold between the mortal realm and the divine."

[Source: Remembrance item]
[Context: Obtained after defeating Romina, Saint of the Bud at the Church of the Bud]

---

## Remembrance of the Wild Boar Rider

> "Remembrance of Commander Gaius, hewn into the Erdtree.
> Gaius was among the most fearsome of Messmer's commanders,
> a knight who rode a great wild boar into battle.
> Even after Messmer's flame was sealed and the war long ended,
> Gaius stood vigil at the gate of the Shadow Keep, faithful to the last."

[Source: Remembrance item]
[Context: Obtained after defeating Commander Gaius at the front gate of the Shadow Keep]

---

## Remembrance of the Mother of Fingers

> "Remembrance of Metyr, Mother of Fingers, hewn into the Erdtree.
> Metyr is the progenitor of the Two Fingers and the Three Fingers alike—
> a vassal of the Greater Will who descended upon the Lands Between in a bygone age.
> She was sealed away in the cathedral of Manus Metyr when her purpose was spent,
> her withered body a testament to the cruel indifference of outer gods."

[Source: Remembrance item]
[Context: Obtained after defeating Metyr, Mother of Fingers in the Cathedral of Manus Metyr, accessed via Count Ymir's questline]

---

## Remembrance of a God and a Lord

> "Remembrance of the Promised Consort Radahn, hewn into the Erdtree.
> Miquella the Kind yearned for a lord strong enough to be his consort—
> one who could carry him across the threshold of godhood.
> And so he chose Radahn, mightiest of the demigod champions,
> charming him and claiming his will, remaking Radahn's ruined body
> as a vessel fit for a god's escort."

[Source: Remembrance item]
[Context: Obtained after defeating Promised Consort Radahn, the final boss of Shadow of the Erdtree, at the summit of Enir-Ilim]

---

## Spear of the Impaler

> "Sacred spear wielded by Messmer the Impaler.
> Coated in hardened blood, this weapon has impaled countless victims.
> Messmer was the son of Queen Marika, born with scarlet flame—
> an omen of violence that his mother saw fit to harness.
> She sent him to the Land of Shadow to subjugate its people,
> then expunged all record of his existence.
> His spear became a symbol of terror for the hornsent
> and all the peoples of the shadow lands."

[Source: Weapon — Great Spear, obtained via Remembrance of the Impaler]
[Context: Traded at the Finger Reader in Roundtable Hold or at a Mausoleum]

---

## Messmer's Orb

> "Incantation of Messmer the Impaler.
> Summons a serpentine orb of Messmer's scarlet flame.
> Messmer was branded with fire from birth—
> the base flames of fell prophecy, which his mother
> Marika sanctified, gifting them a scarlet hue.
> These flames were his birthright and his burden both."

[Source: Incantation, obtained via Remembrance of the Impaler]
[Context: Traded at the Finger Reader in Roundtable Hold or at a Mausoleum]

---

## Euporia

> "Sacred twinblade once wielded by Miquella the Kind.
> A delicate weapon of flowing gold, forged in the image
> of the Erdtree's entwined branches.
> Miquella discarded this blade along his pilgrimage,
> shedding it as he shed all else that defined him—
> for a god must be born anew, free of all attachment."

[Source: Weapon — Twinblade, found at one of Miquella's Crosses]
[Context: Found at a Miquella's Cross in the Shadow Keep, marking where Miquella shed a part of himself]

---

## Greatsword of Radahn (Lord)

> "Greatsword of the promised consort Radahn,
> raised to lordship by the will of Miquella.
> Once a pair of blades wielded in unison,
> they are now fused into a single lordly greatsword,
> their crossing forms recalling the golden sigil of the Erdtree.
> Radahn was a lord not by his own choosing.
> Miquella's charm stripped him of his will, and the starscourge
> was remade as the god's unwilling vessel."

[Source: Weapon — Colossal Sword, obtained via Remembrance of a God and a Lord]
[Context: Traded at the Finger Reader in Roundtable Hold or at a Mausoleum]

---

## Radahn's Light

> "Skill of the Greatsword of Radahn (Lord).
> Raise the greatsword aloft, channeling the grace of a lord
> to unleash a devastating wave of golden light.
> Even in his diminished state, the echo of Radahn's
> cosmological might blazes forth—now in service
> to Miquella's dream of a new age."

[Source: Weapon skill description — Greatsword of Radahn (Lord)]
[Context: Unique skill on the Greatsword of Radahn (Lord)]

---

## Dancing Lion Head

> "Helm fashioned in the image of the Divine Beast Dancing Lion.
> The shamans of the grassy plains worshipped guardian spirits
> who took the form of great lions. When the hornsent conquered their lands,
> they bound one such spirit to serve as sentinel of their tower settlement.
> The lion danced still, but its dance had become a dirge."

[Source: Helm — obtained via Remembrance of the Dancing Lion]
[Context: Traded at the Finger Reader in Roundtable Hold or at a Mausoleum]

---

## Poleblade of the Bud

> "Sacred poleblade of Romina, Saint of the Bud.
> A weapon of exquisite craftsmanship, wreathed in scarlet rot.
> Romina was once a priestess of the ancient rites,
> but the rot claimed her and made her its saint.
> She awaited the flowering of a new god at the Church of the Bud,
> believing that the rot itself might bloom into something divine."

[Source: Weapon — obtained via Remembrance of the Saint of the Bud]
[Context: Traded at the Finger Reader in Roundtable Hold or at a Mausoleum]
